## Start here / conventions

- [All-position index, 2013–Q2 2026](all_positions_index.md): all **54 quarters**, **676 distinct CUSIP/instrument rows**, including **625 non-option/non-debt** rows, first/last observed quarter and peak reported market value. Full SEC tables repaired nine 50-row pagination truncations.
- Every case has schema front matter and all eight sections. **unknown** means unknown. Quarter-end observations do not establish an exact purchase month; metadata does not silently convert Q1 into March. File years group a decision/update/reporting episode where original entry predates the research window or is unknown. In particular `2025_*_disclosures` is an audit episode, not a claimed first purchase in 2025.
- `open` / `ongoing` can mean **archival economic outcome unresolved**, not assertion the security is owned today; `closed` can mean the public reportable episode ended. `outcome_class: ongoing` on a closed episode means P&L unscored, **not flat return**. Actual dates/costs/exits are unknown unless independently sourced.
- Type is evidence-based. A common-stock long held by an activist is not automatically activism; a deal-adjacent long is not proof of a market-neutral hedge. 13F excludes shorts, most foreign ordinary/private assets, most swaps and substantial credit.
- Sources mix dated primary manager letters (advocacy), issuer filings/releases (independent corroboration of mechanics, not endorsement of Loeb), and explicitly labeled press. Quoted words were checked against retrieved text; unquoted analysis is source-grounded paraphrase. Source date may be publication date; the Q2 2023 letter itself is **July 31**, publicly reported August 1.
- Return distinctions: fund return, contribution in basis points, stock-price change, bond/index return, paper mark and realized cash profit are never interchangeable. No cost basis/P&L inferred from share counts or live tracker valuations.
