# Third Point / Dan Loeb: 2013–2019 trade compendium

## Scope and reading conventions

**59 new case files:** 57 security/ownership-phase records plus two portfolio-level notes (credit book and quality/reset). The index also links the existing canonical Ally and Yahoo files rather than creating duplicate trade records. Earlier entries are included where a substantive 2013–2019 decision/update was recovered. This is a sourced research pass, **not a claim that every original quarterly PDF or every position in all 28 quarters has been exhaustively recovered**.

- **Era labels:** B = 2009–2015; C = 2016–mid-2025. Entry dates control episode labels; transition notes may span both. See [research guide](../RESEARCH_GUIDE.md).
- **Source hierarchy:** original Loeb letters/decks/SEC exhibits and issuer responses first; reproduced letters second; contemporary reporting explicitly identified; 13F compilations establish quarter-end common-security observations, not reasoning, trade prices or whole-fund exposure.
- **Point in time:** distinguish first purchase, later public thesis, campaign escalation and retrospective review. A Q3 letter dated November 9 can contain October events and is not a September 30 data pack.
- **Unknowns:** exact cost, hedge, final exit and realized P&L are often unknown. `status: open` means an unresolved/last-verified archival position, **not an assertion of ownership on October 25, 2026**. `outcome_class: ongoing` can mean economic outcome unresolved even after a verified security exit. A `win` can indicate documented interim/structural success rather than audited realized profit; read the outcome text.
- **Date precision:** front-matter dates use verified YYYY-MM/YYYY-MM-DD or `unknown`. Where only year/quarter is established, the period is preserved in a YAML comment and the narrative rather than inventing a month.
- **No double counting:** Dow/DowDuPont are one continuing capital sequence with separate catalyst-phase files. Original/re-entry Alibaba and Sony are distinct episodes. 2014 eBay/PayPal and 2018 PayPal are separate disclosed theses; retained-lot reconciliation remains unknown. Private/SPAC sponsor capital is not public IPO proceeds.
- **Evidence limits:** short source-limited files preserve authentic positions without inventing missing rationale. They are not full-fidelity calibration answer keys. Every case has the schema's eight sections and a named gap where original words or terminal outcomes are unavailable.

## Verification notes: do not manufacture missing trades

Search also surfaced SEC “Loeb” mutual-fund documents, which must not automatically be attributed to Daniel Loeb/Third Point.

A [February 12, 2025 Hedge Fund Alpha article](https://hedgefundalpha.com/investor-letters/dan-loeb-third-point-fannie-mae/) concerns **2024** Fannie/Freddie credit gains; its visible/paywalled excerpt cannot be backdated or used to establish a 2013 entry, preferred series, cost or original reasoning. The research guide/request is a lead, not a source proving the trade. **Open gap, not a proved negative.**
