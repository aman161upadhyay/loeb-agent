## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2014 episode:** re-entered select sovereign credit following the January selloff, looking toward creditor resolution and a change in political leadership. **2019 re-entry is a separate decision:** Loeb believed Macri's election prospects were improving and that even an opposition victory need not imply the severe restructuring priced by the market. Do not import the later primary-election result into that purchase case. [1–2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
**October 2019 self-criticism:** “our most significant mistake was missing the second order thinking” around the period between the primary and general election. “our thesis was left in limbo and to avoid unacceptable downside risk, we reduced our position”. [2]

**Annual hindsight:** “we took our guard down a bit too much” in estimating political and currency risks. These are loss-analysis quotes, not original-entry assurances. [3]

## What Third Point did
Re-entered credit in 2014; the 2019 letter reports significant profits during 2014–2016. Subsequently returned to sovereign bonds in 2019. Following the unexpectedly large August 11 primary defeat, cut exposure before the October letter rather than merely holding for recovery. Exact boundary/exit between ownership episodes and bond identifiers unknown. [1–2]

## What happened after
The 2019 primary created a policy vacuum: Macri remained formally in office while the likely successor had little incentive or ability to calm markets. Currency collapse, reserve losses and restructuring risk followed. Argentina was Q3's largest loss; annual hindsight calls the position oversized. The October expectation of recovery 30–50% above then-prices was a forecast, not realized proceeds. [2–3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Normalization:** early thesis was legal/financing reform. [1]
- **Politics:** subsequent re-entry relied on explicit election probabilities. [2]
- **Alternative outcome:** expected restructuring severity was underwritten, but inadequately. [2]
- **Second-order risk:** interregnum and currency feedback mattered beyond who won. [2–3]
- **Discipline:** exposure reduction followed invalidated downside assumptions. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: critical counterexample to assuming prior country profits confer future edge. Political catalysts can overwhelm recovery-value analysis; sizing is part of the error. `loss` describes the documented 2019 episode, not the net sum of all Argentine investments. [2–3]

## Public discussion
The letters openly acknowledge the error; do not sanitize it into a merely temporary mark. Independent contemporary bond-forum/X material not recovered. [2–3]
