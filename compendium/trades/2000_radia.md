## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**June 2000 private investment; retrospective evidence only.** Loeb’s May 2004 footnote describes a small early-stage wireless semiconductor investment in Radia at an approximately $15m fully diluted valuation. A full contemporary entry memorandum, stake size, ownership vehicle and operating model were not recovered. The story appears as evidence of Third Point’s limited successful venture activity while criticizing unrelated acquisitions by InterCept, not as a recommendation to become a broad venture fund. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“4x our invested capital on a fully diluted basis” — May 27, 2004 retrospective. This is the exact return phrase; the letter does not specify net fund-level performance. [1]

## What Third Point did
Invested June 2000; Loeb later described Texas Instruments acquiring the company July 31, 2003 for over $145m. Do not confuse the entire acquisition value with Third Point’s ownership proceeds. [1]

## What happened after
Loeb’s approximate 4x claim is attributable retrospective testimony, not independently audited P&L. The ratio of company sale valuation to entry valuation is much larger than 4x and cannot be used to override the reported fund return; dilution, financing and ownership matter. [1]
