## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q4 2013 initiation, January 2014 public thesis:** SoftBank combined wireless assets and a large Alibaba interest before Alibaba's IPO. Third Point treated the component values and Son's capital allocation as overlooked. Merger synergies discussed in the January letter were contingent estimates, not a consummated Sprint/T-Mobile transaction. [1–2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“Softbank is led by one of the world's premier creators and compounders of value.” — January 21, 2014 letter. The Q1 update says: “SoftBank's fundamentals are stronger than when we initiated the position during the fourth quarter of 2013.” These date-stamp entry more reliably than a generic “2014 investment.” [1–2]

## What Third Point did
Purchased in Q4 2013; discussed value in January and again in the Q1 2014 letter. The Q3 letter listed SoftBank among positions exited/reduced in a portfolio concentration change; recovered reporting does not resolve the exact final trade date. Later direct Alibaba ownership is recorded separately. [1–3]

## What happened after
Alibaba's public listing removed one valuation uncertainty but does not alone establish SoftBank-lot returns. Realized profit, hedge treatment and whether a residual remained at a particular intervening date are unknown. `outcome_class: ongoing` denotes unresolved return evidence, not continued ownership. [3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Allocator:** Son's record supported valuation of the holding-company portfolio. [1]
- **IPO:** public discovery of Alibaba value was an identifiable catalyst. [1]
- **Telecom:** operating improvement and consolidation could add value beyond Alibaba. [1–2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a passive catalyst trade in a complex corporate structure, not a public board campaign. Do not apply later Vision Fund risks or later SoftBank activist campaigns to this information set. [1–3]

## Public discussion
The letters themselves discuss merger optionality; treating that possibility as certain would overstate the case. Independent contemporaneous social/forum material not recovered. [1]
