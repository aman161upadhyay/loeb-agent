## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: Q2 2023 size-up.** An addition/renewed thesis, not the first historical MSFT position. Public AI product and cloud developments motivated a more constructive stance, while the August letter admits portfolio exposure was initially too small. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

August 2023: “a clear AI winner” through Azure, AI features in core Office, investment in OpenAI and provision of AI services to other enterprises. The focus is monetization/distribution across an installed base, not semiconductor manufacturing. April 2024 again praised Microsoft’s well-run legacy-business advantages. [1,2]

## What Third Point did

Sized up cloud/software investment in Q2 2023. Held 1.1M common at Q3 2025 and 925k Q4; absent Q1/Q2 2026. Exact sale dates, prices and whether economic exposure persisted elsewhere are unknown. [1; filing rows]
