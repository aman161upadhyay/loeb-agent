## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Separate re-entry, April 2009.** Third Point had previously held and exited Deutsche Boerse in 2008 as European/global expectations worsened. It initiated again in early April 2009, citing high market share, about 50% EBIT margins, modest debt and roughly 10x free cash flow. Treat the two episodes separately rather than assuming uninterrupted ownership through the crisis. [1,2]

Loeb believed stable Clearstream settlement/custody earnings left more volume-sensitive businesses cheaply valued. Two major holders’ announced exits reduced a technical selling overhang. Exact prices and size were not provided. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“we welcome Deutsche Boerse back into our portfolio” — July 31, 2009. [1]

## What Third Point did
Re-entered early April after prior 2008 exit. Underwrote stable custody cash flow plus upside from capital-market reopening, issuance/volumes and prospective European credit-derivatives infrastructure. No public activist board demand is established in these sources. [1,2]

## What happened after
The recovered letter states the investment case, not a final realized exit. Derivatives-platform earnings and recovering volumes were forecasts. Cost, later holding duration and complete P&L remain unknown; do not carry forward the prior episode’s reported loss as this re-entry’s result. [1,2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Quality cash flow at lower valuation:** High share, margins and modest leverage supported roughly 10x FCF entry. [1]
- **Clearstream sum-of-parts support:** Stable custody earnings helped isolate cheap cyclicality. [1]
- **Technical overhang ending:** Two large shareholder exits could clear forced supply. [1]
- **Capital-markets/derivatives catalysts:** Reopening issuance and a European credit platform supplied upside scenarios. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a changed valuation/regime can justify re-entry after a correct risk exit or previous loss. The same ticker does not mean the same thesis or one uninterrupted investment. [1,2]

## Public discussion
Original letter is the contemporaneous reasoning source; independent period forum debate was not recovered. Its valuation and technical claims should remain attributable to Loeb rather than labeled market consensus. [1]
