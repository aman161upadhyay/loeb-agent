## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: December 29, 2020.** Intel had lost manufacturing leadership to TSMC/Samsung and lagged peers. The letter identified loss of over $60B market capitalization during the prior year; this is Intel shareholder value loss, **not Third Point P&L**. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

December 29: “Of special concern is Intel’s human capital management problem and the absence of an articulated plan to address it.” Loeb requested an adviser to examine strategic alternatives, including failed acquisitions, and a credible manufacturing/customer strategy. “Intel must figure out how to serve its competitors as customers.” [1]

## What Third Point did

December 29 letter/HSR approval request. Reported 1M common shares at Q4 2020, 14M at Q2 2021, 9M at Q3 and none Q4 2021. **Important qualification:** February 16, 2022 letter still offers a detailed bullish Intel update on reversing brain drain, new leadership and Alder Lake. Common-share disappearance therefore does not independently prove the date of liquidation of all economic exposure. Exact 2021–22 economic exit unknown. [1–3]

## What happened after

Do not score Intel’s later collapse as this fund’s realized loss, or assume the manufacturing split was enacted because Loeb proposed strategic review. P&L unknown; public common-stock exposure exited by Q4 2021 but the February 2022 thesis needs reconciliation with derivatives/reporting. [2,3]
