## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**March 2001 after technology collapse.** Loeb alleged a 90% stock decline under new leadership, flawed conversion of telesales into direct selling, mishandled $125m stock acquisition of Entevo, negative cash flow and revenue-restatement/control issues. These are his campaign assertions, not established fraud findings. [1]

At $2.25/share he calculated $120m equity less $60m cash/investments and virtually no debt, or $60m EV against $103m low-end 2001 sales guidance. He cited bv-Control’s approximately 50% host-vulnerability-assessment share from IDC and useful bv-Admin products; valuable software and technical staff, not trust in management, justified buying. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“we believe that BindView has a committed and hard working technical team as well as outstanding product offerings.” — March 22, 2001. [1]

“selling the Company” — the explicit requested action, not a forecast that software valuations would broadly recover. [1]

## What Third Point did
Held 1.5m shares by March 9 and over 2m by March 22. Partnered publicly with Chapman Capital in advocating a strategic sale and warned against squandering the cash balance. Used AXENT/Symantec, Mission Critical/NetIQ and Sequoia/Citrix as comparative strategic transactions. [1]

## What happened after
Loeb proposed 2–3x revenue valuation implying $5–7/share and possibly more in a strategic sale; those were forecasts. The recovered primary letter does not establish campaign resolution, ultimate buyer, share-sale date or P&L. Do not equate a later company acquisition with continuous Third Point ownership. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Cash-backed enterprise-value discount:** A large cash balance reduced effective purchase cost. [1]
- **Products survive management failure:** Market share and technical quality supported strategic value despite execution problems. [1]
- **Larger buyer can scale distribution:** Sales-channel/customer synergies supplied a sale rationale. [1]
- **Stop further value destruction:** Cash preservation and financial controls were immediate safeguards. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: early repeatable “good assets / bad management / strategic owner” pattern, with explicit enterprise-value arithmetic. Revenue multiples are comparables, not a substitute for profitability and integration analysis. [1]

## Public discussion
The original letter records contemporaneous sell-side disenchantment and cooperation with Chapman, but those are Loeb’s account, not a recovered analyst report or forum thread. No authenticated Mr. Pink post was recovered. [1]
