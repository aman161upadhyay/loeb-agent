## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q4 2017 entry; May 2018 public letter:** UTC combined aerospace, elevators and climate systems. The Q1 2018 letter explicitly says the stake began in Q4 2017. Third Point believed separate companies would allow the market to recognize different earnings/cash-flow profiles, especially Pratt & Whitney's investment-to-cash-generation transition. Exact entry price and original position sizing are unknown. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
**February 2019 update, not original entry:** “The coming separation will shine a greater spotlight on the large valuation gap to UTC's pure-play peers.” The same update expected greater transparency around Pratt & Whitney's “very significant multi-year inflection in free cash flow generation.” [2]

The May letter modeled SOTP above $190 at year-end 2019 and up to $210 including geared-turbofan NPV. These are forecasts; the full original valuation bridge should be recovered before making a numerical calibration key. [1]

## What Third Point did
Bought in Q4 2017; May 2018 letter advocated a three-way breakup. After a separation announcement, the Q4 2018 update maintained the case despite a widening valuation gap. In June 2019 Loeb opposed the proposed aerospace combination with Raytheon. Do not collapse “supports breakup” into “supports any subsequent M&A.” [1–3]

## What happened after
The February update says the discount had continued to widen and Honeywell-relative valuation reached a ten-year gap: an announced catalyst did not immediately produce the forecast rerating. The 2019 Raytheon objection is a thesis-consistency check. Exact disposal of UTX/RTX/Otis/Carrier securities and investment-level P&L remain unknown. [2–3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Focus:** aerospace, elevators and climate assets deserved distinct governance/valuation. [1]
- **SOTP:** standalone peers were the valuation yardstick. [1–2]
- **Cash inflection:** Pratt's near-term investment burden obscured later cash generation. [1–2]
- **Transparency:** separation could expose asset quality and hold managers accountable. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: industrial constructivism with a multi-year cash-flow bridge, not simply a discount-to-book trade. Entry-year correction is 2017, campaign year 2018. [1]

## Public discussion
The contemporary company-breakup debate is documented by CNBC's May 4 coverage; Reuters' June 2019 report records Loeb's contrary merger position. Authenticated social/forum evidence not recovered. [3–4]
