## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2012 entry, retrospectively dated by July 2015 letter coverage:** Third Point first bought around the Mexican beer-brand transaction involving Corona/Modelo Especial. The 2015 case had evolved toward volume growth, favorable Latino demographics and manufacturing efficiencies. Do not label this a 2016 initiation or confuse it with Constellation Software. [1–2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
The letter's framework: “talented management teams, strong and growing free cash flows, and a proven track record of smart capital allocation that drives significant increases of intrinsic value per share”. This is the **shared compounder definition**, explicitly applied to Constellation, Mohawk and Roper, not a company-specific standalone quote. [1]

## What Third Point did
Bought in 2012, retained the position after the original event and presented it as a value compounder in the July 31, 2015 letter. Stake size, purchase lots and final exit unknown. [1–2]

## What happened after
At the update, Third Point considered management's $7.50 EPS target for 2017 achievable and saw increasing capital return as capex declined. These are 2015 expectations, not verified achieved earnings or fund profits. [1–2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Event:** a specific acquisition supplied the initial entry context. [1]
- **Volume:** brands/demographics supported organic demand. [1]
- **Margins:** manufacturing efficiencies could improve earnings beyond sales growth. [1]
- **FCF:** falling investment intensity could release cash to shareholders. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: verified early compounder example, showing the 2018 quality shift had antecedents. Not all successful event positions had to be sold after the first catalyst. [1–2]

## Public discussion
CNBC emphasized the Buffett-like simplicity of the framework, contrasting it with Loeb's activist reputation. That is journalist interpretation. No authenticated forum/X thread recovered. [1]
