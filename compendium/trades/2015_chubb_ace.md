## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q1 2016 discussion:** ACE's acquisition of Chubb had closed in January. Third Point compared the predecessors' approximately 90% combined ratios and 10%-plus book-value compounding with weaker peers. These were historical underwriting measures cited at the update, not proof of a purchase-day valuation. Entry date unknown; filename reflects the merger-announcement phase. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“We are willing to forego short-term cost cuts or buybacks to own a franchise that is a long-term winner”. The letter emphasized an “A+ CEO in Evan Greenberg.” This is direct evidence against characterizing every Loeb financial investment as short-term financial engineering. [1]

## What Third Point did
Held and analyzed the combined insurer as a high-quality financial compounder. Exact original ACE/Chubb instruments, stake size and later disposal unknown. [1]

## What happened after
Expected double-digit earnings growth was a forecast. No realized fund return recovered, and strong historical combined ratios did not eliminate catastrophe/insurance-cycle risk. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Underwriting:** durable operating advantage versus peers. [1]
- **Leadership:** Greenberg's record supported execution. [1]
- **Fit:** geographic, distribution and product complementarity. [1]
- **Long term:** growth could justify foregoing immediate payouts. [1]
