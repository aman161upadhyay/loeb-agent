## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: early 2022 thesis update.** This was an addition to an existing investment, not a verified first purchase in 2022. February 2022 letter refers to early-pandemic buying and recent significant increases. It saw shares at the lower end of historical valuation ranges and a 30–40% discount to estimated intrinsic value; that is manager valuation, not realized upside. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

Thesis: secular cloud/e-commerce demand, operating leverage after a major logistics investment cycle, management change and potential capital return. The 2023–24 AI case extended—not replaced—the cloud franchise advantage and distribution scale. April 2024 letter groups Microsoft/Amazon as well-run legacy businesses exploiting AI to accelerate growth. [1,3,4]

## What Third Point did

2020 letter describes a 5% position initiated in March; the 2019 Q4 13F already reported a smaller AMZN holding, so this cannot be treated as the first-ever purchase. Significantly increased by February 2022; retained through the 2022 drawdown; Q2 2022 named among largest detractors. Subsequent holding changes below are complete quarterly observations, not assumed reactions to each new AI headline. [1,2; historical filing index]
