## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2017 merger/separation phase:** this is a continuation of the earlier Dow investment, not evidence of a new independent purchase. The Q1 2016 letter had already discussed the December 2015 merger announcement and approximately $5 billion potential earnings improvement. By Q3 2017 the case compared 8.6x consensus 2019 EBITDA with likely standalone comparable multiples. [1–2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
The Q3 update described a “substantial discount to its sum-of-the-parts” relative to the planned “three (or more) Spin-Cos.” Those words matter: the thesis included improving the ultimate portfolio design, not merely waiting for a legal merger to close. [2]

## What Third Point did
Maintained the Dow-related exposure through the combination, advocated better asset configuration and continued to treat synergies and the separations as catalysts. The Q1 2018 letter expected recognition over the following 12 months; that is a dated expectation, not a guaranteed completion timetable. This file tracks the 2017–2019 phase without inventing successor-security cost allocations or final sales. [1–3]

## What happened after
The Q2 2018 letter identifies DowDuPont as a drag on results. This is important adverse evidence against a mechanical “announced breakup equals immediate rerating” rule. Ultimate 2019 successor-company distributions require issuer/custody reconciliation; exact Third Point DD/DOW/CTVA retained lots, exit dates and aggregate P&L remain unknown here. [4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Synergies:** merger earnings uplift supported the base case. [1]
- **Portfolio:** placement of businesses affected standalone quality and multiples. [2–3]
- **Valuation:** compare forward earnings with properly matched standalone peers. [2]
- **Execution:** savings and better disclosure needed to become visible. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a multi-stage corporate event with operational and valuation drivers; the 2018 disappointment shows time-to-catalyst risk. Do not double-count profits with `2014_dow_chemical.md`. [1–4]

## Public discussion
The 2017 summary was bullish; the 2018 letter's detractor designation is the necessary counterweight. No authenticated contemporaneous forums/X posts recovered. [2,4]
