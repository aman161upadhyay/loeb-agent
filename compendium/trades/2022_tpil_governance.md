## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: January 2022 renewed requisition.** AVI/Metage/Global Value/Pinehurst >18% ordinary shareholders sought discount-control/redemption rights. This is activism **against Loeb’s listed feeder**, not Loeb targeting an outside issuer. [1,5]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

No fabricated Loeb quote: opposition primary letter argues persistent discount and board resistance harm owners. Board/manager approach used buybacks/exchanges to preserve listed structure; distinguish adversarial perspectives and manager economics. [3,5]

## What Third Point did

January 18 requisition sought Richard Boléat. February settlement added Boléat/Vivien Gould and Rupert Dorey as chair, withdrawing EGM; annual report dates appointments March. 2022 >2M repurchased/$53M; fully subscribed $75M master-fund exchange. Conditional 25% tenders at 2% discount for 2024/2027 if discounts >10%/7.5%. [1–3]

## What happened after

2024 redemption 25% at 2% discount; 1.14M buybacks at 23.1% average discount. Strategic committee April 22, 2024 led toward Malibu conversion (separate case). Persistent discount despite strong 2024 NAV is not automatic success. [4]
