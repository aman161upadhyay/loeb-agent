## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q3 2014:** eBay had reached an inflection under John Donahoe; the public separation plan changed the cash-flow and investor-base proposition. The October letter distinguished a mature marketplace's repurchase capacity from PayPal's growth. Exact trade timing relative to the announcement remains unknown. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
The letter contemplated PayPal becoming “the Visa of the Internet.” This was a potential network/partnership outcome, not a statement that PayPal had Visa's legal or economic structure. Longer quotation is withheld because the recovered mirror has spacing defects. [1]

## What Third Point did
Established a significant Q3 position and analyzed both resulting companies. A secondary Q4 2015 holdings summary reports a 55% eBay reduction. Exact distribution/retained PayPal lots, final disposal and realized P&L remain unknown; 2018 PayPal initiation is treated separately. [1–2]

## What happened after
The letter's estimate that core eBay could repurchase roughly one-third of its float in about 2.5 years was a forecast. Do not present that capacity as achieved repurchases. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Separation:** recognize distinct growth and cash-flow profiles. [1]
- **Cash:** marketplace capital returns supplied per-share value. [1]
- **Network:** scale and merchant acceptance supported PayPal against Apple Pay fears. [1]
- **Optionality:** partnerships could strengthen an independent PayPal. [1]
