## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: Q1 2025 campaign.** CoStar was already an earlier compounder investment (2021); this is the 2025 governance/capital-allocation episode. The April letter argues a commercial-data/marketplace franchise was obscured by roughly $1B/year Homes.com spending. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

April 30 calls CoStar the “Bloomberg of commercial real estate”. Low penetration, pricing power and recurring high margins could support >7x EBITDA over several years under disciplined reinvestment. That was a manager forecast, not a realized result. [1]

## What Third Point did

Separate support agreements with Third Point and D.E. Shaw signed April 6, announced April 7. John Berisford, Rachel Glaser and Christine McCarthy replaced three directors; capital-allocation committee/compensation review established. Separate agreements do **not** prove a single legal 13D group or identical positions. [1,2]

## What happened after

April 10, 2026 letter **explicitly announces complete exit and broken original thesis**: continued Homes.com cash burn, Florance’s entrenchment/golden-parachute terms, reporting resegmentation and neglect of Apartments.com/CoStar Suite amid AI competition. CoStar was a top Q1 detractor; final loss dollars unknown. These are Loeb’s allegations, not adjudicated misconduct. [3]
