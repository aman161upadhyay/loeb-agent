## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: December 2020 IPO.** This public-market decision followed Third Point Ventures’ 2015 Series C investment at a $145M valuation, not an IPO-only speculative purchase. The 2020 Q3 letter says just under 15% ownership. A later 2023 letter calls the entry a 2016 Series B investment; **conflict**: retain the contemporaneous 2015 Series C chronology rather than silently merge them. [1,4]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

The 2020 letter describes AI modeling capable of pricing consumer credit risk better than traditional approaches. May 2021 frames IPOs as another lifecycle stage, not an automatic sale: longstanding board/investor knowledge helped underwrite a much larger public holding. [1,2]

## What Third Point did

Added 1.2M shares at the IPO. By May 2021, about $66M invested had a roughly $1.2B market value and was the largest position. Third Point continued holding through 2021 volatility. [2]

## What happened after

February 2022 retrospective: shares fell 52% in Q4 but were up 271% for the year; Upstart was its biggest winner in 2021 and in firm history as then described. Do not recast the subsequent stock collapse as the return on every venture share without sale/distribution records. Exact realized return and economic exit unknown. [3]
