## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: early 2022.** Later letters explain the drawdown but cannot be used as evidence the fund anticipated the full inflation/rate regime. Q2 and Q3 positioning and subsequent hindsight are separated below. [1–3]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

August 2022 retrospective: defensive exposures protected April/May but hurt July’s rebound; energy/commodities reversed in June, structured credit marked down and private investments were written down. October identified underhedged long-duration investment-grade exposure and poor technicals overwhelming an improving distressed issuer—“primarily errors in hedging”. [1,2]

## What Third Point did

Reduced risk, used/increased single-name shorts, then covered most into the summer rebound; raised senior structured bonds from 20% to 45% and net sold about $500M first-loss paper YTD by October. In Q4 increased exposures and added catalyst positions including AIG/BBWI/DuPont. [1–3]

## What happened after

Q4 lag reflected defense, weak large longs and crypto private writeoffs. August 2023 **hindsight** then admitted tech longs were undersized and gains offset by baskets/single-name shorts and poor longs; greater value-situation emphasis had underperformed quality growth. This is an opportunity-cost/process lesson, not a 2022 portfolio return. [3,4]
