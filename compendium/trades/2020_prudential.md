## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: February 24, 2020 disclosure.** This is the UK insurer Prudential plc, **not Prudential Financial (PRU)**. The company acknowledged receipt of Third Point’s letter and dialogue on strategy/structure. The proposed catalyst was separation of US Jackson from the Asian franchise; the eventual 2021 completion was not then known. [1,2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

April letter, **post-entry**: “Jackson National is a great annuity franchise with significant value”. Loeb argued the market implied over $20B of negative value for Jackson and that the combined structure obscured both franchises. August: independence could close a still-large complexity discount. [2,3]

## What Third Point did

Public breakup campaign February 24; added near Q1 lows despite a 27% share-price decline and 170bp fund loss contribution in Q1. Jackson completed separation September 13, 2021; regular-way JXN trading expected September 20. [1–4]

## What happened after

The separation catalyst occurred, but do not equate that with a verified profitable round trip. Exact fund realization, exit date, derivatives and peak ownership are unknown. The Q1 loss is explicitly retained. [2,4]
