## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: May 2025 agreed terms after strategic review.** Listed feeder discount persisted; proposed insurance acquisition changes business model, not just name. Exact transaction valuation/cash consideration not established here. [1,2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

Source-grounded issuer explanation, **not a recovered first-person Loeb quote**: life/annuity reinsurance combines long-duration liabilities with integrated asset management and growth capital. Structural remedy to feeder discount is a proposition, not guaranteed rerating. [1,2]

## What Third Point did

Terms May 21; EGM approval August 14; completed September 12, 2025. Cayman company renamed Malibu Life Holdings, London-listed. Redeem master-fund investment progressively to fund insurance deployment; related-party structure/manager incentives require prospectus scrutiny. [1,2]

**Prospectus-specific underwriting facts (September 8, not May-entry knowledge):** Malibu was incorporated February 1, 2024. The existing treaty had supplied approximately $951M premiums through Q2 2025 and contemplated up to approximately $3B policies reinsured; these are different measures, neither the acquisition price. Third Point had contributed $66M equity. Consideration included 1,868,805 shares issued to the seller; estimated company transaction/admission expenses were $24.9M. Two of Malibu's three directors were Third Point officers/employees; the prospectus explicitly flags opportunity allocation, trade allocation and cross-trading conflicts from multiple managed/proprietary accounts. A fiduciary/conflicts policy is mitigation, not proof conflicts disappear. [2]

## What happened after

June 30, 2026 interim: book/share $36.57 (+9.7% from Dec 2025), comprehensive income $54M, assets $2.14B, reinsured premiums $1.6B since inception. **Operating-company metrics, not hedge-fund AUM or return**. Asset-liability matching and conflicts remain risks. [3]
