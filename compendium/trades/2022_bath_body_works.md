## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: Q3 2022 entry.** Standalone after the Victoria’s Secret separation, the retailer had succession, capital-allocation and execution challenges. The February 2023 letter’s specific governance accusations are post-entry assessments, not allegations known to be public at purchase. [1,2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

February 22: “confidence in BBWI’s business model, products, and potential for greater growth and efficiency now that it is a standalone entity.” The campaign attacked oversight of compensation, succession, buybacks, communication and strategy while supporting new CEO Gina Boswell. A low-double-digit P/E and potential global DTC brand transformation underpinned the February 2023 investment update. [1,2]

## What Third Point did

8.14M common reported Q3 2022; built to approximately 13.9M in 2023. February 22 threatened proxy contest; March 6, **2023**, company announced Thomas Kuhn, following Lucy Brady (also recommended by Third Point) and Steve Voskuil (company choice). Third Point withdrew nomination plans. Kuhn appointment effective March 10. **Correction: no February 2022 settlement.** [2–4]

## What happened after

Top winner Q1 2024, top detractor Q2 2024; trimmed throughout 2024 and exited remaining roughly 11.8M common in Q4, disclosed February 14, 2025. Exit rationale **none stated** in recovered letters; no realized P&L fabricated. [4–6; Q4 2024 13F index]
