## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: October 27, 2021 disclosure.** Reuters reported about $750M owned. Pressure to decarbonize conflicted with demands for legacy energy cash generation. The company’s statement confirmed preliminary conversations, not agreement to split; it cited 89% shareholder approval for its energy transition strategy. [1,2]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

Reuters quotes Loeb’s concern that incompatible stakeholder pressures produced an “incoherent” set of strategies. Separate legacy oil/refining cash flows from LNG, renewables and growth assets could let investors choose the return/decarbonization profile they wanted. This was a proposed structural remedy, not a claimed completed breakup. [1]
