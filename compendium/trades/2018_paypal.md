## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q2 2018 initiation:** the letter describes 237 million active accounts, 19 million merchants and an approximately $100 billion market cap. These are dated letter statistics, not current metrics. It saw structural digital-commerce growth and differentiated conversion. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“dominant competitive position with a 10x scale advantage” and “hassle-free, one-touch checkout”. The letter forecast $125/share within 18 months, roughly 50% upside, from above-consensus EPS growth. That was a target, not a realized exit. [1]

## What Third Point did
Initiated a long in Q2 2018 and disclosed the network/earnings case. This is a separate initiation from the 2014 eBay separation exposure, whose retained-lot bridge is unknown. [1]

## What happened after
PayPal appears among Q3 2019 detractors. Exact adds, trims, final exit and aggregate return remain unknown. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Scale:** competitive reach mattered to merchants. [1]
- **Conversion:** checkout performance strengthened value proposition. [1]
- **Network:** account and merchant base reinforced utility. [1]
- **Earnings:** market estimates allegedly underrecognized growth. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: explicit extension of the toolkit into growth/quality rather than only classic value events. [1]

## Public discussion
The Q2 letter discusses the evolution from event-driven value into compounders/growth. Authentic contemporary social/forum opposition not recovered. [1]
