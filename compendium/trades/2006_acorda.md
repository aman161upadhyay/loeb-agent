## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Post-first-Phase-III success information set, February 2007.** Fampridine-SR’s September 25, 2006 positive Phase III walking data in multiple sclerosis had transformed Acorda’s share price. Loeb’s letter cited $2.22 immediately before that announcement and $22.61 by February 21. First purchase is unknown; the filename groups the clinical-event period and must not be interpreted as proof Third Point bought before the trial result. [1]

A second Phase III trial, regulatory review and commercialization remained uncertain. The proposed European partnership was a strategic decision before those risks had been fully resolved, not evidence that the drug was already approved. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“Fampridine-SR would have the greatest value in the hands of a seasoned worldwide multiple sclerosis drug developer and marketer.” — February 22, 2007. [1]

## What Third Point did
With 9.9% disclosed, Third Point advocated selling the company instead of negotiating away European rights. Loeb reasoned that a full worldwide asset would be more valuable to an established MS franchise buyer. Acorda publicly responded on February 23. Exact acquisition chronology, subsequent activist agreement and final disposition of Third Point shares remain unknown in this recovery. [1,2]

## What happened after
The contemporaneous sources establish the demand and management’s response, not an executed sale or a measured Third Point loss/win. Neither the later product approval nor much later company distress can by itself settle this historical investor’s P&L. Exit and distribution-adjusted investment result remain unknown.

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Strategic owner can maximize drug value:** Experienced worldwide MS marketing infrastructure was the comparative advantage. [1]
- **Preserve global rights:** European partnering could reduce acquirer synergies and buyer interest. [1]
- **Monetize after clinical de-risking:** First successful Phase III created a sale window while further trial/FDA risk remained. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
