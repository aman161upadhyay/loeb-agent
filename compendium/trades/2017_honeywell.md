## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**April 2017 disclosure:** Honeywell was a diversified industrial group with aerospace and automation/productivity businesses. Third Point argued different business profiles concealed value and that aerospace separation could produce more than $20 billion of sustained shareholder-value increase. This was an activist estimate, not an observed market capitalization gain. Exact entry date and financial snapshot remain gaps. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“Spinning off Aerospace would transform Honeywell into an industrial growth company with a focus on automation and productivity.” — Q1 letter, quoted April 28, 2017. The Q3 update later emphasized a “more streamlined Honeywell” and portfolio optionality. [1–2]

## What Third Point did
Publicly pushed an aerospace spin in the Q1 letter; continued engagement as CEO Darius Adamczyk reviewed the portfolio. The Q3 update welcomed streamlining and capital deployment even though the headline aerospace separation was not the implemented plan in this period. Stake size, sale dates and exact operating improvements attributable to Third Point are unknown. [1–2]

## What happened after
Do not describe 2017 as a completed aerospace spin. The contemporaneous update indicates acceptance of a different, more streamlined company rather than an insistence that only the original blueprint had value. Any much later Honeywell separation belongs to a later information set, not the 2017 outcome. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Separate identities:** aerospace versus industrial growth required different capital/valuation lenses. [1]
- **RemainCo:** focus would emphasize automation and productivity. [1]
- **Asset quality:** strong businesses supplied a base case beyond activism. [2]
- **Capital:** balance-sheet capacity and deployment added optionality. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: constructive conglomerate activism with flexibility on the exact transaction, not “spin-off or nothing.” [1–2]

## Public discussion
The Q3 summary is favorable to Adamczyk's alternative path and thereby documents a change from the original request. Independent contemporary forum/X material remains missing. [2]
