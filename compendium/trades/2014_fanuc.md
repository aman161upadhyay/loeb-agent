## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q4 2014 purchase, February 2015 disclosure:** Fanuc supplied CNC controls, servo motors, robots and Robodrill machines. Third Point cited approximately 60% worldwide CNC share, high switching costs and an unmatched service organization. The letter's $33 billion market cap, $25 billion EV, $8.5 billion cash and 13x FY15 earnings belong to the letter information set; they are not a reconstructed day-of-purchase model. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“There is a reason that Fanuc remains cheap at 13x FY15 earnings: the company's illogical capital structure which does nothing for shareholder value.” The letter compared its tightly focused product approach to Apple, while recognizing cyclical end markets. It did not claim automation eliminates cyclicality. [1]

## What Third Point did
Bought in Q4 2014 and publicly discussed the investment in the Q4 letter released February 2015. Encouraged investor dialogue and capital return. By the Q1 2015 update, Third Point welcomed a 60% dividend payout, treasury-share cancellation and opportunistic repurchases. Exact private-meeting dates, stake size and exit remain unknown. [1–2]

## What happened after
The Q1 update says the stock responded positively and communication improved. This verifies intermediate changes, not a fully realized return or proof that Third Point alone caused the reform. Date correction: **2014 entry, 2015 public campaign**, not a proven first purchase in 2015. [1–2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Moat:** installed controls and service made substitution difficult. [1]
- **Structural demand:** Chinese automation, robotics penetration and US manufacturing investment. [1]
- **Economics:** automated production and vertical integration supported returns. [1]
- **Idle capital:** cash and treasury stock depressed capital efficiency. [1]
- **Catalyst:** buybacks and dialogue could unlock rerating. [1–2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: quality plus capital-structure repair rather than a distressed operating turnaround. The business-quality case mattered independently of persuading management. [1]

## Public discussion
The May letter is an investor's favorable update, not independent causal attribution. No verified contemporaneous forum/X debate recovered. [2]
