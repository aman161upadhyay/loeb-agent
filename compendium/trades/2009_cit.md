## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**November 2009, not a generic early-2009 bank rescue.** CIT could not fund maturities and had failed to obtain hoped-for TLGP support. Its November 1 pre-arranged Chapter 11 offered a specific claim-purchase opportunity after monitoring rather than guessing at a bailout. Loeb estimated senior unsecured prices at more than 25% below orderly wind-down asset value. These are his asset assumptions, not an independently realized liquidation. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“the Chapter 11 filing provided us with an opportunity to purchase senior unsecured debt” — Third Point Q4 2009 letter reproduced March 8, 2010. [1]

“approximately 0.4x plan book value” — his stated equity creation multiple, distinct from the later quoted stock price. [1]

## What Third Point did
Bought sizeable senior unsecured bonds in November. December emergence consideration was $0.70 in a strip of 7% second-lien notes maturing 2013–17 plus the bondholder class’s pro-rata allocation of 77% of new common. **77% belonged to the creditor class, not Third Point.** The reproduction reports 1.75m Third Point common shares at year-end. [1]

## What happened after
Confirmation occurred December 8, within about six weeks, and common traded from approximately 0.4x creation book to 0.7x on listing, according to Loeb. He retained a large position for deposit-funded lending, regulatory approvals and recovery in non-bank assets. That transformation was a forecast. No complete realized return on notes and equity was recovered. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Asset coverage at distressed bond prices:** Orderly wind-down value supplied a downside benchmark. [1]
- **Short pre-arranged bankruptcy:** A negotiated plan limited time to emergence without eliminating execution risk. [1]
- **Debt-to-equity creation discount:** The investment bought an equity option through a senior claim, not the old common. [1]
- **Deposit-funded model optionality:** Reduced debt and extended maturities gave time for a bank-centric strategy, subject to approval. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: textbook create-equity-through-credit, with a short legal catalyst and an optional operational upside. Distinguish plan book from realizable economic value and class ownership from fund ownership. [1]

## Public discussion
The same article reproduces Einhorn’s more cautious assessment: CIT still needed to determine whether the business could survive or should run off. That is a genuine contemporaneous disagreement about the post-reorganization franchise. Original Q4 letter PDF and full security ledger remain recovery gaps. [1]
