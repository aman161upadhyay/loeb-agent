## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: Q1/Q2 2020 entry.** Disney was facing pandemic disruption in parks/cinemas while Disney+ provided an alternative distribution model. **Conflict:** letters describe initiation in Q2 2020, but Stockcircle and the Q1 2020 filing show 1.43M shares in Q1 followed by a large Q2 add. Use Q1 first reported ownership; exact first purchase unknown. [2,3,5]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

October 7 public letter urged diverting roughly $3B annual dividends to original streaming content rather than restoring payouts; expanding the content budget could accelerate Disney+ adoption. August 2020 letter estimated an addressable streaming opportunity potentially $500B across 750M broadband homes ex-China; this was a thesis forecast, not revenue. [1,2]

## What Third Point did

Q1 2020 initial reported position; Q2 added about 4.08M shares. Public October dividend/content letter; subsequent streaming advocacy in August 2021 letter. Reduced from 4.15M to 2M shares in Q4 2021 and absent Q1 2022. August 2022 letter explicitly says the earlier position was exited in late Q4 2021/early 2022. [3–5]

## What happened after

Closed before the second campaign. Realized P&L unknown. Do not apply Disney II’s Hulu/ESPN/board agenda retroactively to the October 2020 streaming capital-allocation case. [1,4,5]
