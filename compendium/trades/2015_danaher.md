## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Summer 2015 entry:** Danaher had announced its Pall acquisition and subsequent split in May. The Q1 2016 letter explicitly says Third Point initiated after those announcements “last summer.” Thus a **2019 initiation is incorrect**: 2019 is an ownership update. DBS, based on continuous improvement, supported integration across hundreds of acquisitions and a shift toward life-science/healthcare businesses. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“We initiated a position following the announcements last summer which mark a transformational step in Danaher's decade-long efforts to continuously improve its portfolio of businesses.” The letter called Fortive an industrial-focused “mini-Danaher” and described the importance of culture and operating principles after a management meeting. [1]

## What Third Point did
Bought in summer 2015 and added by the Q1 2016 letter after management engagement. Underwrote both New Danaher and Fortive as compounding opportunities; the letter expected separation in Q3 2016. Filing compilation shows 3.71 million DHR shares at each of June, September and December 2019, after a Q1 addition. This does not prove Fortive retained-lot history. [1–2]

## What happened after
The letter's projected New Danaher mix—60% consumables, 4% organic growth, 100 basis points annual margin improvement and over 100% FCF conversion—is a **post-split forecast**, not an achieved entry snapshot. Later 2019 ownership is verified; full investment return and the eventual boundary between separate DHR ownership episodes remain unresolved. [1–2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **System/culture:** DBS underpinned repeated integration and operational improvement. [1]
- **Event:** Pall and the split highlighted distinct portfolios. [1]
- **Mix:** consumables/life sciences reduced cyclicality. [1]
- **Per-share growth:** margins, cash conversion and deployment supported compounding. [1]
- **Valuation:** attractive relative to its own historical premium. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: event-driven entry evolving into long-duration quality ownership. A target company's existing separation plan can be a catalyst without Third Point originating it. [1]

## Public discussion
The Q1 letter expressly connects operating culture to future success; a 13F-only reconstruction misses that reasoning. The holdings compiler is secondary, and its market values are not transaction prices. Authentic contemporaneous forum/X debate not recovered. [1–2]
