## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**After January 2014 selloff:** Third Point bought Argentina's main oil company alongside a re-entry into selected sovereign securities. Potential creditor settlement and access to international financing were uncertain catalysts; expected 2015 political change was not a known election result. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“we re-entered our long position in select government credit securities and initiated a stake in Argentina's main oil company, YPF.” — Q2 2014 letter. This establishes distinct equity and sovereign-credit positions, not a single interchangeable instrument. [1]

## What Third Point did
Initiated YPF after the selloff and explained the normalization thesis in July. Exact position size, cost, hedge and final exit unknown. Sovereign credit is separately covered in `2014_argentina_credit.md`. [1]

## What happened after
No YPF-lot P&L recovered. Later sovereign profits/losses must not be assigned to the equity investment. [1]
