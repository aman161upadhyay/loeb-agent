## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: Q2 2022 re-entry.** Streaming transformation was still the broad context; the August letter is post-entry advocacy. It congratulated Chapek on parks/ESPN/DTC momentum and said confidence in the trajectory supported repurchasing a significant stake. Do not use subsequent leadership change or later Disney profitability as pre-entry evidence. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

August 15: five initiatives—cost cutting/disposal of weak assets; continued dividend suspension in favor of debt repayment, buybacks or reinvestment; early acquisition of Comcast’s Hulu minority with Disney+ integration; reassess an ESPN spinoff; board refresh in technology, advertising and consumer engagement. On ESPN: “Can any synergies that exist between the two companies be replicated by contractual arrangements?” [1]

## What Third Point did

Q2 common 1M shares; Q3 1.4M; Q4 950k; absent Q1 2023. **Not total beneficial exposure:** September 30 support agreement discloses 6.35M beneficial shares, much more than common-only 13F. September 11 backed off ESPN separation after learning more about integration potential. September 30 support agreement installed Carolyn Everson, not Robert/Michael Froman, and imposed support/standstill obligations. [2–4]

## What happened after

Q4 2022 letter lists Disney among top detractors. Public common exited Q1 2023; exact derivative unwind and realized P&L unknown. No defensible claim that later Hulu control/ESPN strategy generated a realized campaign win for Third Point. [4,6]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Useful reversal: Loeb abandoned a prominent proposed remedy and accepted a board settlement, then exited common quickly. The 6.35M beneficial exposure versus 1.4M common prevents underestimating size. [1–4]

## Public discussion

Disney’s September support agreement is stronger size/governance evidence than media summaries or a common-only tracker. ESPN-spinoff advocacy was withdrawn, so it must not be treated as an unchanged lifelong conviction. [2,3]
