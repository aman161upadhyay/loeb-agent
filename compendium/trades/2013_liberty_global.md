## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q1 2013:** Liberty shares fell following the announced Virgin Media takeover. Third Point bought the acquirer while also holding a large Virgin Media stake. Exact target-security cost/hedge and whether the exposure was risk arbitrage versus independent fundamental ownership remain unknown. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“Liberty's strategic value as the primary alternative to the incumbent telecom operator's fixed infrastructure in its markets is overlooked.” The letter expected approximately 20% annual share compounding after closing; this was prospective. [1]

## What Third Point did
Bought Liberty in the postannouncement decline and underwrote buybacks and combined-company value. Separate Virgin Media lot chronology was not recovered, so no fictional second trade or duplicate profit is supplied. [1]

## What happened after
