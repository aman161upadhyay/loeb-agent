## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q1 2017:** European banks traded at depressed valuations and UniCredit completed a €13 billion capital raise in March. The issuer-wide amount is not Third Point's stake. New CEO Mustier and recapitalization provided a turnaround framework. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“We were drawn to UniCredit by its low valuation and the rights issue. We believe in the medium-term story because of its new CEO, Jean Pierre Mustier.” — Q1 letter. [1]

## What Third Point did
Invested around the Q1 recapitalization opportunity and discussed the case in April. Exact purchase mechanics, size and exit remain unknown. [1]

## What happened after
No realized investment return recovered. A recapitalized bank is not automatically a profitable common-equity investment. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Capital event:** reduce uncertainty through a rights issue. [1]
- **Price:** low valuation offered upside. [1]
- **Executor:** specific new CEO supported confidence. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: capital-markets entry plus management/earnings repair, not debt ownership; do not put UniCredit equity into the credit book merely because it is a bank. [1]

## Public discussion
Market Folly summarizes the favorable case; no authenticated contemporary forum/X opposition recovered. [1]
