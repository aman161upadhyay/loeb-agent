## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: January 29, 2025 public campaign.** Existing cornerstone IPO investment from 2021, not a first-ever 2025 purchase. Letter says 9.9% of **Class A** shares, not of total votes/equity. Insider privatization proposal raised controller/process conflicts. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

“we applaud the decision to return the Company to private ownership”. But Loeb demanded an independent competitive process maximizing value, challenged Ron Burkle/Yucaipa’s super-voting control and an alleged sweetheart deal, and wanted qualified outside bidders. These are campaign allegations, not legal findings. [1]

## What Third Point did

Public letter/13D January 29. Held 829,988 common at Q1/Q2. August 20 amended 13D reports zero for August 18 event. Same day issuer announced MCR-led $9/share take-private agreement. Do **not** equate agreement price with Third Point sale proceeds absent transaction records. [1–3]
