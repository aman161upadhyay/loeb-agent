## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q1 2019 entry; June 13 public deck:** Sony was now a different mix from Sony I, with valuable gaming, music, pictures and image sensors. The deck's FY18 adjusted operating-profit breakdown was gaming 43%, music 16%, pictures 8%, sensors 20%, excluding financial services and corporate overhead. Its 8x EV/EBIT and 50% discount were Third Point's dated estimates, not audited intrinsic value. [1,3]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“A Stronger Sony” — June 13 deck title. It described “Sony Technologies” as a Japanese technology champion and “New Sony” as a creative entertainment leader. This time the proposed separation was **semiconductors/image sensors**, not 2013's minority entertainment listing. [1]

**Hindsight Q4 2019:** “we saw an incredible collection of media assets: the world's largest video game platform, a top-three music label, and a top-five Hollywood film studio”. The annual letter says cloud-gaming fears had contributed to entry mispricing. [3]

## What Third Point did
Initiated in Q1 2019, held discussions, published the June 13 presentation and advocated sensor separation, non-core listed-stake monetization and disciplined redeployment. Following Sony's September review, the Q3 letter criticized a status-quo conclusion while continuing constructive dialogue. Reuters' August 2020 account explicitly distinguishes sold ADRs from a still-large Tokyo ordinary holding. [1–3,5]

## What happened after
Sony contributed positively in 2019 even without immediate implementation of the headline spin. Do not call a US 13F disappearance a complete economic exit; ordinary shares remained according to 2020 reporting. Exact later final exit, aggregate P&L and allocation among securities remain unknown. [2–3,5]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Gaming:** recurring subscriptions and live services moderated old hardware-cycle assumptions. [1]
- **Media:** streaming demand improved monetization of libraries and music rights. [1]
- **Sensors:** a global leader deserved standalone attention/capital. [1]
- **Structure:** separate distinct media and semiconductor investor propositions. [1]
- **Capital:** listed stakes/net cash could support focused reinvestment or repurchases. [1–2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a new entry into a transformed franchise, not merely revival of an old rejected idea. Retain independent downside/value analysis because the company could again reject intervention. [1–3]

## Public discussion
Sony's September 2019 stakeholder letter defended integration and reviewed the activist proposals; it is crucial opposing evidence. The Q3 letter's disappointment should be read against that response rather than as uncontested fact. No authenticated contemporary Loeb X thread recovered. [2,6]
