## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: June 2020 equity recapitalization.** Third Point had held distressed bonds before entering post-reorganization equity through a PIPE. This is a capital-structure transition, not an initial discovery of the utility; earlier 13F ownership is separately indexed. Wildfire liability and regulation were central risks. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words

August 6: “good business/bad capital structure”. The thesis was the survival/value of core utility operations through restructuring, followed by operational and regulatory derisking. The February 2023 letter forecast dividend restoration and valued 2024 earnings at 16x for a $21.50 target; that target is **later analysis**, not the PIPE entry price. [1,3]

## What Third Point did

Distressed bonds → Q2 2020 PIPE / post-reorg common equity. February 2023 praised Patti Poppe and legislative/regulatory certainty. November 2023 called PCG the largest equity position and discounted enough to be less rate-sensitive than peers. February 2025 defended AB1054 protections after Los Angeles fires, noting PG&E did not serve that region. [1–5]
