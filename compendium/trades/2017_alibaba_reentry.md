## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Q2 2017 re-entry:** Third Point saw an advertising-platform improvement and renewed revenue acceleration. This is not an uninterrupted continuation of the 2014 IPO investment; the letter explicitly says reinitiated. Guidance for FY2018 revenue growth of 45–49% was forward-looking. [1]

## Loeb's thesis in his own words
“We view these changes as an important catalyst for meaningful revenue acceleration over the next few years”. — Q2 2017 letter, discussing advertising changes. [1]

## What Third Point did
Reinitiated in Q2 and articulated a core-plus-assets valuation. Subsequent exact sizing, sales and final exit unknown. [1]

## What happened after
The Q3 2018 loser list includes Alibaba; a strong growth thesis did not prevent a later adverse quarter. No aggregate realized return recovered. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Product:** ad-platform changes improved monetization. [1]
- **Inflection:** earnings path could exceed embedded expectations. [1]
- **Valuation:** separate core commerce from other assets. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
