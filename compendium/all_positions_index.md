## Reading rules and verification

- First/last are **first/last quarter observed inside this window**, not inception/exit dates. Positions can disappear and re-enter; CUSIP changes, spin-offs, share classes and renamings are not collapsed. A last quarter before Q2 2026 means last observed, not proof all economic exposure ended.
- Peak is the greatest reported **quarter-end market value**, in USD thousands; it is not cost, realized proceeds, AUM percentage or intraday peak. Options use reportable underlying-security value, not premium, and cannot be added to common stock to infer capital invested. Post-2022 SEC dollar values were divided by 1,000; older SEC values already use thousands. Values displayed by trackers may round.
- All 54 quarters were read. Q3 2013 uses the November 19 RESTATEMENT. Nine paginated tracker pages (2020–22 and Q2 2025) showed only 50 holdings; their complete SEC text filings were parsed instead, preserving all 126 Q2 2021 rows. This is not a top-holdings-only index.
- Research triangulation: [13f.finance](https://13f.finance/fund/third-point) (2026-08-14 filing indexed by search; direct fetch returned Cloudflare Tunnel error), [Stockcircle](https://stockcircle.com/portfolio/daniel-loeb) (accessed 2026-10-25), [13f.info manager ledger](https://13f.info/manager/0001040273-third-point-llc) (accessed 2026-10-25), then SEC for complete tables. Stockcircle live portfolio valuations are not quarter-end values and were not used for peaks. Whalewisdom searches did not yield an independently usable complete historical export.
- Cross-checks: [Disney transactions](https://stockcircle.com/portfolio/daniel-loeb/dis/transactions), [Intel](https://stockcircle.com/portfolio/daniel-loeb/intc/transactions), [BBWI](https://stockcircle.com/portfolio/daniel-loeb/bbwi/transactions), [Alphabet](https://stockcircle.com/portfolio/daniel-loeb/googl/transactions), all accessed 2026-10-25.
