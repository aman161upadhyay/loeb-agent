# Third Point / Daniel Loeb — 1995–2012 initiation compendium

## Scope and reading rules

**35 evidence-backed case files**, plus explicit requested-case exclusions where manager attribution could not be verified. This is not a claim that every Third Point trade since its 1995 launch is reconstructed. No specific 1995–97 position with an attributable original rationale was verified; earliest recovered entry is Agribrands in March 1998. The founding date is confirmed in the [New Jersey allocator memorandum, March 18, 2011](https://nj.gov/treasury/doinvest/pdf/AlternativeInvestments/HedgeFund/ThirdPoint-PershingSquare.pdf).

- **Primary-first:** SEC-filed Loeb letters and original investor letters supply the main reasoning; issuer filings verify responses. Secondary press and reproduced letters are labeled. Holdings alone do not establish rationale. Numbered sources in each case include URL and date.
- **Entry / disclosure / retention:** filename years follow the earliest identifiable episode where exact first purchase is unknown. PDL begins with 2006 dialogue; Acorda is grouped with the 2006 clinical event, not verified pretrial ownership. Dade uses the 2002 distressed-cycle episode, with holdings first independently checked at year-end 2003. Huntsman and financial-shorts filenames identify 2008 decision evidence, not proven first fills. Front matter and body disclose these limitations.
- **Unknown is intentional:** unrecovered exact dates, weighted costs, sizes, exits, distributions and complete P&L stay unknown. Share-price change, market value, sale proceeds, corporate acquisition value and fund profit are different quantities.
- **Status convention:** closed is an archival grouping for historical cases, not proof of a complete liquidation. Unknown exit stays unknown. Because the schema lacks an unknown outcome enum, ongoing means **archival economic outcome unresolved**, not asserted present ownership. Some win labels explicitly mean governance/structural success or interim gains, not verified realized profit. Read the outcome text.
- **Point-in-time firewall:** later results and retrospective explanations are separated. Targets, stress scenarios and proposed asset values are not realized outcomes. Era A tags/pattern comparisons are research interpretation, not claimed quotations of a later manifesto.
- **Public discussion:** contemporaneous issuer rebuttals, press and sourced commentary are included. No authenticated Mr. Pink message-board post, VIC/COBF thread or period Reddit/Twitter discussion was recovered for these specific cases; none is invented.

- Greece: new post-PSI 20-bond UK-law strip bought Q3 2012 around 17 cents, not pre-haircut debt bought at par.

Do not invent control-introduction dates or turn a probability-weighted 2% downside guideline into a hard stop-loss.

4. **Ford/Dana:** July 2009 primary letter establishes inclusion and industry-level rationale, not exact claims, prices, individual recoveries or fund return.
