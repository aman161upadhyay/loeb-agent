# David Einhorn — accounting-intensive long/short value

Comparative profile, 2026-10-25. Distinguish Greenlight Capital funds from listed Greenlight Capital Re; the latter's insurance and investment results are not a direct substitute for hedge-fund returns.

## Philosophy, sourcing and valuation

Einhorn tests discrepancies between reported accounts, economic reality, management claims and market valuation. Both cheap longs and overstated/problematic shorts belong in the opportunity set. *Fooling Some of the People All of the Time* is a detailed primary account of the Allied Capital dispute: forensic work, management responses and prolonged realization risk, not a simple claim that an expensive stock must fall. [1]

Search filings, loan and asset marks, cash conversion, accounting changes, capital structures and conference presentations. For longs, estimate normalized earnings and asset/cash-flow value; for shorts, examine whether reported earnings and solvency depend on aggressive assumptions or fragile financing. A short's correct fundamental diagnosis does not eliminate borrow, squeeze, timing or management-response risks. Apple illustrates capital-allocation activism within an otherwise attractive business rather than fraud research. [1–3]

## Construction, duration, exits and avoidance

Greenlight has used a concentrated set of important long and short theses. The 2015 letter says that being “very wrong” loses money and describes reducing gross exposure by about 30 percentage points during a difficult quarter. That is a dated response, not a permanent optimal gross or net exposure. Long and short notional are economically asymmetric: short losses can grow as price rises; offsetting dollars do not remove factor, squeeze or liquidity risk. [4]

Hold while price versus value and the thesis justify it; exit when a thesis fails, value is realized or prospective return no longer compensates for risk. Avoid equating management assurances with evidence and treating a good accounting critique as a complete short-sizing policy. No universal numerical stop, fixed holding period or exact concentration rule is established in these sources. [1–4]

## Decisions and stated reasoning

| Decision | Stated rationale and evidence |
|---|---|
| Allied Capital short, disclosed 2002 | Questioned asset valuations and accounting in a lending/investment company; the book traces research and the lengthy public dispute. The book is retrospective and should not be backdated into an early replay packet. [1] |
| Lehman short, 2007–08 | Challenged real-estate valuations, leverage and the credibility of reported balance-sheet strength; his 2008 presentations supply the contemporary reasoning. Differentiate identifying vulnerability from knowing the precise bankruptcy date. [2] |
| Apple “iPrefs,” February 2013 | Praised the operating franchise but argued that perpetual preferred distributions could make cash-generation value more visible and return value to owners. Apple's business quality and capital allocation were separate claims. [3] |
| SunEdison, 2015–16 | Originally a value/growth opportunity tied to renewable development and financing structures; after deterioration Greenlight sought governance changes and asset sales. The activist response is not proof that the entry liquidity assumptions were sound. Exact initial underwriting detail requires the original entry letter. [4–5] |

## Mistakes and what changed

The 2015 letter identifies serious problems in CONSOL, SunEdison and Micron and reduces gross risk. SunEdison shows the danger of complexity and financing dependence overwhelming an apparent discount; the company later entered bankruptcy. The board-seat campaign in January 2016 illustrates the temptation—and sometimes necessity—to change tools after a long deteriorates. It must not be relabeled a successful original operating thesis. [4–6]

The 2018 annual letter, reproduced in January 2019, distinguishes the concentrated mistakes of 2015 from broader losses in 2018 and defends the underlying process. This is the manager's self-assessment, not an independent finding that the process was sound. A retrospective should examine persistent factor exposure and opportunity cost without assuming that either a bad year proves no skill or eventual recovery validates every original position. [7]

## How an agent must be prompted differently

**Proposed instruction:** “Reconcile cash flows, assets, liabilities and management narrative. Write both the benign and adverse accounting interpretations. For shorts, separately underwrite borrow availability, squeeze risk, path dependence and realization time; a correct thesis is not enough. Permit selective financial activism but do not make intervention mandatory.” Relative to Ackman, allow a standing long/short toolkit and more forensic financial complexity; relative to a fraud detector, require valuation and implementation economics. [1–7; prompt is synthesis]
