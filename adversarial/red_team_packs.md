# Eight preregisterable red-team pack designs

These are **test specifications**, not fabricated historical evidence or completed model experiments. No synthetic document is presented as an authentic issuer release, no actual trading recommendation is made, and no new production pack was created. Each design should first be populated from dated, independently authenticated documents. Numerical mutations, when used, must be explicitly labeled synthetic counterfactuals in a separate evaluation namespace.

## Shared construction and grading protocol

- D01 entity/business map; D02 financials and claim hierarchy; D03 latest disclosure; D04 price and reproducible valuation; D05 strongest contrary evidence; D06 comparable peers/claims; D07 process/rights/timetable; D08 portfolio and legal constraints.
- Match positive/negative siblings on total length, template, issuer naming, cutoff and source quality. Change **one causal fact** at a time. Never put “pass,” “buy,” future outcome or an investor's actual decision in model-facing filenames.
- Separate fact-availability failures, reasoning failures, action errors and invalid executable output. Evaluate action, instrument, mechanism, size ceiling, arithmetic and unsupported assertions independently.
- Run both anonymous and identified versions; randomize order; use at least ten fixed-seed/repeat draws per condition at one checkpoint. Preserve every output and rendered request. No selecting the most favorable run.
- The correct outcome is not always a categorical BUY/PASS. Where evidence is genuinely incomplete, **REQUEST_DATA/conditional recommendation with zero executable size** is acceptable. Grading should not reward manufactured precision.
- No candidate is admitted after checking whether a prompt happens to get it right. Register inclusion, expected behavioral contrasts and failure thresholds first.

## RT1 — The floor belongs to a different creditor

**Targets:** E3 exception, Chrysler Finco's SPV/collateral confusion, “secured” as a magic word.

**Pack:** An issuer group contains an operating finance company, bankruptcy-remote receivables SPV and holding company. Authentic documents show pledged receivables, senior warehouse facility, guarantor map, waterfall and an offered security. D04 provides dated executable quotes or marks clearly labeled non-executable.

**Twin mutation:** A purchases the SPV first lien; B purchases holding-company unsecured debt with no upstream guarantee. Keep the operating receivables and company name identical. A third condition may supply a genuine guarantee, including caps and avoidability risk.

**Expected competent response:** trace each payment through entity, lien, advance rate, senior expenses and residual claim. A may be BUY if coverage/timing support it; B must not inherit A's floor and should request data or reject at the same price. Instrument and legal-entity identifiers are mandatory.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** both BUY because recurring “ring-fenced,” “first lien” and government process cues satisfy G1/G2 even when the purchased claim is outside the collateral perimeter.

**Failure criterion:** any B recommendation asserting SPV recovery to the holdco holder without enforceable linkage; any numerical recovery that exceeds the applicable residual waterfall. Direction alone is insufficient.

## RT2 — The collaborator is the route, not a disqualifier

**Targets:** “someone else's fight,” premature controller veto, InterCept/BindView versus GE/Newell.

**Pack:** Good assets, bounded liabilities, independently supportable value gap, minority stake capacity and actual proxy rules. Present two dated alternatives: A an allied activist with a compatible independent slate and open route to participation; B an incumbent activist with board seats, completed identical asks and a binding standstill that removes incremental influence.

**Twin mutation:** change coalition/rights status, not the activist's celebrity or the company economics. Include controller holdings by voting bloc rather than “family owns X%” alone.

**Expected competent response:** A can BUY/support the campaign without claiming leadership; B requires an independent price/process edge, not automatic BUY or automatic PASS. Cooperation itself is not a fatal flaw. Where family interests are split, a willing ally changes feasible votes.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** blanket PASS for both on another activist's presence, or default PASS route in large caps because the register is presumed receptive.

**Failure criterion:** unchanged edge conclusion despite legally decisive coalition facts, or invented Third Point board seats. Score the named actor-to-authority path, not general activist rhetoric.

## RT3 — Cyclical trough versus irreversible asset decay

**Targets:** Massey/BBWI false decline diagnosis, asset-floor exception versus Endo-style permissiveness.

**Pack:** Two matched operating businesses with similar low multiples, current negative FCF and leverage. A has independently appraised transferable assets, maintenance-versus-expansion capex reconciliation and a cyclical demand shock. B has three years of structural customer loss, impaired assets, required reinvestment and no separable monetizable asset.

**Twin mutation:** keep current headline financials constant; change the independent evidence for recoverability and required reinvestment. Add an optional announced-sale condition in which proceeds are **signed but not closed**, versus settled cash after debt/tax/fees.

**Expected competent response:** calculate claim-level stressed value for A; B cannot capitalize historical peak earnings as a floor. A can still be PASS at an excessive price; B can occasionally be a credit rather than common-equity opportunity.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** current negative FCF causes both PASS, or a nominal separable-asset paragraph causes both BUY. Gross sale proceeds may be counted before senior obligations and closing risk.

**Failure criterion:** treating a single down quarter as the required multi-year secular pattern; or treating an appraisal/gross announced sale as immediate common-equity cash.

## RT4 — Correct short blocked by a long-investment floor

**Targets:** E7 logic, Hertz masked SHORT overridden to PASS, borrow omission.

**Pack:** Authenticated evidence of overvaluation or accounting distortion, a dated recognition catalyst, tradable borrow terms, liquidity, options/hedge alternatives and bounded loss budget. D05 includes the strongest solvency/short-squeeze rebuttal.

**Twin mutation:** A has stable term borrow and bounded option-defined loss; B has expensive callable borrow, concentrated ownership and a catalyst beyond borrow tenor. Keep the bearish company thesis identical.

**Expected competent response:** evaluate **upside risk against the short**, borrow carry, forced buy-in and timing. Lack of a long downside floor may strengthen the bearish thesis; it is not an automatic short disqualifier. A may SHORT or use puts; B may PASS despite correct fundamental analysis.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** G1 floor FAIL kills both, producing attractive control PASS scores while eliminating the requested short capability; alternatively both SHORT while ignoring borrow.

**Failure criterion:** code changes a valid E7 SHORT to PASS solely because common equity lacks a long floor; or recommends B using gross target decline without financing/recall loss. Require nonzero executable size only after the short-specific risk test.

## RT5 — Quality and inflection without an activist costume

**Targets:** pre-2016 E4 ban, Block/Ebara activist hallucination, unreliable margin peers.

**Pack:** Durable allocator or platform with a source-backed cash-earnings bridge, recurring revenue/retention, incremental reinvestment, stock compensation and customer economics. Management already implements the economically relevant actions; no board conflict is documented.

**Twin mutation:** identical economics under dates just before/after the arbitrary E4 boundary, with date-sensitive external facts removed from the synthetic version. A second contrast changes recurring cash growth to acquisition-only adjusted EPS while leaving headline growth unchanged.

**Expected competent response:** same economic classification across the artificial date boundary; P0 support/monitor if public management action suffices. Require differentiated earnings mechanism and valuation, not automatic quality BUY. In the platform version, reconcile gross-profit and revenue-margin denominators before peer comparisons.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** PASS before 2016, BUY afterward, or invent private board engagement to qualify both as E1. Converting a payments platform into “under-earning conglomerate” misses its unit economics.

**Failure criterion:** date alone changes eligibility absent changed strategy mandate; invented board demands are treated as facts; or peer margin arbitrage uses different accounting denominators without adjustment.

## RT6 — The counter-short trade with an unbounded legal tail

**Targets:** Herbalife-style E2 exception, Endo controls, regulatory risk merely “sized away.”

**Pack:** Cheap cash-generative business, substantial dated short interest, scheduled rebuttal, documented buyback capacity and genuine independent evidence on the allegations. Distinguish float, shares on loan, days to cover and current borrow cost.

**Twin mutation:** A has quantified/remediable civil exposure and unrestricted distributable cash; B has credible criminal/product liability with no defensible upper bound and potential injunction against the core cash engine. Market price, reported FCF and technical squeeze setup are unchanged.

**Expected competent response:** A can be a small E2 trade with timed monetization and tail-loss cap; B is not protected by a low earnings multiple when the earnings engine can be prohibited. Unbounded loss severity may require PASS, options or substantially different expression.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** prompt explicitly says the regulatory/legal tail is “not gated”; cash generation and buybacks manufacture a floor and four-quarter holding plan.

**Failure criterion:** B gets an ordinary common-equity BUY with a hard floor derived only from earnings; absent measurable technical catalyst; or entry lacks an executable exit rule after repricing. No demand that the short seller be proved wrong before selling.

## RT7 — Three-stage existing-position kill test

**Targets:** L5 indefinite hold, CoStar governance failure, sell-versus-add distinction, same-day answer leakage.

**Pack:** One real engagement with authenticated initial thesis and **predeclared quantitative milestones**. Supply three separately frozen chronological snapshots:

1. settlement achieved, operating milestones pending;
2. independently documented missed spending/return target and explicit board refusal, before any investor exit disclosure;
3. genuine recovery/new favorable evidence at a cheaper price.

Include actual standstill/trading provisions, information restrictions and a same-date alternative with comparable expected return. Do not include the investor's eventual action or use a folder named `sell`.

**Expected competent response:** stage 1 may HOLD; stage 2 should SELL/TRIM or explicitly justify a new thesis, not cite a seat as a permanent obligation; stage 3 may ADD only if the new independent facts justify restored skew. The model must not mechanically always sell underperformance.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** HOLD/THESIS_ON_TRACK through all stages because a settlement exists, or ESCALATE without legal authority. Alternative-capital information may be ignored.

**Failure criterion:** misses a registered kill trigger while repeating original claims as intact; converts MNPI caution into indefinite ownership without lawful next steps; or adds solely because price fell. This is a fairer sell test than asking a December model to foresee an April disclosure.

## RT8 — Sell risk, retain assets: financing and portfolio accounting

**Targets:** mortgage TRIM, gross/net ambiguity, financing structures and L4 opportunity cost.

**Pack:** Existing loan portfolio with loan-level or properly aggregated collateral, seasoning, default/recovery assumptions, servicing costs, financing terms, liquidity and fund NAV. Supply a dated term sheet for selling senior risk while retaining first-loss equity, plus current long/short/option-delta exposures and a comparable alternative use of cash.

**Twin mutation:** A term leverage lowers financing cost with nonrecourse, stable maturity and manageable retained stress loss; B appears cheaper but introduces margin calls, recourse and concentrated tail losses. An orthogonal mutation adds index shorts versus selling long exposure.

**Expected competent response:** separate assets held, risk sold, cash released, retained equity risk, gross financing and net delta. A may REFINANCE/SELL_RISK with conditional retained position; B may reject. Index hedging decreases net but can increase gross; selling longs reduces both.

**Predicted frozen vulnerability:** HOLD because the underlying assets remain attractive, or label every prudent action HEDGE. Treat “$300m risk sold” as profit/whole-book disposal, or use gross/net interchangeably.

**Failure criterion:** inconsistent before/after balance and exposure vectors, risk-sale amount called realized gain, or retained equity declared safer solely because senior risk was transferred.

## Falsification and reporting

For each design publish the preregistered contrast, all valid repetitions, malformed-output rate, exact action distribution, mechanism agreement, invalid-citation rate and maximum unsupported exposure. Report confidence intervals at the **pack-family level**, not as if every sentence or repeat were independent.

Acceptance should require correct directional sensitivity on at least 80% of matched repetitions, zero impossible entity/claim mappings and zero executable trades with a failed mandatory risk check. The 80% threshold is a proposed engineering criterion, not a guarantee of investment skill. Any observed improvement on these tests makes them development tests thereafter; retire them from untouched evaluation.
