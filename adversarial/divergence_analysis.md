# Divergences: saved artifacts, not retrospective explanations

Use the path shorthand in [README](README.md). The fourteen rows below exhaust the published calibration divergence table. “Cause” distinguishes an observed stage failure from a plausible causal interpretation; one paired run cannot isolate memory effects.

## Calibration

### 1. `2005_massey_buy`: both PASS, but not the stated shared floor veto

**Record:** K says BUY/E1/P2, valuable reserves at a management discount, prospective $800–900m EBITDA versus ~$750m consensus and $300–350m capex. R(N) identifies no event and fails edge/route; its floor is **UNSURE**, not FAIL. It says, “No specific catalyst, restructuring, or fixable governance issue is present.” R(M) has five FAILs, including floor, and likewise rejects the activist catalyst.

**Pack:** D01 acknowledges valuable coal reserves and strong demand. D02 shows 2004 CFO $226.7m less capex $347.2m = **-$120.5m**, and Q3 EBITDA $99.4m includes $38.2m reserve gain and $4.1m legal-reserve benefit: normalized $57.1m. That is not evidence that reserves disappeared. Conversely the pack does not reproduce the key's full prospective $800–900m model.

**Internal defect:** D02 says “D04 now supplies November 15 close and dated valuation bridge”; D04 says no reliable November 15 close/current EV multiple was recovered. A G1 conflict-resolver should flag this contradiction rather than silently reject the investment.

**Diagnosis:** failure to create an E1 plan from a documented problem, missing asset valuation and inconsistent pack descriptions. The scoreboard's shared “melting ice cube” diagnosis does not describe the named run. Also November fresh capital after September ownership is not the original entry.

### 2. `2009_chrysler_finco_buy`: direction and size explanation are wrong targets

Both runs BUY **3%**, medium conviction, instrument **bond**; K's instrument is **loan**, with first-lien prices in the 50s and second liens in the 30s, P5. Named is PARTIAL and masked MATCH. The scoreboard explanation “smaller size” cannot distinguish two equal 3% outputs; K explicitly says issuer NAV weight is unavailable.

Named reasoning treats a **$1.5bn Treasury-backed financing SPV** as hard floor. Masked reasoning gets credit for a first-priority lien on new consumer collateral. The critical question is whether that collateral secures **the purchased loan**, not whether some government facility has collateral. The key itself concedes that GAO establishes separate operations, not a complete legal ring fence. Precise claim-to-collateral mapping is absent.

**Diagnosis:** instrument fidelity and legal-entity grounding matter more than the apparent named/masked direction discrepancy. Both BUYs can be economically wrong if they buy the wrong claim or assume another lender's security. May 1 is after actual April purchases and is labeled a fresh-capital counterfactual.

### 3. `2012_greece_buy`: pre-election counterfactual miscalled a historical miss

R(N) floor FAIL triggers recomputed hard fail, overriding a bullish BUY draft to PASS. Final memo still says it is initiating, and target size remains **3%**. R(M) BUY **4%** names the Hellenic Republic/Greek sovereign debt; the allegedly masked issuer is recognized.

K explicitly says actual entry was **Q3 at around 17 cents**, after the June 17 election and later policy developments; pack date is **June 15**. Legal protections and official-sector incentives can create asymmetric expected value, but sovereign creditors have no corporate collateral liquidation floor.

**Diagnosis:** a legitimate ambiguity in sovereign payoff modeling plus a demonstrably mismatched action date. Do not infer that named PASS is a memory failure or that masked BUY proves correct sovereign underwriting.

### 4. `2013_hlf_buy`: penalty compares issuer ownership with fund NAV

## Holdout: every nonmatch and all five apparently successful purchases

Named says it lacks visibility to justify “a significant increase.” K's first driver is the **later share-count increase from 1.975m to 3.085m**, not a reason accessible at June 30; K expressly calls precise add rationale inferred.

## Shared causes and non-causes

1. **Not one “sell problem”:** CoStar is governance-remedy response; Soho event monetization; Kenvue factor/exposure rotation; mortgages financing optimization; portfolio reduction gross/net accounting. One more generic sell paragraph will not solve them.
2. **Not a demonstrated named-memory advantage:** masked identifiers reappear; some named runs fail while masked pass; no repeated matched-seed experiment separates identity from variation.
3. **Not all conservative PASSes are errors:** Greece's early date, missing loan documents and incomplete merger inputs justify uncertainty. Score economic plausibility separately from eventual action resemblance.
4. **Not all BUY matches are successes:** fake targets, wrong claims, unbounded risk and invented activism can coexist with the historical trade label.
5. **Not all missing key drivers are model failures:** later-private diligence, later market prices and future share-count changes are not recoverable from the permitted evidence. Driver availability needs an explicit denominator.
