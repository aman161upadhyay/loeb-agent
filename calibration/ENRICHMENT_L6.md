## Source and cutoff controls

Primary financial sources are issuer annual/interim statements and SEC financial histories. Price observations come from date-filtered Yahoo histories or Digrin historical real-price columns, not current quote metadata. Rates use FRED; index earnings context uses dated Multpl observations. Later-hosted SEC/annual histories are labelled as retrieval sources for historical reported figures. PG&E's later-filed note is used only for July 1 issuance facts; S&P's June 15 forecast remains explicitly a forecast.

Monthly prices, observed monthly-close ranges, diluted-share capitalisation proxies and approximate book adjustments are labelled rather than passed off as exact decision-day observations. Peer tables distinguish last-FY from trailing earnings; guidance ratios are not called consensus. Loss-making P/E and EV/EBITDA are marked NM. PRU means Prudential plc, not US Prudential Financial. Shell ADR/ordinary units and pre/post-demerger PRU periods are not mixed without qualification.

The portfolio additions use the February report published March 4, before the March 16 cutoff. No later quarterly letter, realized de-risking path, manager explanation, security deployment or quarter-end fund return was added. Exposure disclosure is not presented as a live March 16 ledger.

## Remaining limitations / retrieval attempts

- **PG&E emergence cash:** exact unrestricted cash was not resolved from the July 1 issuer release, SEC plan-financing notes and S&P credit analysis. D04 therefore gives explicit cash-dependent net-debt/EV formulas, not zero cash or a fabricated point estimate. The $38.1bn debt anchor is a contemporaneous credit-agency forecast.
- **Portfolio security-specific cash/margin:** the February factsheet, December factsheet and SEC holdings filing do not establish March 16 cash, margin or security-level bond prices/YTM. ETF prices are explicitly per share, not cents per bond par.
- **Historical HY effective yield:** FRED's historical-series endpoint, the eco-archive mirror and DBnomics did not yield a verified March 16 observation. The verified HY OAS and Treasury par yields are supplied instead; their sum is not mislabelled an observed YTM.
- **Precision limits:** several old-class prices/ranges and share-count-based capitalisations remain labelled historical proxies. Archived forward consensus is not substituted with current estimates; where numerical company guidance is available its valuation multiple is separately labelled. Pro-forma insurance book and debt are not claimed to be newly reported February balances.
