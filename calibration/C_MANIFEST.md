## Evidence/format limitations

- Eight required documents per pack, 300–900 body words including explicitly analytical underwriting treatment and source ledger; all source publication dates checked against cutoff. No later investor letter is used as an earlier desk fact.
- Source-native financial observations are retained; no synthetic consensus, target price, undocumented quote, private mark or fund AUM was invented. Source retrieval emphasizes original issuer releases/SEC exhibits and original manager letters/decks or clearly labeled reproductions.
- The repeated analytical treatment is explicitly non-factual framework, not a substitute for sourced business claims. For old/new campaign labels, metadata explains where disclosure-window re-underwriting differs from a literal first purchase.
- Current holdings/proposed actions are excluded from docs. Existing-case original thesis and earlier governance facts are retained; all historical outcomes remain in answer keys only.
- `instrument: common` is the principal replay expression. Keys discuss options/SPV/credit where actual economic exposure differed; hedge common is a schema-compatible principal equity-book label, not a claim every transaction used common.
- Firm AUM is numerical and sourced in every metadata file, but some early observations are materially stale. D08 explicitly dates AUM, US-long holdings and any recovered exposures; no stale observation is represented as exact decision-day risk. Additional source recovery would be needed for A-quality valuation and full fund-context scoring.
