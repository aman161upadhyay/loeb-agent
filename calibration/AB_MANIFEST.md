# Era A/B calibration manifest

All 23 requested IDs are present. B = usable but missing historical market/portfolio inputs; C = material timing or primary-source limitation. No pack is represented as grade A.

## Replay interpretation and firewall

The requested dates and modes are preserved. Several requested 'new' dates postdate real purchases; these are explicitly counterfactual fresh-capital checkpoints, not a claim to predict the original first fill. Several escalation dates follow the first public letter. Greece is the most serious mismatch: the original letter explicitly identifies Q3 entry, while June 15 precedes both the June 17 election and the late-July policy catalyst. The key records the real chronology and the pack does not import those future developments.

Docs are analyst-prepared factual extracts plus explicitly analytical accounting/risk tests, not purported verbatim source documents. Source references identify the original dated records. Same-day event evidence is a closing-of-day snapshot, not necessarily available before the market open. Yahoo's July 22 case is an event-conditioned internal desk replay using negotiated terms; the July 25 filing is not falsely described as public July 22 evidence.

Financial prices, exact fund cash/exposures and many AUM observations were not recovered. Unknown AUM is JSON null rather than a fabricated number; this is an explicit exception to the brief's numeric preference. No forward-filled modern market data are used. Existing ownership supplied by the task is desk state, labeled as such. Source URLs can contain later archive upload dates without importing later document content.

## Material corrections and source gaps

- Star Gas: distressed entry after the collapse; not a fund loss from buying near $24.
- Ligand: December 2006 issuer update describes the December 2, 2005 board arrangement; do not date the first seats a year late.
- Potlatch: primary proxy verifies salary/options, salary freeze and no cash performance bonus. Full CEO-plane criticism was not recovered and is not fabricated.
- Pogo and PDL: initial sale/separation demands were public before the requested dates. These are continuing escalation snapshots, not first-letter forecasts.
- 2008: Partners -32.84% belongs to the hindsight NJ allocator record. No full original Q4 2008 letter recovered; a commonly surfaced Q4 2009 letter is not substituted.
- Delphi: DIP loan is the original instrument; later equity targets and 'largest winner' descriptions are not June facts. Largest-winner ranking remains unverified.
- Chrysler: Finco secured claims are not Carco unsecured claims; actual purchases were in April.
- Yahoo 2013: TRIM, not complete exit; 40m × $29.11 = $1.1644bn gross proceeds, not profit. July 25 actual closing differs from July 24 originally scheduled.
- Greece: primary investor text explicitly says Q3 entry; the requested June date cannot contain the June election result or late-July policy signal.
- Sony: $1.1bn economic exposure includes swaps; 15–20% entertainment listing, not sale of all entertainment. SELL key has L1 primary/L2 secondary.
- Herbalife: January 3 holding predates January 8 checkpoint; March 1 is not verified sale day. Complete original Q1 exit passage remains a gap; exit reasoning is qualified rather than quoted.
- Sotheby's: verified October letter says 9.3%, not requested 9.6%; board seats were obtained by settlement and holding continued into 2019.
- Dow: largest-investment description verified; requested $1.3bn not certified from recovered primary passage.
- Baxter: August 5 primary letter already says nearly 10% including equivalents, worth over $2bn; a supposed 7% August inception is incorrect. No August foreknowledge of Almeida appointment.
- Seven & i: full retrieved Q3 excerpt published November 2; a standalone October 27 signature/publication date was not authenticated.
- Fanuc: actual purchase Q4 2014, disclosure February 9; February 10 is a fresh-capital counterfactual. Independently extracted January issuer tables supply earnings, cash, treasury stock and segment growth; cutoff market price remains missing.

## Validation

All 23 pairs and eight documents per pack were checked for valid JSON, 300–900 whitespace words per doc, three-line headers, compilation dates and original source dates not beyond cutoff, document sizes and total pack <=40KB. Docs were separately scanned for key-field leakage and future-year tokens for manual review. Later years that are expressly prospective guidance or deadlines are not later outcomes. Header dates identify the analyst compilation; separately dated references identify the underlying original documents. This is artifact validation only; no application or workflow was run.
