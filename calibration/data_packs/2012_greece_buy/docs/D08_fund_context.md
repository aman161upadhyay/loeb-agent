The replay is a new sovereign-credit allocation. The desk needs the legal bond package, country scenarios, executable bids and a portfolio loss budget that tolerates a political discontinuity. No existing holding is assumed. Current fund AUM, cash, gross/net and sovereign concentration are not available in the recovered sources. Face amount, market value and percentage of NAV must not be treated as interchangeable.

## Economic cross-check

The investment is the newly issued sovereign bond strip following the private-sector debt exchange, not old par-value claims before the haircut. Repayment depends on government revenues, spending discipline, official funding and continued monetary membership rather than corporate EBITDA. The debt documentation and the composition of creditor claims matter because the official sector's policy incentives can differ from private investors' priorities. [1,2]

The debt exchange precedes the cutoff. The June 17 election is still ahead and its result is unknown. Coalition formation, official reviews and disbursements are contingent. The investor's route is security selection and official/legal-process analysis, not corporate board control. The next relevant checkpoint is political formation and willingness to implement the program. A timetable for financing is an uncertainty rather than a contractual investor-controlled catalyst. [1,2]

## Underwriting interpretation

Portfolio accounting for this desk: use current market exposure, not the issuer ownership percentage, to calculate capital at risk. Gross and net can conceal concentrated sector, currency, liquidity or event dependencies. A credit position and an equity position may respond to the same business shock despite occupying different strategy buckets. Derivative economic exposure, voting shares, collateral and counterparty obligations should be tracked separately. An absent exposure report is not evidence that no such risk exists.

The available record does not justify inventing current cash, top holdings or risk limits. Size must remain conditional where those inputs are missing. For an existing holding, separate the original thesis from new facts and sunk cost; for a fresh idea, do not assume ownership merely because the company has appeared in historical commentary. The fund's ability to wait for value depends on portfolio liquidity and obligations as well as the company's assets. No eventual trade, public campaign choice or realized return is encoded in the mandate above.
