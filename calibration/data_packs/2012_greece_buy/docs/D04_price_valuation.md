Mixed-date benchmark yields are not substitutes for a verified Greek package YTM. At 10¢, a hypothetical move to 20¢ doubles principal value before coupons/costs; a zero recovery loses all invested principal. Neither scenario is a price target or an assigned probability.

A recovery model must distinguish staying in the euro with official funding, further restructuring, and redenomination/default. Cash prices are already deeply distressed, but 10 cents can still fall toward zero. A price-to-yield rerating requires both credible payment continuity and a reasonable discount rate. The pricing reference is a broad indicative distress quote, not an executable bid for the exact bond package under consideration. [1,2; analyst framework]

## Economic cross-check

The PSI exchange has substantially reduced private creditor claims and replaced them with new securities. Exact June 15 strip constituents, settlement quotation, yield curve and accrued-interest schedule were not independently recovered from the primary terms in this pack. The June 15 Reuters report quotes a trader describing Greek bonds near 10 cents on the euro; that is a broad market indication, not a verified executed strip price or Third Point's entry. [1,2]

The election could produce a government unwilling or unable to meet bailout conditions. Deposit flight, a shortage of funding and political deadlock could force capital controls, redenomination or another restructuring. Legal protections help define bargaining rights but cannot create euros when the sovereign lacks funding. Trading liquidity may be least available when an investor wishes to change its political-risk assessment. The imminent binary event argues for special caution on size. [1,2]

## Underwriting interpretation

Valuation discipline for this desk: purchase price, scenario value, realizable proceeds and total investment profit are four different quantities. A prospective return needs both an entry price and an achievable exit/recovery value, including taxes, fees, dilution and time. When an input is absent, an exact multiple, upside percentage or annualized return is not justified. A low nominal price is not itself a margin of safety; the claim's priority and the business's remaining cash requirements determine the floor.

The base case should not depend on perfect execution and the adverse case should not assume a favorable transaction. Component values need a consistent date, accounting scope and treatment of debt. Avoid double-counting cost savings in both higher earnings and a separate asset value. Similarly, excess cash cannot be added without checking access and associated obligations. A peer multiple is a reference, not a contractual takeout price. No later market quotation or realized outcome is substituted for a missing cutoff input.
