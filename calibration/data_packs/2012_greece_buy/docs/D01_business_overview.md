The investment is the newly issued sovereign bond strip following the private-sector debt exchange, not old par-value claims before the haircut. Repayment depends on government revenues, spending discipline, official funding and continued monetary membership rather than corporate EBITDA. The debt documentation and the composition of creditor claims matter because the official sector's policy incentives can differ from private investors' priorities. [1,2]

## Economic cross-check

The PSI exchange has substantially reduced private creditor claims and replaced them with new securities. Exact June 15 strip constituents, settlement quotation, yield curve and accrued-interest schedule were not independently recovered from the primary terms in this pack. The June 15 Reuters report quotes a trader describing Greek bonds near 10 cents on the euro; that is a broad market indication, not a verified executed strip price or Third Point's entry. [1,2]

These bonds should be compared with distressed sovereign claims rather than ordinary euro-area investment-grade debt. Governing law, creditor ranking, collective-action provisions and official-sector seniority can dominate headline yield. A single historical recovery average is not sufficient for a country facing currency redenomination risk. Reuters reports many hedge funds running only 10–30% of permitted risky-asset exposure and fund-manager cash balances of 5.3%, illustrating risk aversion rather than safety. [2]

## Underwriting interpretation

Analyst distinctions, not additional reported facts: identify which assets actually generate cash, which require reinvestment, and which can be transferred independently. Contractual ownership, economic exposure and voting control need not coincide. A recognizable product or attractive asset is not sufficient evidence of attractive common-equity economics. Separate a going-concern franchise from its financing before assigning a floor. Consider what remains if the proposed event does not occur: recurring cash, realizable assets, enforceable priority or only optionality. These distinctions prevent the business description from silently turning into a recommendation.

The observable tests are customer or counterparty retention, unit profitability, required maintenance investment and the ability to fund obligations without diluting the claim being evaluated. Where consolidated reporting combines unlike activities, a segment bridge is needed before comparing margins. Missing segment or legal information is not equivalent to a zero liability. No assumption about a future transaction, governance concession or recovery is embedded in this description.
