Sovereign revenue, fiscal balance and debt replace corporate sales, EPS and book equity. There is no meaningful sovereign P/B, dividend per share or ROE. Early debt gaps, a fully reconciled post-PSI June debt stock and government unrestricted cash remain unresolved after IMF staff tables, the PDMA primary-document archive and Eurostat/ELSTAT historical searches. Principal reductions in an exchange are not free cash flow, and pre-exchange debt cannot be substituted for new-strip face value.

The PSI exchange has substantially reduced private creditor claims and replaced them with new securities. Exact June 15 strip constituents, settlement quotation, yield curve and accrued-interest schedule were not independently recovered from the primary terms in this pack. The June 15 Reuters report quotes a trader describing Greek bonds near 10 cents on the euro; that is a broad market indication, not a verified executed strip price or Third Point's entry. [1,2]

## Economic cross-check

The country votes on June 17 after an earlier election failed to produce a durable government. The June 15 report says investors are reducing risk and some CDS counterparties are declining to quote before the weekend. G20 officials say central banks are prepared to calm markets if needed. That conditional readiness is not a commitment to repay a particular sovereign bond or to keep Greece in the currency union. [2]

A recovery model must distinguish staying in the euro with official funding, further restructuring, and redenomination/default. Cash prices are already deeply distressed, but 10 cents can still fall toward zero. A price-to-yield rerating requires both credible payment continuity and a reasonable discount rate. The pricing reference is a broad indicative distress quote, not an executable bid for the exact bond package under consideration. [1,2; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
