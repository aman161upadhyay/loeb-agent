A useful valuation separates discounted finite-life royalties, realizable commercial-product proceeds and risk-adjusted research value, deducting overhead, taxes and liabilities. No binding whole-company bid is assumed. Expressions of interest are not proceeds, and spending cuts cannot be added to a royalty valuation twice. The market bridge above is not an independent component appraisal.

## Economic cross-check

The November filing supplies a dated balance sheet, not exact January balances or a royalty cash-flow forecast. Gross royalties differ from cash after research, selling costs, taxes and overhead. A finite patent stream cannot support indefinite growth without a replacement assumption.

The clinical failure is evidence that pipeline value can evaporate, not just a temporary market worry. Eliminating research may maximize near-term cash while removing future growth. A split can add duplicate overhead and tax leakage; royalty expiration can make superficially low earnings multiples deceptive. Management has already announced asset sales, so an incremental governance intervention must improve on that plan rather than merely restate it. [1; risk analysis]

## Underwriting interpretation

Valuation discipline for this desk: purchase price, scenario value, realizable proceeds and total investment profit are four different quantities. A prospective return needs both an entry price and an achievable exit/recovery value, including taxes, fees, dilution and time. When an input is absent, an exact multiple, upside percentage or annualized return is not justified. A low nominal price is not itself a margin of safety; the claim's priority and the business's remaining cash requirements determine the floor.

The base case should not depend on perfect execution and the adverse case should not assume a favorable transaction. Component values need a consistent date, accounting scope and treatment of debt. Avoid double-counting cost savings in both higher earnings and a separate asset value. Similarly, excess cash cannot be added without checking access and associated obligations. A peer multiple is a reference, not a contractual takeout price. No later market quotation or realized outcome is substituted for a missing cutoff input.
