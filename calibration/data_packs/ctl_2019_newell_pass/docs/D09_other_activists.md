# D09 — Other activists already in the situation (as of 2019-02-22)

- Starboard Value disclosed a ~4% Newell stake on 9 February 2018 and nominated a full slate of ten directors, including former
  Jarden executives Martin Franklin, Ian Ashken and James Lillie, arguing that the 2016 Jarden merger had been mismanaged and
  that the "Accelerated Transformation Plan" (sale of ~35% of revenue, proceeds to cut debt and buy back stock) was inadequate.
- Carl Icahn disclosed a ~6.9% stake on 16 March 2018 and settled with the company on 18 March 2018: four Icahn-designated
  directors (including Brett Icahn) joined the board, the chairman changed, and the company committed to the asset-sale and
  debt-reduction plan. Starboard settled on 23 April 2018 for three board seats (one of them the former Jarden CFO Ian
  Ashken); the board was reconstituted with eleven directors, seven of them nominated by the two activists.
- By the date of this pack the asset-sale programme was largely executed or announced (Waddington, Rawlings, Goody, Pure
  Fishing, Jostens sold; Process Solutions, Rexair and US Playing Cards announced), with ~$10bn of gross proceeds targeted
  against ~$9bn of net debt at the 2018 peak; the remaining asks (margin, working capital, CEO succession) were already the board's agenda.
- Core sales declined in 2018 (full-year core sales −5.2% on continuing operations; Q4 2018 −1.2%, per D02/D03) and the
  company's 2019 outlook in the Q4 release (15 February 2019) was for a further low-single-digit core sales decline.

Sources: Starboard 13D and nomination letter (9 Feb 2018); Icahn 13D (16 Mar 2018) and settlement 8-K (19 Mar 2018); Starboard
settlement 8-K (23 Apr 2018); Newell Q4 2018 results release (15 Feb 2019).
