## Recovered evidence

February 15: fourth-quarter and full-year release. The plan calls for further portfolio and organizational transformation during the current year. Track sale proceeds, debt retired, continuing sales and remaining central costs independently. A disposal announcement is not complete until cash arrives; a completed sale does not itself prove higher per-share earnings. No later leadership change is included. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Map each stage of the mechanism to a decision maker and an observable deliverable. The relevant actors are Michael Polk, the board, large shareholders, buyers of noncore assets and creditors. Ask which approvals are binding, which commitments are aspirational and which actions depend on financing, a regulator, a customer or a controlling shareholder. A public plan is not a completed transaction; a completed transaction is not proof that every shareholder received the same price. Distinguish operating catalysts from contractual cash-payment dates.

The event being evaluated is the Accelerated Transformation Plan and debt reduction through divestitures. A timetable should allow for implementation expenditure, delay, disclosure lag and the possibility of no resolution. The next evidence should illuminate continuing core sales, normalized margins and operating cash flow. Treat a future year or target date explicitly described as guidance in a pre-cutoff source as a forecast, not a subsequent fact. No actual event after this desk date is admissible merely because it fits the expected calendar.

Construct a base-case route and a failure route. In the latter, identify whether the investor can change the outcome through engagement, must rely on counterparties, or can only change the instrument and size. The adverse mechanism is that divestitures may reduce earnings faster than overhead, while weak core demand makes adjusted EPS misleading. Governance access, board representation and voting agreements can affect both leverage over management and trading freedom; they are not interchangeable. Exact legal restrictions and nomination dates must be checked in source documents rather than invented. This chronology reports only recovered evidence and does not convert a quarter-end observation into an exact trading day.
