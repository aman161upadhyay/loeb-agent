## Recovered evidence

The cutoff share price is recovered above; the forward ratio uses management guidance rather than consensus. Gross debt leverage at year-end was reported as 3.5x; do not call it net leverage. Short-term debt/current maturities were $318.7 million and long-term debt $6.6963 billion. A value bridge must reflect the earnings sold with assets and stranded costs. Historical normalized EPS cannot simply be multiplied by a peer ratio before reconciling discontinued operations. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Newell Brands, build the valuation around the Accelerated Transformation Plan and debt reduction through divestitures. Potential asset support comes from remaining consumer brands and cash earnings after debt, pensions, taxes and stranded overhead. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are continuing core sales, normalized margins and operating cash flow. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
