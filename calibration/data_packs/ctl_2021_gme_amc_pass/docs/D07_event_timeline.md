## Recovered evidence

December 8: GameStop quarterly results. January 25: AMC financing announcement. January 27: dramatic common-share repricing and the cutoff. A bull can imagine forced covering and recapitalization; neither event promises a terminal price. Size and exit liquidity should be stress-tested against trading halts and a sudden disappearance of incremental buyers. No subsequent trading restriction or price reversal is included. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Map each stage of the mechanism to a decision maker and an observable deliverable. The relevant actors are Ryan Cohen and GameStop’s board; Adam Aron, AMC’s board, lenders and equity holders. Ask which approvals are binding, which commitments are aspirational and which actions depend on financing, a regulator, a customer or a controlling shareholder. A public plan is not a completed transaction; a completed transaction is not proof that every shareholder received the same price. Distinguish operating catalysts from contractual cash-payment dates.

The event being evaluated is crowded-short covering combined with retail participation and financing optionality. A timetable should allow for implementation expenditure, delay, disclosure lag and the possibility of no resolution. The next evidence should illuminate unrestricted cash, cash burn, fully diluted market value and short interest. Treat a future year or target date explicitly described as guidance in a pre-cutoff source as a forecast, not a subsequent fact. No actual event after this desk date is admissible merely because it fits the expected calendar.

Construct a base-case route and a failure route. In the latter, identify whether the investor can change the outcome through engagement, must rely on counterparties, or can only change the instrument and size. The adverse mechanism is that a squeeze price need not be supported by a going-concern valuation and financing can dilute common holders. Governance access, board representation and voting agreements can affect both leverage over management and trading freedom; they are not interchangeable. Exact legal restrictions and nomination dates must be checked in source documents rather than invented. This chronology reports only recovered evidence and does not convert a quarter-end observation into an exact trading day.
