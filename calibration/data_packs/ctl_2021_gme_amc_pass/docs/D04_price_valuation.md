## Recovered evidence

Use historical unadjusted prices consistently with historical share counts. The exact cutoff fully diluted share counts and forward valuation estimates were not recovered; no P/E is asserted. A short-covering payoff depends on available float, borrow, options and exit liquidity rather than a contractual takeover price. An equity issuance might reduce insolvency probability while lowering the claim of each existing share on recovered earnings. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For GameStop and AMC Entertainment, build the valuation around crowded-short covering combined with retail participation and financing optionality. Potential asset support comes from retail inventory/cash and cinema operating assets after debt, leases and prospective dilution. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are unrestricted cash, cash burn, fully diluted market value and short interest. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
