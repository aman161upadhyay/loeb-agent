## Recovered evidence

The August 8 presentation reports second-quarter revenue of $475 million versus $924 million in the first quarter. Ecommerce units fell from 19,473 to 9,233, while ecommerce gross profit per unit increased from $1,763 to $3,629. Adjusted EBITDA improved from a $107 million loss to an $86 million loss. Better unit economics therefore coexist with material consolidated losses and sharply smaller throughput. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Vroom, the primary operating checks are gross profit per vehicle after operating costs, unrestricted liquidity and cash burn. The potential asset support is unrestricted liquidity and realizable inventory net of floorplan debt, corporate obligations and losses. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of a deliberate inventory and operating-cost reset preserving liquidity is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
