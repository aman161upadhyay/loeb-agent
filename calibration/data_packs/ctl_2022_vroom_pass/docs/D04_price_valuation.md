## Recovered evidence

The cutoff price and last disclosed share count are recovered above; convertible trading levels and funding advance rates remain unresolved. A price-to-sales multiple is particularly weak when management is deliberately shrinking volume. Compare enterprise value with realizable inventory and normalized gross profit, then subtract financing claims and the cash needed to reach breakeven. Credit or converts may offer a different risk profile, but no recovery value is invented. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Vroom, build the valuation around a deliberate inventory and operating-cost reset preserving liquidity. Potential asset support comes from unrestricted liquidity and realizable inventory net of floorplan debt, corporate obligations and losses. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are gross profit per vehicle after operating costs, unrestricted liquidity and cash burn. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
