Title: Vroom: The strongest contemporaneous counter-case
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1580864/000095017022015535/vrm-ex99_2.htm
Date: 2022-11-04

## Recovered evidence

The skeptic’s strongest objection is a race between restructuring and cash depletion. Lower volumes may improve gross profit per unit while worsening overhead absorption. Inventory markdowns can consume liquidity, funding lines may tighten and consumer credit performance may deteriorate as rates rise. An announced liquidity range is not an enforceable creditor backstop. Common shareholders bear dilution and residual-value risk. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Give the skeptical mechanism equal analytical weight rather than reducing it to a generic volatility warning. The central threat is that higher rates and falling used-car prices may exhaust cash before the operating model becomes self-financing. This can harm the cash earnings, the timing of their receipt or the amount attributable to the selected security. Distinguish temporary mark-to-market stress from a permanent transfer of value to creditors, customers, counterparties or new shareholders. An attractive operating business can coexist with a structurally poor security claim.

The proposed asset support is unrestricted liquidity and realizable inventory net of floorplan debt, corporate obligations and losses; test how accessible it remains under distress. Book value, collateral value, enterprise value and common equity value are different quantities. Restrictions on disposal, taxes, legal costs, financing covenants and ongoing cash needs can make an apparently visible asset a weak liquidity source. No independent appraisal is supplied merely by naming an asset. For Vroom, current recovery amounts and probabilities should remain unasserted where not recovered.

Observable checks should focus on gross profit per vehicle after operating costs, unrestricted liquidity and cash burn, together with funding, governance and execution. Specify which adverse observations would invalidate the mechanism of a deliberate inventory and operating-cost reset preserving liquidity, rather than waiting for a share price to recover. Conversely, specify the operating or contractual evidence that would rebut the bear case. Portfolio sizing must allow for a delayed catalyst, correlated liquidation and imperfect exit liquidity. These are analyst stress considerations, not a claim that any particular adverse scenario occurs. Do not turn an allegation into an adjudicated fact or attribute a skeptic’s reasoning to the investor without a dated source.
