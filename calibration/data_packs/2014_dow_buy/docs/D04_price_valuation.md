A segmented model should distinguish commodity earnings at a defensible cycle point from specialty earnings and joint-venture stakes. The relevant valuation question is what each business earns after allocable costs and transfer prices, not merely a blended sales multiple. The January 21 close is recovered above; a complete independent SOTP is not. North American gas advantages can be capitalized by competitors and do not justify unlimited terminal margins. ## Economic cross-check

Third-quarter sales were $13.7bn, up 1%; adjusted EPS was $0.50 versus reported EPS $0.49. EBITDA was $1.8bn and operating cash flow exceeded $1.4bn. Year-to-date operating cash flow reached $5.6bn. Gross debt fell $2.4bn year to date, and net debt/total capitalization was 34.7%. Equity earnings of $322m included meaningful joint-venture contributions and are not all wholly owned operating earnings. [1]

Separation can destroy integration benefits, add taxes and overhead or weaken purchasing and feedstock optimization. The board can point to improving cash flow, debt reduction and existing self-help as reasons to avoid disruption. Chemical-cycle normalization, new supply and heavy capital projects can absorb expected returns. An aggressive forecast of standalone EBITDA must be distinguished from already achieved operating earnings. [1; risk analysis]

## Underwriting interpretation

Valuation discipline for this desk: purchase price, scenario value, realizable proceeds and total investment profit are four different quantities. A prospective return needs both an entry price and an achievable exit/recovery value, including taxes, fees, dilution and time. When an input is absent, an exact multiple, upside percentage or annualized return is not justified. A low nominal price is not itself a margin of safety; the claim's priority and the business's remaining cash requirements determine the floor.

The base case should not depend on perfect execution and the adverse case should not assume a favorable transaction. Component values need a consistent date, accounting scope and treatment of debt. Avoid double-counting cost savings in both higher earnings and a separate asset value. Similarly, excess cash cannot be added without checking access and associated obligations. A peer multiple is a reference, not a contractual takeout price. No later market quotation or realized outcome is substituted for a missing cutoff input.
