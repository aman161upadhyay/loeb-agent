Short-term borrowing adds $2,360m/$2,139m/$1,467m in 2008/2009/2010 to the long-term row. Preferred dividends explain why common EPS cannot be reconstructed from total net income alone. A standardized GAAP operating-income and tangible-capital-return series remains unreconciled after SEC facts, original annual reports and Macrotrends history search: the displayed operating subtotal explicitly excludes restructuring and other separately classified items and must not be mistaken for EBIT. Integration and joint-venture accounting materially affect comparability.

Third-quarter sales were $13.7bn, up 1%; adjusted EPS was $0.50 versus reported EPS $0.49. EBITDA was $1.8bn and operating cash flow exceeded $1.4bn. Year-to-date operating cash flow reached $5.6bn. Gross debt fell $2.4bn year to date, and net debt/total capitalization was 34.7%. Equity earnings of $322m included meaningful joint-venture contributions and are not all wholly owned operating earnings. [1]

## Economic cross-check

The October release reports Performance Plastics EBITDA up 32%, with gains also in coatings/infrastructure and electronic/functional materials. Price increased 3% and volume fell 2%, or 1% excluding divestitures. Management emphasizes self-help and integration while targeting non-core disposals. Strong cash flow is tangible counterevidence to a claim that the entire corporate model lacks merit, even if a greater improvement is possible. [1]

A segmented model should distinguish commodity earnings at a defensible cycle point from specialty earnings and joint-venture stakes. The relevant valuation question is what each business earns after allocable costs and transfer prices, not merely a blended sales multiple. No January 21 close or complete independent SOTP is recovered. North American gas advantages can be capitalized by competitors and do not justify unlimited terminal margins. [1; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
