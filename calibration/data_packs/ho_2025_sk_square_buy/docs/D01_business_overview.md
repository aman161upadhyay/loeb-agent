Title: SK Square: Business economics and the investable event
Source: https://www.sksquare.com/eng/news/newsMediaDetail.do?idx=262
Date: 2025-06-30

## Recovered evidence

SK Square is an investment holding company with a large SK hynix interest and other ICT businesses. The relevant analytical model is asset value per share rather than consolidated sales growth alone. Management’s May release describes operating improvement, noncore monetization and prospective semiconductor/AI investment. Capital return and reinvestment therefore compete for the same pool of cash. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

For SK Square, distinguish the operating asset from the security through which the asset is owned. The investable mechanism is a liquid-asset holding-company discount and announced capital return. The existence of that mechanism does not establish a numerical expected return. Identify which customer payment reaches the enterprise, what direct expense is required to earn it, what central costs remain and which capital commitments must be funded before common shareholders receive cash. The candidate operating indicators are discount to NAV, cash returned, share cancellation and look-through earnings. A rising revenue line without the associated cash and capital bridge is insufficient evidence of improving business quality.

The proposed asset support is marked portfolio assets and cash less holding-company liabilities, taxes and reinvestment leakage. This is a description of the potential source of recovery, not an appraisal or guaranteed minimum value. The claim hierarchy can matter as much as operating performance. Customer demand, supplier obligations, debt service and working capital must be considered together. An event-driven interpretation should explain why the mechanism changes cash flows or the market’s recognition of them, rather than relying on a favorable narrative alone.

The relevant actors are Han Myung-Jin, SK Square’s board, controlling stakeholders and portfolio companies. Establish what each can approve, delay or veto before assigning value to a management change, operating reset or possible transaction. Any operating improvement should be measured against a stated baseline and the resources needed to obtain it. Unrecovered financial inputs remain missing; they are not assigned a zero value. An attractive franchise can still be an unattractive common security if senior claims consume its value, while a difficult operating situation can have a better expression elsewhere in the capital structure.
