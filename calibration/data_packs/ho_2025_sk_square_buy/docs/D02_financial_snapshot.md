## Recovered evidence

First-quarter consolidated revenue was KRW 402.8 billion, operating income KRW 1,652.3 billion and net income KRW 1,612.3 billion. Large equity-method gains from SK hynix explain why operating income exceeds consolidated revenue; it is not a conventional operating margin. Quarter-end cash equivalents were KRW 431.6 billion. Management describes maintaining a zero-debt strategy while seeking investment resources. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For SK Square, the primary operating checks are discount to NAV, cash returned, share cancellation and look-through earnings. The potential asset support is marked portfolio assets and cash less holding-company liabilities, taxes and reinvestment leakage. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of a liquid-asset holding-company discount and announced capital return is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
