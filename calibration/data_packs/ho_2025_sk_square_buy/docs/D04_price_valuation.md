## Recovered evidence

The issuer reports a Q1 NAV discount of 62.8%, versus 65.7% at the prior year-end. Its text cites KRW103,500 for May 13, but Yahoo's dated history assigns that close to May 14 and KRW99,900 to May 13; the June 30 close above does not depend on that discrepancy. No cutoff look-through P/E was recovered. Compute the discount with consistent asset and holding-company prices; taxes, overhead, liabilities and illiquid investments can justify some discount. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For SK Square, build the valuation around a liquid-asset holding-company discount and announced capital return. Potential asset support comes from marked portfolio assets and cash less holding-company liabilities, taxes and reinvestment leakage. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are discount to NAV, cash returned, share cancellation and look-through earnings. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
