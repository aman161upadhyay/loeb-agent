## Economic cross-check

Quarterly operating cash flow was $124.142 million versus $209.342 million; discretionary cash flow, a different measure, was $220.254 million. Management says drilling has been active and successful and describes a substantial prospect inventory. It has begun marketing certain offshore and Gulf Coast properties and plans a further phase involving the Permian, Texas panhandle and western Canada. These are processes, not closed transactions. A reserve-based sum-of-parts needs separate estimates for oil, gas and geography, less debt, taxes and future development capital. D04 now supplies the market price and balance-sheet bridge; independently appraised reserve NAV and a binding whole-company offer remain different inputs. Management's non-core sales could provide price discovery but do not prove all reserves can be sold at the same multiple. [1; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. D04's enterprise value uses the latest reported September balance sheet, not an asserted January cash balance. Ratios retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
