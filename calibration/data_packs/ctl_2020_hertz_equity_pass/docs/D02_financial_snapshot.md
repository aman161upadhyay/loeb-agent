## Recovered evidence

No complete cutoff creditor waterfall, appraised vehicle value or claim-by-claim recovery model was recovered. The financing distinction is essential: vehicle ABS has collateral and structural protections that existing corporate common does not. A rise in used-car values can benefit collateralized creditors without reaching the residual. Cash at a subsidiary is not automatically available to equity at the parent. The May 22 release says approximately $1 billion of cash is available to support continuing operations; that amount is operating liquidity, not an equity distribution. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Hertz Global Holdings — pre-reorganization common equity, the primary operating checks are cash rental demand, used-vehicle realizations and the creditor waterfall. The potential asset support is residual enterprise value only after vehicle financing, corporate debt, leases and administrative claims. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of a bankrupt-equity rally reflecting reopening and residual-option speculation is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
