## Recovered evidence

The June 8 close and the last disclosed basic share count are recovered above; contingent dilution is not assumed zero. The appropriate valuation is residual recovery after senior claims and administrative expense. Recovery optionality can rise dramatically from a small base while the probability-weighted equity value remains low. A healthy franchise does not establish a positive residual without the claims bridge. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Hertz Global Holdings — pre-reorganization common equity, build the valuation around a bankrupt-equity rally reflecting reopening and residual-option speculation. Potential asset support comes from residual enterprise value only after vehicle financing, corporate debt, leases and administrative claims. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are cash rental demand, used-vehicle realizations and the creditor waterfall. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
