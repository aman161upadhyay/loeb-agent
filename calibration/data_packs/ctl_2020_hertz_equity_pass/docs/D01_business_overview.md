Title: Hertz Global Holdings — pre-reorganization common equity: Business economics and the investable event
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/47129/000110465920065674/tm2020858d1_ex99-1.htm
Date: 2020-06-08

## Recovered evidence

Hertz operates rental-car brands supported by a financed vehicle fleet. A genuine equity bull argues that recovering travel and used-vehicle realizations could lift enterprise value enough to preserve residual value. Bankruptcy can provide a process for liabilities and leases to reset. The traded common, however, is a residual option and must be distinguished from vehicle-backed bonds, corporate debt or securities of a future reorganized issuer. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

For Hertz Global Holdings — pre-reorganization common equity, distinguish the operating asset from the security through which the asset is owned. The investable mechanism is a bankrupt-equity rally reflecting reopening and residual-option speculation. The existence of that mechanism does not establish a numerical expected return. Identify which customer payment reaches the enterprise, what direct expense is required to earn it, what central costs remain and which capital commitments must be funded before common shareholders receive cash. The candidate operating indicators are cash rental demand, used-vehicle realizations and the creditor waterfall. A rising revenue line without the associated cash and capital bridge is insufficient evidence of improving business quality.

The proposed asset support is residual enterprise value only after vehicle financing, corporate debt, leases and administrative claims. This is a description of the potential source of recovery, not an appraisal or guaranteed minimum value. The claim hierarchy can matter as much as operating performance. Customer demand, supplier obligations, debt service and working capital must be considered together. An event-driven interpretation should explain why the mechanism changes cash flows or the market’s recognition of them, rather than relying on a favorable narrative alone.

The relevant actors are Hertz management, the bankruptcy court, vehicle financiers, corporate creditors and common holders. Establish what each can approve, delay or veto before assigning value to a management change, operating reset or possible transaction. Any operating improvement should be measured against a stated baseline and the resources needed to obtain it. Unrecovered financial inputs remain missing; they are not assigned a zero value. An attractive franchise can still be an unattractive common security if senior claims consume its value, while a difficult operating situation can have a better expression elsewhere in the capital structure.
