## Recovered evidence

The strongest bear case is absolute priority: enterprise improvements may accrue entirely to creditors. Volatility and turnover can create an illusion of liquidity even when the exit bid is fragile. Administrative expenses, fleet liquidation losses and financing restrictions can erode the option. No later equity issuance proposal, court ruling or post-reorganization trading value is part of this packet. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Give the skeptical mechanism equal analytical weight rather than reducing it to a generic volatility warning. The central threat is that an improving rental franchise may still be worth less than the claims senior to existing common equity. This can harm the cash earnings, the timing of their receipt or the amount attributable to the selected security. Distinguish temporary mark-to-market stress from a permanent transfer of value to creditors, customers, counterparties or new shareholders. An attractive operating business can coexist with a structurally poor security claim.

The proposed asset support is residual enterprise value only after vehicle financing, corporate debt, leases and administrative claims; test how accessible it remains under distress. Book value, collateral value, enterprise value and common equity value are different quantities. Restrictions on disposal, taxes, legal costs, financing covenants and ongoing cash needs can make an apparently visible asset a weak liquidity source. No independent appraisal is supplied merely by naming an asset. For Hertz Global Holdings — pre-reorganization common equity, current recovery amounts and probabilities should remain unasserted where not recovered.

Observable checks should focus on cash rental demand, used-vehicle realizations and the creditor waterfall, together with funding, governance and execution. Specify which adverse observations would invalidate the mechanism of a bankrupt-equity rally reflecting reopening and residual-option speculation, rather than waiting for a share price to recover. Conversely, specify the operating or contractual evidence that would rebut the bear case. Portfolio sizing must allow for a delayed catalyst, correlated liquidation and imperfect exit liquidity. These are analyst stress considerations, not a claim that any particular adverse scenario occurs. Do not turn an allegation into an adjudicated fact or attribute a skeptic’s reasoning to the investor without a dated source.
