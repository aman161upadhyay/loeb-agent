## Recovered evidence

Rental companies combine travel demand, utilization, vehicle purchase and disposal economics, and financing structure. Comparing their equity revenue multiples ignores differences in fleet leverage and residual values. ABS recovery analysis requires separate collateral, advance rates and waterfalls. A distressed common position and a senior vehicle-backed bond can express opposite conclusions about the same rental business without logical inconsistency. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Choose comparable economics before comparing trading multiples. Revenue can be gross or net, earnings can be adjusted or reported, and capital requirements can sit on different balance sheets. A useful peer comparison reconciles accounting, business mix, geography, customer concentration and financing before assigning a discount or premium. A stock does not become inexpensive merely because a less comparable company trades at a higher multiple. Where peer data have not been recovered, the numerical comparison remains unavailable.

For Hertz Global Holdings — pre-reorganization common equity, the specific mechanism is a bankrupt-equity rally reflecting reopening and residual-option speculation, and the relevant outcome measures are cash rental demand, used-vehicle realizations and the creditor waterfall. Ask whether peers have already demonstrated the operating outcome being proposed here, whether their managements control the same levers and whether their claim hierarchy is comparable. Industry growth does not guarantee supplier returns when customers retain bargaining power, capacity expands or required investment absorbs the incremental profit. Conversely, a slow-growing industry can support attractive returns where scarce assets, recurring relationships or forced transactions create pricing power.

Separate common industry risk from issuer-specific repair. The key bear mechanism is that an improving rental franchise may still be worth less than the claims senior to existing common equity. A relative-value case must explain why this issuer can improve rather than simply assuming that the entire industry rerates. Identify what evidence would distinguish a cyclical rebound from structural change. This packet supplies no unstated peer target margins, share-price history or consensus forecasts. Any proposed benchmark is an underwriting question, not an additional fact or an investor quotation.
