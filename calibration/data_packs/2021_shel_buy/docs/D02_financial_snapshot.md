EBIT proxy=pre-tax income plus gross interest expense, including associates/investment effects; it is not a separately reported operating-profit subtotal. DD&A includes impairments, especially in 2020. FCF=CFO minus capital expenditure, excluding disposal proceeds. ROTC divides that EBIT proxy by net PP&E plus non-cash operating working capital, excluding goodwill. Debt includes leases after IFRS 16 in 2019. The 2020 dividend is the amount declared for the year's quarters, not cash paid for the previous fourth quarter. Amounts are reported figures for the periods, retrieved from Shell's 2020 Form 20-F and SEC historical filings.

Second-quarter adjusted earnings were $5.534 billion versus $638 million a year earlier. Income attributable to shareholders was $3.428 billion, with identified impairments and derivative valuation effects. Operating cash flow was $12.617 billion and reported FCF $9.671 billion. Cash capex was $4.383 billion. Net debt was $65.735 billion and gearing 27.7%, down from 29.9% in the previous quarter. Production available for sale was 3.254 million boe/day. Strong cash generation reflects the commodity rebound; it must not be extrapolated as a flat perpetual return. [S1]

Accounting treatment. The figures above retain their original period, currency and definition. Reported and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable, and segment profit is not automatically consolidated distributable cash. Reconcile recurring operating performance with financing costs, taxes, exceptional charges, working capital and necessary reinvestment. An acquisition, divestiture or change in reporting perimeter can change growth and share counts without representing organic improvement. Do not sum overlapping segment or geographic disclosures.

Financial underwriting should use a bridge, not a headline ratio. Start from recurring revenue and operating profit, charge maintenance investment and financing obligations, and test cash conversion under weaker demand or higher costs. A non-cash charge may not consume cash this quarter but can reveal that prior capital allocation failed. A working-capital release may be real cash without being repeatable earnings. Debt maturities, restricted cash, leases and decommissioning require separate modelling beyond the annual balances. D04 distinguishes issued ordinary shares, diluted weighted-average shares and the two-for-one ADR basis.

Analyst cross-check: For any apparent earnings improvement, test three reconciliations: operating income to operating cash flow, operating cash flow to free cash flow after capital spending, and enterprise earnings to the actual security's claim after interest, preferred distributions and dilution. Missing inputs prevent a precise return calculation.
