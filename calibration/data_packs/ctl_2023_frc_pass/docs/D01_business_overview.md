Title: First Republic Bank: Business economics and the investable event
Source: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23020.html ; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230113005044/en/First-Republic-Reports-2022-Results
Date: 2023-03-16

## Recovered evidence

First Republic serves affluent households with relationship banking, residential lending and wealth services. Its franchise can have value to an acquirer or a recapitalized independent bank if customer relationships survive. On March 16 regulators acknowledged eleven banks’ $30 billion deposit placement. A plausible bull case is a liquidity bridge that gives a good customer franchise time to stabilize rather than sell assets under pressure. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

For First Republic Bank, distinguish the operating asset from the security through which the asset is owned. The investable mechanism is a $30 billion deposit placement by eleven banks to stabilize funding. The existence of that mechanism does not establish a numerical expected return. Identify which customer payment reaches the enterprise, what direct expense is required to earn it, what central costs remain and which capital commitments must be funded before common shareholders receive cash. The candidate operating indicators are deposit retention, funding cost, liquidity and tangible common equity under fair-value stress. A rising revenue line without the associated cash and capital bridge is insufficient evidence of improving business quality.

The proposed asset support is loan and securities realizations after deposits, secured advances, preferred claims and liquidity costs. This is a description of the potential source of recovery, not an appraisal or guaranteed minimum value. The claim hierarchy can matter as much as operating performance. Customer demand, supplier obligations, debt service and working capital must be considered together. An event-driven interpretation should explain why the mechanism changes cash flows or the market’s recognition of them, rather than relying on a favorable narrative alone.

The relevant actors are First Republic management, the eleven depositing banks, Treasury, Federal Reserve, FDIC and OCC. Establish what each can approve, delay or veto before assigning value to a management change, operating reset or possible transaction. Any operating improvement should be measured against a stated baseline and the resources needed to obtain it. Unrecovered financial inputs remain missing; they are not assigned a zero value. An attractive franchise can still be an unattractive common security if senior claims consume its value, while a difficult operating situation can have a better expression elsewhere in the capital structure.
