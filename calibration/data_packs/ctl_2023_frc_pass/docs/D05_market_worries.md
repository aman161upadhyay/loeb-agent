## Recovered evidence

The strongest bear case is that emergency liquidity slows a run without repairing the economics of expensive funding against low-yield assets. A good wealth franchise does not necessarily cover realized balance-sheet losses. Depositors, secured creditors and preferred holders stand ahead of common. Regulatory resolution, a forced asset sale or a capital raise can transfer franchise value away from existing equity even without high credit defaults. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Give the skeptical mechanism equal analytical weight rather than reducing it to a generic volatility warning. The central threat is that liquidity assistance protects depositors but can leave common equity exposed to asset marks and expensive replacement funding. This can harm the cash earnings, the timing of their receipt or the amount attributable to the selected security. Distinguish temporary mark-to-market stress from a permanent transfer of value to creditors, customers, counterparties or new shareholders. An attractive operating business can coexist with a structurally poor security claim.

The proposed asset support is loan and securities realizations after deposits, secured advances, preferred claims and liquidity costs; test how accessible it remains under distress. Book value, collateral value, enterprise value and common equity value are different quantities. Restrictions on disposal, taxes, legal costs, financing covenants and ongoing cash needs can make an apparently visible asset a weak liquidity source. No independent appraisal is supplied merely by naming an asset. For First Republic Bank, current recovery amounts and probabilities should remain unasserted where not recovered.

Observable checks should focus on deposit retention, funding cost, liquidity and tangible common equity under fair-value stress, together with funding, governance and execution. Specify which adverse observations would invalidate the mechanism of a $30 billion deposit placement by eleven banks to stabilize funding, rather than waiting for a share price to recover. Conversely, specify the operating or contractual evidence that would rebut the bear case. Portfolio sizing must allow for a delayed catalyst, correlated liquidation and imperfect exit liquidity. These are analyst stress considerations, not a claim that any particular adverse scenario occurs. Do not turn an allegation into an adjudicated fact or attribute a skeptic’s reasoning to the investor without a dated source.
