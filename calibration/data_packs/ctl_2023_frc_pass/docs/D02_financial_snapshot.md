## Recovered evidence

The January 13 results reported year-end deposits of $176.4 billion, up 12.9%, and nonperforming assets at five basis points of total assets. These measures describe the prior quarter, not current deposit retention. Low borrower defaults do not eliminate duration losses or a funding mismatch. D03 adds disclosed emergency borrowing rates; the exact March 16 deposit mix and current tangible-equity adjustment remain unresolved. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For First Republic Bank, the primary operating checks are deposit retention, funding cost, liquidity and tangible common equity under fair-value stress. The potential asset support is loan and securities realizations after deposits, secured advances, preferred claims and liquidity costs. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of a $30 billion deposit placement by eleven banks to stabilize funding is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
