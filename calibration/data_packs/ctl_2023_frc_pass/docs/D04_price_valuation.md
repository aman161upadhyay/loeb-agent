## Recovered evidence

The cutoff common-share close is recovered above; a reconciled cutoff fair-value tangible book figure remains unresolved. A price-to-book ratio cannot be interpreted without marking the duration-sensitive assets and replacement funding. Value the operating franchise only after a solvency bridge. A loan held to maturity may pay par, but equity cannot assume the bank has unlimited time to collect it when deposits are callable. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For First Republic Bank, build the valuation around a $30 billion deposit placement by eleven banks to stabilize funding. Potential asset support comes from loan and securities realizations after deposits, secured advances, preferred claims and liquidity costs. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are deposit retention, funding cost, liquidity and tangible common equity under fair-value stress. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
