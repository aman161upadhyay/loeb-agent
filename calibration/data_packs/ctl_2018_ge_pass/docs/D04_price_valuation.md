## Recovered evidence

The cutoff price is recovered above, but a fully liability-adjusted sum-of-parts remains unresolved. The correct valuation is a bridge from segment earnings to realizable proceeds, less taxes, debt, pensions, financial commitments and stranded costs. A $23 billion goodwill write-off does not create cash or cancel liabilities. A fresh CEO can change the opportunity set but is not a numerical valuation floor. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For General Electric, build the valuation around Larry Culp’s appointment and a portfolio/balance-sheet reset. Potential asset support comes from aviation and healthcare franchises net of industrial debt, financial obligations and restructuring costs. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are industrial free cash flow and segment earnings after separation costs. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
