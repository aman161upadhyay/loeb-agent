## Recovered evidence

Third Point’s Q2 13F, disclosed August 10, reports roughly $14.352 billion of securities. Baxter, NXP, United Technologies and DowDuPont are major lines. Existing industrial/conglomerate exposure matters when comparing a new sum-of-parts situation. Reportable security value does not establish firm NAV, and no cutoff cash or gross/net estimate is invented. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Separate the investment manager, the trading fund and any listed feeder. Firm-wide assets, Offshore net assets, reported 13F security value and a feeder’s capitalization measure different things. A reported securities table excludes much credit, foreign ordinary shares, shorts, swaps and private assets. It therefore cannot establish current cash, gross exposure, net exposure or the amount of liquidity available for a new idea. Historical exposure tables remain dated rather than rolled forward mechanically.

The opportunity under review is Larry Culp’s appointment and a portfolio/balance-sheet reset. Its business and financing sensitivities must be compared with the existing book, not judged in isolation. The relevant operating measures are industrial free cash flow and segment earnings after separation costs. A small position in a binary or illiquid security can require more risk capital than its market value suggests. Short exposure, collateral requirements and private commitments can consume liquidity even where headline net equity exposure appears modest. Common industry labels can conceal a shared factor or refinancing sensitivity.

The relevant engagement actors are Larry Culp, GE’s board, creditors and business-unit management. Access to management, existing agreements, information barriers and standstills can constrain a position independently of the valuation. Do not infer authority from another shareholder’s board seat or treat a disclosed common holding as the investor’s entire economic exposure. The figures supplied above describe only what was publicly recovered by the cutoff. No subsequent trade, manager explanation or result is part of this context. Where cash, exposure, nomination windows or a precise firm AUM were not recovered, those remain open inputs rather than silently assumed capacity.
