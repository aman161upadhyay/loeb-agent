# D09 — Governance and legacy-liability facts on the record (as of 2018-10-01)

## Other activists already at the table
- Trian Fund Management (Nelson Peltz, Ed Garden) disclosed a ~$2.5bn GE stake in October 2015 with a public white paper
  ("Transformation Underway… But Nobody Cares") and received a board seat for Ed Garden on 9 October 2017 after the stock's
  2017 collapse; Trian's asks (cost cuts, buybacks, portfolio simplification) were adopted in the November 2017 and June 2018
  restructuring announcements (dividend cut in half November 2017; plan to spin Healthcare and exit Baker Hughes, 26 June 2018).
- The board was cut from 18 to 12 directors in April 2018; three new directors joined with Garden on the finance and
  capital-allocation committee. The CEO change to H. Lawrence Culp Jr. on 1 October 2018 (announced that morning) was decided
  by the reconstituted board.

## Legacy liabilities that cannot be bounded from public data
- On 16 January 2018 GE disclosed a $6.2bn after-tax charge for the GE Capital run-off insurance portfolio (long-term-care
  reinsurance written 2004-2006 by North American Life & Health) and a requirement to contribute ~$15bn of statutory capital
  to the insurance subsidiaries over seven years ($3.5bn in Q1 2018); the Kansas Insurance Department reviews the reserves
  annually and the company said future charges could not be excluded.
- The SEC opened an investigation into the insurance reserve accounting and revenue recognition on long-term service
  agreements (disclosed 24 January 2018), later widened (October 2018) to the $22bn Power goodwill impairment announced with
  the Culp appointment. The US Department of Justice was also reviewing the WMC subprime mortgage unit (no resolution as of this date).
- GE Capital's remaining assets (~$120bn) carried $47bn of debt guaranteed by the parent; Moody's and S&P had GE on review for
  downgrade as of 1 October 2018.
- GE Power's 2018 orders fell 26% year-on-year with the heavy-duty gas turbine market at roughly half its 2016 size; the
  segment's $23bn goodwill was written off on 1 October 2018 (announced with the CEO change).

Sources: GE 8-K and press releases 16 Jan 2018, 24 Jan 2018, 26 Jun 2018, 1 Oct 2018; Trian 13D and white paper (Oct 2015); GE
proxy statement April 2018; Kansas Insurance Department filings; press coverage (WSJ, Reuters) on the dates stated.
