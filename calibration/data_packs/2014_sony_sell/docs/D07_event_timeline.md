Date: 2014-10-20

The August board response rejected the minority entertainment listing after review. The September mobile announcement resets expectations and the operating plan. There is no accepted entertainment IPO timetable in the file. The next decision should evaluate whether current self-help and the residual price justify remaining capital. This snapshot excludes the next-day fund letter and any description of the fund's completed disposition. [1,2]

## Economic cross-check

The mobile business's new midrange plan reflects a significantly changed competitive environment and lower expected cash flows. Management shifts from substantial sales growth toward lower risk, less volatility and more stable profits, including geographic and product focus. The forecast exchange assumptions remain ¥103/$ and ¥137/€. The company says no other factors were incorporated into this forecast revision; it does not claim all remaining business risks have disappeared. [2]

The desk owns a position originally underwritten on hidden entertainment value, electronics focus and a possible structural catalyst. Management has acted on some operational priorities but the central ownership proposal is not proceeding. Current common/derivative split, firm AUM, cash and exact NAV weight were not recovered. The decision must be about remaining expected return, not vindication of the original public proposal. [1,2; internal position state]

## Underwriting interpretation

Process map for the desk: distinguish an announced intention, a signed agreement, formal approval, closing and receipt of cash. The actor with legal authority may differ from the person explaining the economic case. A minority equity holder, a secured lender and a government creditor have different remedies. Any governance route must respect actual voting rights, nomination rules, consent requirements and confidentiality obligations; a large dollar position alone does not establish control.

The next checkpoint should produce observable evidence, such as a filing, vote, hearing, financing condition or comparable operating result. A timetable expressed in quarters is an analytical horizon, not a verified promise that a transaction closes then. The document stops at its stated information cutoff. Prospective dates mentioned above remain prospective and no later resolution is assumed. If an exact deadline was not recovered, the pack does not invent one. The relevant question is what new information the process can resolve and how much capital or time can be consumed while it remains unresolved.
