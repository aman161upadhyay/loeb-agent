Peer caps use annual share counts and are approximate. FY denominators and accounting scopes differ. The apparent book discount is not an after-tax entertainment valuation, and balance-sheet cash is not all excess cash.

Any remaining sum-of-parts discount must be recalculated after weaker mobile forecasts and the board's rejection of a separate entertainment listing. A historical activist target is not a current realizable price. The precise October 20 close, updated fund basis and fully reconciled segment valuation were not recovered. A profitable mark relative to original cost, if present, does not by itself determine expected return from this date. [1,2; analyst framework]

## Economic cross-check

The September forecast retains annual revenue of ¥7,800bn but revises expected operating income from ¥140bn profit to ¥40bn loss. Expected net loss attributable to shareholders rises from ¥50bn to ¥230bn. The change reflects approximately ¥180bn impairment of the entire mobile-communications goodwill balance. Last fiscal-year revenue was ¥7,767.3bn and net loss ¥128.4bn. An impairment is noncash but signals lower expected future cash flows. [2]

The strongest case for retaining exposure is that writing off goodwill may clear unrealistic expectations while gaming, sensors and entertainment retain value. A rejected ownership restructuring does not eliminate all self-help. Conversely, repeated electronics disappointment can absorb the asset cushion and prolong cash requirements. The original governance path cannot be treated as intact when the board has explicitly chosen another structure. [1,2; risk analysis]

## Underwriting interpretation

Valuation discipline for this desk: purchase price, scenario value, realizable proceeds and total investment profit are four different quantities. A prospective return needs both an entry price and an achievable exit/recovery value, including taxes, fees, dilution and time. When an input is absent, an exact multiple, upside percentage or annualized return is not justified. A low nominal price is not itself a margin of safety; the claim's priority and the business's remaining cash requirements determine the floor.

The base case should not depend on perfect execution and the adverse case should not assume a favorable transaction. Component values need a consistent date, accounting scope and treatment of debt. Avoid double-counting cost savings in both higher earnings and a separate asset value. Similarly, excess cash cannot be added without checking access and associated obligations. A peer multiple is a reference, not a contractual takeout price. No later market quotation or realized outcome is substituted for a missing cutoff input.
