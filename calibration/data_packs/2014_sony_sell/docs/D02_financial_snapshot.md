March 2014 goodwill and other intangibles were approximately ¥691.8bn and ¥675.7bn, giving calculated tangible equity of ¥890.7bn. SEC companyfacts records operating cash flow of ¥664.1bn for March 2014, making OCF less the summary's capex ¥499.5bn; this is consolidated, not industrial distributable FCF. A uniform restated five-year total-borrowing, FCF and operating tangible-capital-return bridge remains unreconciled after the SEC 20-F, Sony annual-report archive and Macrotrends history search. Noncurrent debt must not be mistaken for all borrowing.

The September forecast retains annual revenue of ¥7,800bn but revises expected operating income from ¥140bn profit to ¥40bn loss. Expected net loss attributable to shareholders rises from ¥50bn to ¥230bn. The change reflects approximately ¥180bn impairment of the entire mobile-communications goodwill balance. Last fiscal-year revenue was ¥7,767.3bn and net loss ¥128.4bn. An impairment is noncash but signals lower expected future cash flows. [2]

## Economic cross-check

The mobile business's new midrange plan reflects a significantly changed competitive environment and lower expected cash flows. Management shifts from substantial sales growth toward lower risk, less volatility and more stable profits, including geographic and product focus. The forecast exchange assumptions remain ¥103/$ and ¥137/€. The company says no other factors were incorporated into this forecast revision; it does not claim all remaining business risks have disappeared. [2]

Any remaining sum-of-parts discount must be recalculated after weaker mobile forecasts and the board's rejection of a separate entertainment listing. A historical activist target is not a current realizable price. The precise October 20 close, updated fund basis and fully reconciled segment valuation were not recovered. A profitable mark relative to original cost, if present, does not by itself determine expected return from this date. [1,2; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
