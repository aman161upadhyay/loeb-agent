Existing owner state: a substantial position was held at the start of the year against the public short thesis. The desk has already passed the immediate initial-rebuttal calendar and now evaluates remaining upside versus capital at risk. Current exact holding, firm cash, NAV weight and other consumer exposures were not recovered. No requirement to defend an ideological side of the public argument is assumed.

## Economic cross-check

Herbalife remains an international nutritional-products distributor operating through independent sellers. The existing position was underwritten as a profitable business at a controversy-driven discount, not a commitment to hold until all legal debate is resolved. A public short challenge and opposing investors create substantial technical as well as operating volatility. End-user demand and compensation design remain more important to enduring value than the personalities in the dispute. [1; internal thesis history]

The position was established around the public controversy; the management rebuttal and latest earnings release are now available. No final regulatory resolution or specified legal deadline is in the evidence. The next event could be another investor statement as easily as a fundamental development. This pack stops March 1 and does not report a completed fund disposition or later quarter-end filing. [1; internal history]

## Underwriting interpretation

Portfolio accounting for this desk: use current market exposure, not the issuer ownership percentage, to calculate capital at risk. Gross and net can conceal concentrated sector, currency, liquidity or event dependencies. A credit position and an equity position may respond to the same business shock despite occupying different strategy buckets. Derivative economic exposure, voting shares, collateral and counterparty obligations should be tracked separately. An absent exposure report is not evidence that no such risk exists.

The available record does not justify inventing current cash, top holdings or risk limits. Size must remain conditional where those inputs are missing. For an existing holding, separate the original thesis from new facts and sunk cost; for a fresh idea, do not assume ownership merely because the company has appeared in historical commentary. The fund's ability to wait for value depends on portfolio liquidity and obligations as well as the company's assets. No eventual trade, public campaign choice or realized return is encoded in the mandate above.
