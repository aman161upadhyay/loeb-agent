Fourth-quarter net sales were approximately $1.1bn, up 20%, and volume points increased 18%. Net income was $117.8m and diluted EPS $1.05, versus $105.4m and $0.86 a year earlier. Those results demonstrate continued reported growth through the quarter. They do not prove that every legal/business-model criticism is false or that future regulatory risk should be discounted to zero. [1]

## Economic cross-check

The February 19 release reports substantial sales and volume growth and supplies updated outlook information. This is a new operating checkpoint after the initial short attack, not a final ruling on the distributor model. Strong results can support the original valuation case while also changing the expected reward after a rebound. D04 supplies the cutoff close; a consistently measured consensus-surprise series is not supplied as though it were reported earnings. [1]

D04 supplies the current market close, not the fund's execution price, average cost or realised proceeds. The original 10–12x/$55–68 investor framework was a scenario rather than a permanent price anchor. The desk should recompute forward return using current expected earnings and controversy risk, not treat an initial upside percentage as unchanged after a technical repricing. [2; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
