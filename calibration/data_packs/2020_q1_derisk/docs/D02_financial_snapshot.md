This five-year sequence shows that low spreads and volatility at the end of 2019 were not sufficient evidence of ample financing capacity in March.

At March 16 the two-year Treasury yield was 0.36% and the ten-year 0.73%, a 37bp slope. The 838bp high-yield OAS had widened by 478bp from December 31, while the S&P 500 had fallen 26.1%. Those are index-level stress measures, not a forecast of this portfolio's loss or its private-credit recovery values. Cash-flow leverage must be recomputed issuer by issuer as revenues fall; no aggregate company FCF or book-value multiple can safely stand in for that work.

On March 15 the Federal Reserve lowered its target range to 0–0.25%. It announced at least $500 billion additional Treasury holdings and at least $200 billion agency mortgage-backed securities purchases, alongside funding and liquidity measures. These are policy commitments, not realized cash flows of portfolio companies. Exact March 16 fund cash, margin requirements and security-level bid/ask spreads are not recovered. No quarter-end fund return or subsequent credit deployment is admitted. The relevant solvency question is the fund's ability to meet obligations and preserve capital under correlated markdowns. [S1]

Accounting treatment. The figures above retain their original period, currency and definition. Reported and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable, and segment profit is not automatically consolidated distributable cash. Reconcile recurring operating performance with financing costs, taxes, exceptional charges, working capital and necessary reinvestment. An acquisition, divestiture or change in reporting perimeter can change growth and share counts without representing organic improvement. Do not sum overlapping segment or geographic disclosures.

Portfolio underwriting should use a liquidity bridge, not an aggregate corporate earnings ratio. Separate gross exposure from net exposure and traded marks from realizable cash. Company-level maintenance investment and financing obligations still affect individual holdings, but fund NAV must also fund margin, redemptions and settlement. The February exposure table is the latest published snapshot here, not a reconstruction of March 16 positions. Do not substitute 13F gross market value for NAV, or valuation hierarchy for cash immediately available to meet obligations.

Analyst cross-check: For any apparent earnings improvement, test three reconciliations: operating income to operating cash flow, operating cash flow to free cash flow after capital spending, and enterprise earnings to the actual security's claim after interest, preferred distributions and dilution. Missing inputs prevent a precise return calculation.
