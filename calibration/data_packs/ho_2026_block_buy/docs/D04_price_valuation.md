The letter reports Q4 repurchases of 11.9 million Class A shares for $790 million. That implies approximately $66.39 average repurchase expenditure per share, not the March 31 close or investor entry price. Cutoff sell-side consensus EPS remains unreconciled; the table supplies a separately labeled guidance multiple and capital bridge. Gross profit must fund credit losses, customer acquisition, technology, compliance and central costs before it becomes equity cash flow. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Block, build the valuation around monetization of a scaled consumer network and an organizational cost reset. Potential asset support comes from payment and financial-services gross profit after credit losses, costs, capital needs and dilution. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are gross profit, transacting actives, lending losses and operating leverage. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
