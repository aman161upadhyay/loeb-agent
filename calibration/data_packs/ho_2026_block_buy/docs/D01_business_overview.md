## Recovered evidence

Block combines Square merchant commerce and Cash App consumer financial services, alongside Afterpay and other activities. The opportunity is deeper monetization of installed distribution rather than merely adding users. Lending, payments and workflow products have distinct economics and regulatory requirements. A bull can see operating leverage from a larger financial-services mix, but revenue alone obscures the underlying gross-profit contribution. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

For Block, distinguish the operating asset from the security through which the asset is owned. The investable mechanism is monetization of a scaled consumer network and an organizational cost reset. The existence of that mechanism does not establish a numerical expected return. Identify which customer payment reaches the enterprise, what direct expense is required to earn it, what central costs remain and which capital commitments must be funded before common shareholders receive cash. The candidate operating indicators are gross profit, transacting actives, lending losses and operating leverage. A rising revenue line without the associated cash and capital bridge is insufficient evidence of improving business quality.

The proposed asset support is payment and financial-services gross profit after credit losses, costs, capital needs and dilution. This is a description of the potential source of recovery, not an appraisal or guaranteed minimum value. The claim hierarchy can matter as much as operating performance. Customer demand, supplier obligations, debt service and working capital must be considered together. An event-driven interpretation should explain why the mechanism changes cash flows or the market’s recognition of them, rather than relying on a favorable narrative alone.

The relevant actors are Jack Dorsey, Amrita Ahuja, the board, banking regulators, merchants and consumers. Establish what each can approve, delay or veto before assigning value to a management change, operating reset or possible transaction. Any operating improvement should be measured against a stated baseline and the resources needed to obtain it. Unrecovered financial inputs remain missing; they are not assigned a zero value. An attractive franchise can still be an unattractive common security if senior claims consume its value, while a difficult operating situation can have a better expression elsewhere in the capital structure.
