## Recovered evidence

The February shareholder letter reports full-year gross profit of approximately $10.36 billion. Fourth-quarter gross profit was $2.872 billion, including Cash App $1.831 billion and Square $993 million. Segment amounts do not sum to the whole without considering other activities and adjustments. Cash App had 59 million monthly transacting actives; actives are not equivalent to paying subscribers or primary banking relationships. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Block, the primary operating checks are gross profit, transacting actives, lending losses and operating leverage. The potential asset support is payment and financial-services gross profit after credit losses, costs, capital needs and dilution. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of monetization of a scaled consumer network and an organizational cost reset is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
