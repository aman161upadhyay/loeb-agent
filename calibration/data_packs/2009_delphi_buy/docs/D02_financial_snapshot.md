The annual revenue series has a perimeter break: B recasts 2007 sales to $19,526m, so comparable 2008 revenue fell 13.9%, not the decline calculated against A's $22,283m. FY2008 net income includes $5,147m reorganisation income; it does not demonstrate operating profitability. Shares were about 565m issued at 2008 year-end, but old common is not the denominator for prospective reorganised equity.

Remaining five-year CFO/capex/D&A, share counts, tangible equity and tangible-capital-return fields were sought in SEC annual/quarterly archives, AnnualReports and Macrotrends historical searches; unresolved cells are dashes, not zeroes. Current claim priority and price are in D04.

The first-quarter filing covers the period ended March 31 and includes 564,637,307 old common shares outstanding. That count is not a prospective reorganized-equity denominator. The current analysis requires operating cash burn, secured and administrative claims, pension/labor obligations and facility-specific collateral. A fully reconciled entry-date recovery waterfall and executable DIP-loan quotation are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered for this pack. [1]

## Economic cross-check

The June 1 announcement describes a proposed transaction and GM support including up to $250 million of subordinated DIP financing. A final financing hearing is scheduled for June 16. Court authorization, financing conditions and closing are still material uncertainties; an announced path is not equivalent to a completed emergence. The first-quarter filing and current sale announcement supersede a generic assumption that suppliers can simply wait for normal auto demand. [1,2]

The relevant approach is enterprise/recovery value relative to the purchase price and legal priority of a specific DIP claim, with any conversion into new equity modeled separately. A liquidation scenario can differ greatly from going-concern value if customer programs are disrupted. Neither a reliable June 1 DIP execution price nor a normalized EBITDA target was recovered. No retrospective public-equity target is inserted into the entry snapshot. [1,2; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
