A standalone tangible-capital-return and fully reconciled July balance sheet remain unresolved after original 10-Ks/SEC facts, July results and issuer separation materials. Most importantly, historical consolidated EPS cannot be applied to the post-separation price.

The July 29 release reports second-quarter results including the bioscience operation because the quarter ended before the separation. It also gives a separate outlook for continuing operations after the spin. Thus reported consolidated sales and EPS cannot be capitalized as if all earnings remain at Baxter. A fully reconciled standalone balance sheet, cutoff net-debt bridge and exact August 5 price were not recovered in this pack. [1]

## Economic cross-check

Management says results exceeded prior guidance and supplies second-half continuing-operations EPS before special items of $0.58–0.62, with $0.29–0.31 for the third quarter. These are post-separation forecasts rather than the old company's historical run rate. Foreign exchange affects reported sales. The operating opportunity is to assess a now-focused business against appropriate peers, not to assume a completed spin alone guarantees improved margins. [1]

Valuation needs parent-only revenue, sustainable operating margins, cash taxes, capital expenditure and shares/debt after the distribution. A peer-margin normalization case can create substantial value but requires an executor and a timetable. The parent's historic consolidated multiple is not comparable with the new business. No complete current SOTP, execution price or shareholder-return target was recovered; an exact prospective IRR would be unsupported. [1; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
