## Recovered evidence

Compare store economics, online conversion, services attachment and product mix rather than equating this retailer with an internet marketplace. Domestic computing/mobile represented 35% of December sales, up from 31%; consumer electronics represented 40%, down from 41%, and entertainment 16%, down from 20%. Those mix shifts identify a mechanism to investigate, not a guarantee of improved gross margins. The dated fiscal-year peer multiples are in D04. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Choose comparable economics before comparing trading multiples. Revenue can be gross or net, earnings can be adjusted or reported, and capital requirements can sit on different balance sheets. A useful peer comparison reconciles accounting, business mix, geography, customer concentration and financing before assigning a discount or premium. A stock does not become inexpensive merely because a less comparable company trades at a higher multiple. Where peer data have not been recovered, the numerical comparison remains unavailable.

For Best Buy, the specific mechanism is connected-product growth and an online operating reset inside an incumbent retailer, and the relevant outcome measures are comparable sales, online growth, gross profit dollars and cash conversion. Ask whether peers have already demonstrated the operating outcome being proposed here, whether their managements control the same levers and whether their claim hierarchy is comparable. Industry growth does not guarantee supplier returns when customers retain bargaining power, capacity expands or required investment absorbs the incremental profit. Conversely, a slow-growing industry can support attractive returns where scarce assets, recurring relationships or forced transactions create pricing power.

Separate common industry risk from issuer-specific repair. The key bear mechanism is that online price competition and weak legacy categories may outweigh mobile growth and make restructuring a permanent cash expense. A relative-value case must explain why this issuer can improve rather than simply assuming that the entire industry rerates. Identify what evidence would distinguish a cyclical rebound from structural change. This packet supplies no unstated peer target margins, share-price history or consensus forecasts. Any proposed benchmark is an underwriting question, not an additional fact or an investor quotation.
