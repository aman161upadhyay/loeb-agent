## Recovered evidence

Five-week December revenue was $8.4 billion, unchanged year over year. Domestic revenue was $6.5 billion, up 0.4%; international revenue was $1.9 billion, down 1.7%. Total comparable sales fell 1.2%, including domestic negative 0.4% and international negative 4.3%. New stores support revenue even when established-store productivity falls. International sales outside Canada are reported on a two-month lag, limiting the apparent simultaneity of the comparison. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Best Buy, the primary operating checks are comparable sales, online growth, gross profit dollars and cash conversion. The potential asset support is retail inventory, services and continuing franchise cash flows after leases and restructuring obligations. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of connected-product growth and an online operating reset inside an incumbent retailer is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
