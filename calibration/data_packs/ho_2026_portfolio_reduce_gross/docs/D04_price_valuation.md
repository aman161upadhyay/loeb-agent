These index metrics supply an independently observable opportunity-cost and hedge-risk baseline. The monthly earnings multiple is not a January 30 live forward-consensus multiple. A 3.57% broad-market trailing earnings yield versus a 4.26% Treasury yield does not directly equal an equity risk premium: earnings growth, inflation, taxes and duration differ. Neither the index level nor VIX establishes the portfolio's own fair value, market capitalization, EV or target price. Those issuer concepts are inapplicable to a multi-asset hedge decision.

## Recovered evidence

The packet contains no reliable January marked-to-market position weights, aggregated forward P/E or factor-model covariance. Do not invent a target or imply that gains alone prove overvaluation. The valuation question is whether expected returns after a rerating still compensate for correlated downside, financing and opportunity cost. Price-target attainment is a scenario to test against company models, not an observed event supplied here. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Third Point portfolio risk, build the valuation around reassessment of concentrated winners, valuation targets and factor correlation. Potential asset support comes from portfolio liquidity and risk limits rather than a single issuer’s liquidation value. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are gross exposure, net exposure, factor sensitivity, crowding and liquidity. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
