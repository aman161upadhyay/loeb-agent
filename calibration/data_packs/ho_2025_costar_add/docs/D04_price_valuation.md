## Recovered evidence

The Q1 13F, public May 15, reports 1.975 million common shares worth $156.479 million at March 31. Dividing value by shares implies a historical quarter-end mark of about $79.23, not the June 30 close or cost basis. The April letter’s long-range EBITDA potential is a contingent forecast. The dated multiples above do not by themselves establish a calibrated target or upside/downside ratio. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For CoStar Group, build the valuation around a board refresh and capital-allocation oversight following the April settlement. Potential asset support comes from recurring commercial data and marketplace earnings, less residential investment and group obligations. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are core commercial cash earnings, Homes.com investment and board accountability. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
