## Recovered evidence

CoStar combines commercial real estate information with online marketplaces. The original disclosed Third Point thesis calls the commercial franchise the Bloomberg of commercial real estate and argues that value is obscured by residential investment. This is an existing-position update: the question is whether the agreed governance changes improve the opportunity, not whether the fund has discovered a new issuer. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

For CoStar Group, distinguish the operating asset from the security through which the asset is owned. The investable mechanism is a board refresh and capital-allocation oversight following the April settlement. The existence of that mechanism does not establish a numerical expected return. Identify which customer payment reaches the enterprise, what direct expense is required to earn it, what central costs remain and which capital commitments must be funded before common shareholders receive cash. The candidate operating indicators are core commercial cash earnings, Homes.com investment and board accountability. A rising revenue line without the associated cash and capital bridge is insufficient evidence of improving business quality.

The proposed asset support is recurring commercial data and marketplace earnings, less residential investment and group obligations. This is a description of the potential source of recovery, not an appraisal or guaranteed minimum value. The claim hierarchy can matter as much as operating performance. Customer demand, supplier obligations, debt service and working capital must be considered together. An event-driven interpretation should explain why the mechanism changes cash flows or the market’s recognition of them, rather than relying on a favorable narrative alone.

The relevant actors are Andy Florance, the board, John Berisford, Rachel Glaser, Christine McCarthy and concerned shareholders. Establish what each can approve, delay or veto before assigning value to a management change, operating reset or possible transaction. Any operating improvement should be measured against a stated baseline and the resources needed to obtain it. Unrecovered financial inputs remain missing; they are not assigned a zero value. An attractive franchise can still be an unattractive common security if senior claims consume its value, while a difficult operating situation can have a better expression elsewhere in the capital structure.
