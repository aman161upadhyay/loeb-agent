## Recovered evidence

The June 30 price and explicitly dated capital bridge are recovered above. A useful model values Precision Machinery separately from industrial operations, deducts group costs and liabilities, and avoids counting the same earnings both in segments and consolidated profit. The observed 10.6% group margin is a starting point, not proof of a quantified addressable margin gap against peers. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Ebara, build the valuation around semiconductor equipment growth inside a diversified machinery portfolio. Potential asset support comes from industrial and semiconductor cash flows net of debt and separation/restructuring costs. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are precision machinery orders, group margins and return on invested capital. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
