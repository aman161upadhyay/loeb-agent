## Recovered evidence

The June 30 close is recovered above; a fully allocated sum-of-parts multiple and cutoff sell-side consensus remain unreconciled. An appropriate bridge values infrastructure and the remaining manufacturing franchise separately, deducts allocated debt and separation costs, and avoids assigning a premium multiple to low-margin pass-through manufacturing revenue. The release expects low-to-mid-single-digit growth and cash generation in remaining Flex; those claims require independent operating verification. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Flex, build the valuation around announced separation of Cloud and Power Infrastructure into an independent public company. Potential asset support comes from cash earnings of infrastructure and remaining manufacturing operations after debt and separation costs. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are infrastructure revenue, branded mix, cash margins and separation execution. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
