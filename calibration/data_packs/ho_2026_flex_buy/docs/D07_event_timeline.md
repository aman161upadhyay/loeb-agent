## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Map each stage of the mechanism to a decision maker and an observable deliverable. The relevant actors are Revathi Advaithi, Michael Hartung, Flex’s board, customers and regulators. Ask which approvals are binding, which commitments are aspirational and which actions depend on financing, a regulator, a customer or a controlling shareholder. A public plan is not a completed transaction; a completed transaction is not proof that every shareholder received the same price. Distinguish operating catalysts from contractual cash-payment dates.

The event being evaluated is announced separation of Cloud and Power Infrastructure into an independent public company. A timetable should allow for implementation expenditure, delay, disclosure lag and the possibility of no resolution. The next evidence should illuminate infrastructure revenue, branded mix, cash margins and separation execution. Treat a future year or target date explicitly described as guidance in a pre-cutoff source as a forecast, not a subsequent fact. No actual event after this desk date is admissible merely because it fits the expected calendar.

Construct a base-case route and a failure route. In the latter, identify whether the investor can change the outcome through engagement, must rely on counterparties, or can only change the instrument and size. The adverse mechanism is that hyperscaler concentration, expansion requirements and separation costs can reduce the apparent sum-of-parts uplift. Governance access, board representation and voting agreements can affect both leverage over management and trading freedom; they are not interchangeable. Exact legal restrictions and nomination dates must be checked in source documents rather than invented. This chronology reports only recovered evidence and does not convert a quarter-end observation into an exact trading day.
