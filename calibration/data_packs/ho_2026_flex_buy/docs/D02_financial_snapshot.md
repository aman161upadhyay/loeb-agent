## Recovered evidence

The May announcement describes 22 engineering/manufacturing centers in the proposed infrastructure business and more than 75 manufacturing/logistics sites across 30 countries in the remaining company. Those are operating footprint counts, not revenue or valuation. A complete standalone debt/cash, pension and stranded-cost allocation was not recovered; no precise equity value is assigned to either entity. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Flex, the primary operating checks are infrastructure revenue, branded mix, cash margins and separation execution. The potential asset support is cash earnings of infrastructure and remaining manufacturing operations after debt and separation costs. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of announced separation of Cloud and Power Infrastructure into an independent public company is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
