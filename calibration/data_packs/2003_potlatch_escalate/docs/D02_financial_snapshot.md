The 2002 loss includes a large discontinued-business disposal charge. Historical ROE above deliberately uses year-end equity, so it is not directly comparable with the proxy's performance-compensation measure. Unfilled early EBIT/D&A, tangible book and return-on-tangible-capital fields were checked against this SEC filing, [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pch/financials/) and [Macrotrends](https://www.macrotrends.net/stocks/charts/PCH/potlatch/financial-statements); their accessible history does not supply a consistent five-year tangible-capital bridge.

The proxy reports 2002 loss per share of $8.23 and negative 33.1% return on common equity, versus negative 4.1% for a sample of 15 forest-products peers. CEO Siegel's reported base salary was $578,880 and his annual grant covered 66,000 options, with a $475,200 grant-date model value. No cash management-performance bonus was paid for 2002 because earnings failed the plan minimum. [1]

## Economic cross-check

The April 17 local report describes a first-quarter loss of $8.9 million, smaller than the year-earlier loss, after three losing years. A new Lewiston labor contract includes a 3% hourly pay cut for 19 months and is expected to save $5 million annually. Some analysts see improving performance. Those improvements are genuine counterevidence to a claim that every aspect of management's operating record is deteriorating. [2]

The April 17 quotation of $21.95 is superseded for market valuation by the dated closing-price table in D04. The March 20 record-date share count is 28,636,755. Debt and cash are now supplied separately from independently appraised timber value; neither book equity nor enterprise value constitutes an appraisal of the timber. [1,2; financial statements F]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Enterprise value in D04 is a dated, mixed-date desk calculation, not a fully diluted appraisal. Ratios retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
