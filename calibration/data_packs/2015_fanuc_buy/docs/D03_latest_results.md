The issuer provides cumulative nine-month results rather than a normalized annual earnings figure. The increase in machining sales is unusually large and may depend on a concentrated equipment-investment cycle. The table preserves that cyclicality instead of using the growth rate as a perpetual forecast.

FA sales were ¥188.700bn, up 14.8%; Robot ¥135.421bn, up 23.0%; Robomachine ¥202.285bn, up 282.9%. Total nine-month orders were ¥551.2bn. Management cites broader machine-tool recovery, robot demand in the Americas and China, and lively short-term IT demand. Full-year guidance remains ¥688.2bn sales, ¥268bn operating income and ¥185.1bn net income, or ¥945.98 EPS. The large machining growth component makes extrapolating the group growth rate especially risky. [1]

## Economic cross-check

Nine-month sales were ¥526.406bn, up 60.9%; operating income was ¥211.910bn, up 85.4%, a calculated 40.3% sales margin. Net income was ¥149.453bn, up 93.9%, and basic EPS ¥763.82. Cash/deposits were ¥808.703bn plus ¥120bn securities, chiefly negotiable certificates of deposit. Total liabilities were ¥187.608bn, including ¥42.847bn retirement-benefit liability; the balance sheet shows no separate borrowing line. Do not describe all liabilities as debt. [1]

The January 28 report leaves full-year guidance unchanged and says the year-end dividend forecast will be disclosed when available. Nine-month financial statements had not completed their statutory quarterly review at publication. Changes to retirement-benefit accounting reduced opening retained earnings by ¥1.452bn, with negligible nine-month profit impact. The board can alter payout, treasury-share treatment and communication without a breakup, but the company statement does not commit to an additional capital-return program. [1]

## Underwriting interpretation

Reading the update: changes in reported earnings must be attributed among volumes, prices, mix, costs, accounting and changes in the asset perimeter. A favorable headline does not establish an inflection, and an adverse headline does not prove every asset has become worthless. Management guidance is a statement of expectation, not an achieved result. The important evidence is the part of the operating bridge that can be checked at the next reporting date.

For a position depending on an event, compare operational progress with the legal or governance process rather than treating the two as interchangeable. A board review, adviser appointment, hearing or strategy announcement is not a completed transaction. Delay can consume cash or reduce expected return even when a long-term value argument survives. The absence of a recovered current metric must remain explicit: no estimate here is quietly reconstructed from a future report. The agent can reason conditionally on the available information but should not claim a fully measured operating trend when comparable data are missing.
