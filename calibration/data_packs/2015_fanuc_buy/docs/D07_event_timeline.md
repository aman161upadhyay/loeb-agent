Title: FANUC Corporation — Dated event and authority map
Source: https://www.fanuc.co.jp/en/ir/announce/pdf/2015/financialresult201412_e.pdf; internal desk state where labeled
Date: 2015-02-10

The January 28 report leaves full-year guidance unchanged and says the year-end dividend forecast will be disclosed when available. Nine-month financial statements had not completed their statutory quarterly review at publication. Changes to retirement-benefit accounting reduced opening retained earnings by ¥1.452bn, with negligible nine-month profit impact. The board can alter payout, treasury-share treatment and communication without a breakup, but the company statement does not commit to an additional capital-return program. [1]

## Economic cross-check

FA sales were ¥188.700bn, up 14.8%; Robot ¥135.421bn, up 23.0%; Robomachine ¥202.285bn, up 282.9%. Total nine-month orders were ¥551.2bn. Management cites broader machine-tool recovery, robot demand in the Americas and China, and lively short-term IT demand. Full-year guidance remains ¥688.2bn sales, ¥268bn operating income and ¥185.1bn net income, or ¥945.98 EPS. The large machining growth component makes extrapolating the group growth rate especially risky. [1]

The desk mandate is a new Japanese common-equity investment. It must evaluate operating quality, industrial cyclicality, currency and the willingness of management to engage, rather than assuming an approved capital-return plan. Exact firm AUM, cash, gross/net and existing Japanese manufacturing exposure were not recovered. No target stake or future investor correspondence is supplied, and a new-mode replay is not a claim that the firm historically had no prior holding.

## Underwriting interpretation

Process map for the desk: distinguish an announced intention, a signed agreement, formal approval, closing and receipt of cash. The actor with legal authority may differ from the person explaining the economic case. A minority equity holder, a secured lender and a government creditor have different remedies. Any governance route must respect actual voting rights, nomination rules, consent requirements and confidentiality obligations; a large dollar position alone does not establish control.

The next checkpoint should produce observable evidence, such as a filing, vote, hearing, financing condition or comparable operating result. A timetable expressed in quarters is an analytical horizon, not a verified promise that a transaction closes then. The document stops at its stated information cutoff. Prospective dates mentioned above remain prospective and no later resolution is assumed. If an exact deadline was not recovered, the pack does not invent one. The relevant question is what new information the process can resolve and how much capital or time can be consumed while it remains unresolved.
