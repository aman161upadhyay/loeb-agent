Title: FANUC Corporation — Fund mandate, prior state and constraints
Source: https://www.fanuc.co.jp/en/ir/announce/pdf/2015/financialresult201412_e.pdf; internal desk state where labeled
Date: 2015-02-10

The desk mandate is a new Japanese common-equity investment. It must evaluate operating quality, industrial cyclicality, currency and the willingness of management to engage, rather than assuming an approved capital-return plan. Exact firm AUM, cash, gross/net and existing Japanese manufacturing exposure were not recovered. No target stake or future investor correspondence is supplied, and a new-mode replay is not a claim that the firm historically had no prior holding.

## Economic cross-check

Fanuc supplies factory-automation controls, servo motors, robots and small machining centers. Installed controls, customer processes and service capability can create substantial switching costs. Manufacturing demand is cyclical even when automation penetration has a secular tailwind. Robotics, control systems and machining centers have different order patterns and should not be treated as one undifferentiated technology-growth business. [1; analyst framework]

The January 28 report leaves full-year guidance unchanged and says the year-end dividend forecast will be disclosed when available. Nine-month financial statements had not completed their statutory quarterly review at publication. Changes to retirement-benefit accounting reduced opening retained earnings by ¥1.452bn, with negligible nine-month profit impact. The board can alter payout, treasury-share treatment and communication without a breakup, but the company statement does not commit to an additional capital-return program. [1]

## Underwriting interpretation

Portfolio accounting for this desk: use current market exposure, not the issuer ownership percentage, to calculate capital at risk. Gross and net can conceal concentrated sector, currency, liquidity or event dependencies. A credit position and an equity position may respond to the same business shock despite occupying different strategy buckets. Derivative economic exposure, voting shares, collateral and counterparty obligations should be tracked separately. An absent exposure report is not evidence that no such risk exists.

The available record does not justify inventing current cash, top holdings or risk limits. Size must remain conditional where those inputs are missing. For an existing holding, separate the original thesis from new facts and sunk cost; for a fresh idea, do not assume ownership merely because the company has appeared in historical commentary. The fund's ability to wait for value depends on portfolio liquidity and obligations as well as the company's assets. No eventual trade, public campaign choice or realized return is encoded in the mandate above.
