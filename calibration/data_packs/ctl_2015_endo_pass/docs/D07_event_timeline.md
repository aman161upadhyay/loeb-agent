## Recovered evidence

January 29: Auxilium closes. March 2: full-year financial results are available. Subsequent integration checkpoints should include product sales, cash restructuring costs, leverage and management’s earnings reconciliation. No later acquisition or litigation development is included. A plausible event-driven thesis has a concrete integration mechanism but no assured date for equity realization. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Map each stage of the mechanism to a decision maker and an observable deliverable. The relevant actors are Rajiv De Silva, Endo’s board, creditors and pharmaceutical regulators. Ask which approvals are binding, which commitments are aspirational and which actions depend on financing, a regulator, a customer or a controlling shareholder. A public plan is not a completed transaction; a completed transaction is not proof that every shareholder received the same price. Distinguish operating catalysts from contractual cash-payment dates.

The event being evaluated is integration of Auxilium following the January 29 acquisition. A timetable should allow for implementation expenditure, delay, disclosure lag and the possibility of no resolution. The next evidence should illuminate organic product sales, adjusted EPS reconciled to cash earnings and leverage. Treat a future year or target date explicitly described as guidance in a pre-cutoff source as a forecast, not a subsequent fact. No actual event after this desk date is admissible merely because it fits the expected calendar.

Construct a base-case route and a failure route. In the latter, identify whether the investor can change the outcome through engagement, must rely on counterparties, or can only change the instrument and size. The adverse mechanism is that acquisition-led adjusted earnings can conceal weak organic demand, patent exposure, litigation and refinancing risk. Governance access, board representation and voting agreements can affect both leverage over management and trading freedom; they are not interchangeable. Exact legal restrictions and nomination dates must be checked in source documents rather than invented. This chronology reports only recovered evidence and does not convert a quarter-end observation into an exact trading day.
