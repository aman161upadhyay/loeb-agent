Neither the Q4 adjusted run rate nor the announced disposal cash can be substituted for settled litigation liabilities.

## Recovered evidence

The March 2 financial release covers the period before a full quarter of combined Auxilium operations. Endo reports completion of the acquisition on January 29. Integration economics are prospective, not an established run rate. The increase in fourth-quarter adjusted EPS is not itself evidence of accelerating organic growth across every product; product mix, acquisitions, costs and tax must be separated. On the same day Endo announced a sale agreement for AMS Men’s/Prostate Health for up to $1.65 billion, including $1.6 billion upfront cash, subject to closing. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Separate the measurement period from the publication date. The last quarter reported by the desk date may be considerably older than the contemplated investment decision. A filing received after the cutoff is not available simply because its financial period ended earlier. Likewise, a strategic announcement can change the opportunity without supplying a full quarter of evidence under the new strategy. The excerpt above states the latest recovered information, not a claim that every contemporaneous release has been retrieved.

The operating question is whether integration of Auxilium following the January 29 acquisition changes organic product sales, adjusted EPS reconciled to cash earnings and leverage. Construct a bridge through price, volume, mix, cost, financing and required reinvestment rather than assuming every favorable change is organic. Management commentary is evidence of expectations and intended actions; it is not independent verification of market size, attainable margins or terminal valuation. A single strong or weak quarter can reflect timing and working capital. A comparison needs a stable accounting perimeter before it can support an earnings inflection.

The contrary mechanism is that acquisition-led adjusted earnings can conceal weak organic demand, patent exposure, litigation and refinancing risk. Test that proposition against the next observable operating disclosures, not an eventual share-price outcome. A positive market reaction cannot establish customer retention, cash conversion or liability coverage. Identify the data that would overturn the optimistic interpretation and the data that would overturn the skeptical one. Missing consensus estimates, segment details or current liquidity measures remain explicit limitations. Do not replace them with numbers remembered from another date, a future manager letter or an unaudited reconstruction that lacks contemporaneous support.
