## Recovered evidence

The $4.31 annual adjusted EPS figure is historical, not a forward denominator for the combined group without an integration bridge. Acquisition debt must be added in the equity-to-enterprise-value bridge, and subtracted when working back from operating value to equity; it is not deducted from EV. The dated $19.45bn bridge above omits subsequent acquisition-financing cash uses and is not a pro-forma current EV. Cost synergies cannot be counted twice. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Endo International, build the valuation around integration of Auxilium following the January 29 acquisition. Potential asset support comes from marketed pharmaceutical products and generic-drug cash flows, subordinated to acquisition debt and litigation obligations. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are organic product sales, adjusted EPS reconciled to cash earnings and leverage. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
