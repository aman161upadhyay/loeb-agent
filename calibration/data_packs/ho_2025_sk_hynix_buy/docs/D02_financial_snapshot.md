## Recovered evidence

First-quarter revenue was KRW 17.6391 trillion, operating profit KRW 7.4405 trillion and net profit KRW 8.1082 trillion. Reported operating margin was 42% and net margin 46%. Net profit exceeds operating profit; no analyst should equate the net margin with recurring factory profitability without reconciling below-the-line items. These are reported quarter results, not an annual forecast. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For SK hynix, the primary operating checks are HBM shipments, product mix, operating margin and free cash flow. The potential asset support is profitable memory manufacturing, subject to cycle, capex and geopolitical risk. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of an AI-memory mix and technology inflection is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
