## Recovered evidence

The June 30 close and dated trailing valuation are recovered above; a historical consensus forward multiple remains unreconciled. No price target or later manager multiple is imported. A valuation bridge needs through-cycle DRAM profitability, HBM mix, NAND economics, capital expenditure and net debt. Capitalizing peak margins as perpetual earnings can create a false bargain, even where the strategic product position is attractive. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For SK hynix, build the valuation around an AI-memory mix and technology inflection. Potential asset support comes from profitable memory manufacturing, subject to cycle, capex and geopolitical risk. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are HBM shipments, product mix, operating margin and free cash flow. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
