## Recovered evidence

Commercial subscription data and property marketplaces should be compared on retention, conversion and cash margins. Residential portals have different sales economics from commercial information. No peer P/E comparison can resolve whether present spending is discretionary growth investment or an enduring subsidy. The issuer’s strong headline revenue growth and the activist’s discipline thesis are competing interpretations of business economics, not two different share classes. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Choose comparable economics before comparing trading multiples. Revenue can be gross or net, earnings can be adjusted or reported, and capital requirements can sit on different balance sheets. A useful peer comparison reconciles accounting, business mix, geography, customer concentration and financing before assigning a discount or premium. A stock does not become inexpensive merely because a less comparable company trades at a higher multiple. Where peer data have not been recovered, the numerical comparison remains unavailable.

For CoStar Group, the specific mechanism is assessment of the board-settlement remedy against residential investment and core growth, and the relevant outcome measures are net new bookings, segment cash earnings and spending accountability. Ask whether peers have already demonstrated the operating outcome being proposed here, whether their managements control the same levers and whether their claim hierarchy is comparable. Industry growth does not guarantee supplier returns when customers retain bargaining power, capacity expands or required investment absorbs the incremental profit. Conversely, a slow-growing industry can support attractive returns where scarce assets, recurring relationships or forced transactions create pricing power.

Separate common industry risk from issuer-specific repair. The key bear mechanism is that management can preserve residential spending and obscure segment returns despite the governance intervention. A relative-value case must explain why this issuer can improve rather than simply assuming that the entire industry rerates. Identify what evidence would distinguish a cyclical rebound from structural change. This packet supplies no unstated peer target margins, share-price history or consensus forecasts. Any proposed benchmark is an underwriting question, not an additional fact or an investor quotation.
