FCF=CFO minus PP&E capex, not Nestlé's differently defined published free cash flow. ROTC=operating profit/(net PP&E + current assets − cash − non-interest-bearing current liabilities), before tax and excluding goodwill. Trading operating profit in 2016 was CHF13,693m, distinct from the CHF13,163m operating subtotal above. The 2014 L'Oréal/Galderma transaction distorts net earnings and disposal-related cash. Values are reported figures for the periods, retrieved from Nestlé's 2013, 2014 and 2016 financial statements.

Full-year sales were CHF89.469 billion, reported growth 0.8%. Organic growth was 3.2%, comprising 2.4% real internal growth and 0.8% pricing. Reported trading operating margin was 15.3%, up 20 basis points; the comparable margin excluding selected items was about 16%. Underlying EPS was CHF3.40. Management proposed CHF2.30 per share in dividends, the twenty-second consecutive increase. Average working capital declined from 4.7% to 2.8% of sales. These are different indicators: working-capital release raises cash without necessarily raising recurring operating earnings. [S1]

Accounting treatment. The figures above retain their original period, currency and definition. Reported and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable, and segment profit is not automatically consolidated distributable cash. Reconcile recurring operating performance with financing costs, taxes, exceptional charges, working capital and necessary reinvestment. An acquisition, divestiture or change in reporting perimeter can change growth and share counts without representing organic improvement. Do not sum overlapping segment or geographic disclosures.

Financial underwriting should use a bridge, not a headline ratio. Start from recurring revenue and operating profit, charge maintenance investment and financing obligations, and test cash conversion under weaker demand or higher costs. A non-cash charge may not consume cash this quarter but can reveal that prior capital allocation failed. A working-capital release may be real cash without being repeatable earnings. Debt maturities, restricted cash, leases and off-balance-sheet obligations require separate modelling beyond the annual balances. D04 labels the annual diluted-share proxy explicitly; it is not an exact spot ownership denominator or an assumption of zero future dilution.

Analyst cross-check: For any apparent earnings improvement, test three reconciliations: operating income to operating cash flow, operating cash flow to free cash flow after capital spending, and enterprise earnings to the actual security's claim after interest, preferred distributions and dilution. Missing inputs prevent a precise return calculation.
