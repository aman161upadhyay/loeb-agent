Title: Third Point portfolio — Fund mandate, prior state and constraints

Date: 2008-09-30

The fund is carrying event-driven positions after a positive second quarter and an adverse July reversal. Reported quarterly winners included EXCO, MasterCard, Plains and Questar, while Deutsche Boerse, Daimler, Yahoo, Huntsman and Phoenix were detractors. This is partial historical attribution, not the September holdings file. Exact cash, credit composition, gross/net and remaining redemption capacity are not available in the recovered source. [1]

## Economic cross-check

The object is an event-driven, multi-asset portfolio, not a single operating company. Equity longs, equity shorts, credit and merger situations can share liquidity, funding and counterparty risks despite different fundamental stories. The midyear letter describes substantial energy exposure and financial shorts. A portfolio decision must evaluate those joint exposures rather than infer safety from the nominal number of positions. [1]

This desk is frozen at quarter-end. The last recovered fund letter is July 25 and documents the first adverse factor reversal. No subsequent quarterly-letter explanation, final annual return or later formal risk-policy description is an input. Current counterparties, redemption dates, collateral terms and executable prices must be treated as unknown. The decision concerns the current portfolio risk budget, not anticipation of a named future market bottom. [1]

## Underwriting interpretation

Portfolio accounting for this desk: use current market exposure, not the issuer ownership percentage, to calculate capital at risk. Gross and net can conceal concentrated sector, currency, liquidity or event dependencies. A credit position and an equity position may respond to the same business shock despite occupying different strategy buckets. Derivative economic exposure, voting shares, collateral and counterparty obligations should be tracked separately. An absent exposure report is not evidence that no such risk exists.

The available record does not justify inventing current cash, top holdings or risk limits. Size must remain conditional where those inputs are missing. For an existing holding, separate the original thesis from new facts and sunk cost; for a fresh idea, do not assume ownership merely because the company has appeared in historical commentary. The fund's ability to wait for value depends on portfolio liquidity and obligations as well as the company's assets. No eventual trade, public campaign choice or realized return is encoded in the mandate above.
