Title: Yahoo! Inc. — Financial record and accounting bridge
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1011006/000115752312001850/a50240268ex99-1.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1011006/000089914012000299/y7749418b.htm; internal desk state where labeled
Date: 2012-05-03

First-quarter revenue ex-TAC was $1.077bn, up 1%, and GAAP revenue $1.221bn, also up 1%. Operating income fell 11% to $169m. Net earnings rose 28% to $286m and diluted EPS rose 38% to $0.23. Net profit is not the same as core operating profit; the operating decline limits a simple interpretation that a higher EPS number validates the turnaround. [1]

## Economic cross-check

The company says results beat expectations and describes a new Consumer, Technology and Regions organization. The May 3 evidence identifies a discrepancy between the CEO biography in company SEC materials and Stonehill College's record: the claimed degree combines accounting and computer science, whereas the college confirms accounting only and says computer-science degrees started after his graduation. This is a contemporaneous allegation supported by identified checks, not a final investigation finding. [1,2]

The original sum-of-parts method still requires separate after-tax affiliate values and a realistic core earnings multiple. No fully refreshed May 3 SOTP or daily close was recovered. A governance controversy can increase the probability of change without adding a dollar to intrinsic operating value. The relevant incremental question is whether improved oversight makes realization of existing asset value more likely relative to the costs of disruption. [1,2; analyst framework]

## Underwriting interpretation

Accounting treatment for the desk: keep reported results, adjusted measures, cash movement and forecasts in separate columns. A disposal gain, impairment or reserve adjustment can materially change net income without changing recurring cash generation by the same amount. Consolidated earnings are not automatically distributable to the security under consideration. Check debt, taxes, pensions, minority interests and restrictions before describing an asset as surplus. A stale balance-sheet figure is not a verified current cash balance.

The critical bridge is from operating activity to cash available after maintenance investment and senior obligations. A peer margin or normalized-profit scenario is an assumption until an executor, cost base and implementation timetable support it. A fund's ownership percentage of an issuer is not its percentage of NAV. Missing fully diluted shares, current debt or cash prevents a precise current enterprise value; the pack does not fill those gaps using a later filing. Ratios should retain the period and accounting scope of their numerator and denominator.
