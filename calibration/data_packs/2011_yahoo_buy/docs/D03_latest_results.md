Title: Yahoo! Inc. — Latest operating and disclosure update

Date: 2011-09-07

On September 6 the board removed Carol Bartz and appointed CFO Tim Morse interim CEO. A search for a permanent CEO and a comprehensive strategic review are announced. The executive leadership council supports day-to-day operations and the review. No new permanent chief has been selected. The board says it sees significant growth opportunities, but the management change does not itself prove the company's monetization problem is fixed. [2]

## Economic cross-check

Second-quarter revenue ex-TAC was $1.076bn, down 5%; GAAP revenue was $1.229bn, down 23%, materially affected by search-agreement presentation. Operating income increased 9% to $191m. Net income was $237m and diluted EPS $0.18 versus $0.15. Excluding stated agreement and special items, revenue ex-TAC rose approximately 1%. Reported headline revenue decline is not an apples-to-apples measure of audience collapse. [1]

The July results are the latest recovered operating release. September 6 creates a live governance/strategy event: interim leadership, a permanent-CEO search and a comprehensive review. The board controls recruitment and asset decisions; outside shareholders can propose directors through the annual-meeting process. No investor letter dated after this cutoff, agreed board representation or completed Asian monetization is in the pack. [1,2]

## Underwriting interpretation

Reading the update: changes in reported earnings must be attributed among volumes, prices, mix, costs, accounting and changes in the asset perimeter. A favorable headline does not establish an inflection, and an adverse headline does not prove every asset has become worthless. Management guidance is a statement of expectation, not an achieved result. The important evidence is the part of the operating bridge that can be checked at the next reporting date.

For a position depending on an event, compare operational progress with the legal or governance process rather than treating the two as interchangeable. A board review, adviser appointment, hearing or strategy announcement is not a completed transaction. Delay can consume cash or reduce expected return even when a long-term value argument survives. The absence of a recovered current metric must remain explicit: no estimate here is quietly reconstructed from a future report. The agent can reason conditionally on the available information but should not claim a fully measured operating trend when comparable data are missing.
