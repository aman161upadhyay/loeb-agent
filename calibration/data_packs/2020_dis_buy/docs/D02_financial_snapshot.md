FCF=CFO minus capex. ROTC uses segment OP/(net PP&E + current assets − cash − non-interest-bearing current liabilities); its exclusion of goodwill and corporate expenses makes it an optimistic operating-capital proxy, not GAAP ROIC. Fox consolidation changes the 2019 perimeter and share count. Figures are reported figures for the periods, retrieved from the SEC annual-report archives for Disney and its predecessor registrant.

Quarterly revenue was $11.779 billion versus $20.262 billion, down 42%. Continuing-operation loss was $4.718 billion and diluted EPS a $2.61 loss. Excluding comparability items, EPS was $0.08, down 94%. Segment operating income fell 72% to $1.099 billion. Nine-month revenue was $50.681 billion, approximately flat, while nine-month adjusted EPS fell 53% to $2.22. Acquisition-period comparability matters because the prior period included Fox and Hulu for only part of the year. Segment income is not consolidated cash available to finance content, interest and parks investment. [S1]

Accounting treatment. The figures above retain their original period, currency and definition. Reported and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable, and segment profit is not automatically consolidated distributable cash. Reconcile recurring operating performance with financing costs, taxes, exceptional charges, working capital and necessary reinvestment. An acquisition, divestiture or change in reporting perimeter can change growth and share counts without representing organic improvement. Do not sum overlapping segment or geographic disclosures.

Financial underwriting should use a bridge, not a headline ratio. Start from recurring revenue and operating profit, charge maintenance investment and financing obligations, and test cash conversion under weaker demand or higher costs. A non-cash charge may not consume cash this quarter but can reveal that prior capital allocation failed. A working-capital release may be real cash without being repeatable earnings. Debt maturities, restricted cash, leases and off-balance-sheet obligations require separate modelling beyond the annual balances. D04 distinguishes the latest published ownership count from annual diluted weighted-average shares; neither implies zero future dilution.

Analyst cross-check: For any apparent earnings improvement, test three reconciliations: operating income to operating cash flow, operating cash flow to free cash flow after capital spending, and enterprise earnings to the actual security's claim after interest, preferred distributions and dilution. Missing inputs prevent a precise return calculation.
