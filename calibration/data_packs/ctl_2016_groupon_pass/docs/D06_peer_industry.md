## Recovered evidence

The comparison set includes local advertising, merchant services and retail discounting, each with different revenue recognition and capital intensity. A merchant network can have attractive incremental margins if repeat purchases are organic. A business reliant on recurring promotional incentives has different economics from a subscription service. No recovered peer valuation establishes a numerical margin or multiple gap. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Choose comparable economics before comparing trading multiples. Revenue can be gross or net, earnings can be adjusted or reported, and capital requirements can sit on different balance sheets. A useful peer comparison reconciles accounting, business mix, geography, customer concentration and financing before assigning a discount or premium. A stock does not become inexpensive merely because a less comparable company trades at a higher multiple. Where peer data have not been recovered, the numerical comparison remains unavailable.

For Groupon, the specific mechanism is a local-commerce turnaround under Rich Williams, and the relevant outcome measures are North American Local billings, repeat cohorts and contribution after marketing. Ask whether peers have already demonstrated the operating outcome being proposed here, whether their managements control the same levers and whether their claim hierarchy is comparable. Industry growth does not guarantee supplier returns when customers retain bargaining power, capacity expands or required investment absorbs the incremental profit. Conversely, a slow-growing industry can support attractive returns where scarce assets, recurring relationships or forced transactions create pricing power.

Separate common industry risk from issuer-specific repair. The key bear mechanism is that paid customer acquisition may buy transient traffic while merchant economics and customer retention remain weak. A relative-value case must explain why this issuer can improve rather than simply assuming that the entire industry rerates. Identify what evidence would distinguish a cyclical rebound from structural change. This packet supplies no unstated peer target margins, share-price history or consensus forecasts. Any proposed benchmark is an underwriting question, not an additional fact or an investor quotation.
