## Recovered evidence

Third Point’s Q2 13F, filed August 12, reports $10.492 billion in securities, not fund NAV. Baxter, Allergan, Dow and Facebook are major reportable holdings. A consumer-internet turnaround adds growth and discretionary-demand exposure to an already diversified event book. Actual gross/net, financing and the amount of capital available for another turnaround were not recovered in this packet. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Separate the investment manager, the trading fund and any listed feeder. Firm-wide assets, Offshore net assets, reported 13F security value and a feeder’s capitalization measure different things. A reported securities table excludes much credit, foreign ordinary shares, shorts, swaps and private assets. It therefore cannot establish current cash, gross exposure, net exposure or the amount of liquidity available for a new idea. Historical exposure tables remain dated rather than rolled forward mechanically.

The opportunity under review is a local-commerce turnaround under Rich Williams. Its business and financing sensitivities must be compared with the existing book, not judged in isolation. The relevant operating measures are North American Local billings, repeat cohorts and contribution after marketing. A small position in a binary or illiquid security can require more risk capital than its market value suggests. Short exposure, collateral requirements and private commitments can consume liquidity even where headline net equity exposure appears modest. Common industry labels can conceal a shared factor or refinancing sensitivity.

The relevant engagement actors are Rich Williams, management, merchants and the board. Access to management, existing agreements, information barriers and standstills can constrain a position independently of the valuation. Do not infer authority from another shareholder’s board seat or treat a disclosed common holding as the investor’s entire economic exposure. The figures supplied above describe only what was publicly recovered by the cutoff. No subsequent trade, manager explanation or result is part of this context. Where cash, exposure, nomination windows or a precise firm AUM were not recovered, those remain open inputs rather than silently assumed capacity.
