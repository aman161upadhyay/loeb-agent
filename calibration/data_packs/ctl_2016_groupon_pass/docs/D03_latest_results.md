## Recovered evidence

The July 27 release shows North American Local billings growth of 9% and an increased full-year outlook. These are observable operational improvements supporting a genuine turnaround case. They do not establish that every geography or category has repaired unit economics. The next quarter has not been released at this cutoff; no later acquisition or earnings surprise is included. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Separate the measurement period from the publication date. The last quarter reported by the desk date may be considerably older than the contemplated investment decision. A filing received after the cutoff is not available simply because its financial period ended earlier. Likewise, a strategic announcement can change the opportunity without supplying a full quarter of evidence under the new strategy. The excerpt above states the latest recovered information, not a claim that every contemporaneous release has been retrieved.

The operating question is whether a local-commerce turnaround under Rich Williams changes North American Local billings, repeat cohorts and contribution after marketing. Construct a bridge through price, volume, mix, cost, financing and required reinvestment rather than assuming every favorable change is organic. Management commentary is evidence of expectations and intended actions; it is not independent verification of market size, attainable margins or terminal valuation. A single strong or weak quarter can reflect timing and working capital. A comparison needs a stable accounting perimeter before it can support an earnings inflection.

The contrary mechanism is that paid customer acquisition may buy transient traffic while merchant economics and customer retention remain weak. Test that proposition against the next observable operating disclosures, not an eventual share-price outcome. A positive market reaction cannot establish customer retention, cash conversion or liability coverage. Identify the data that would overturn the optimistic interpretation and the data that would overturn the skeptical one. Missing consensus estimates, segment details or current liquidity measures remain explicit limitations. Do not replace them with numbers remembered from another date, a future manager letter or an unaudited reconstruction that lacks contemporaneous support.
