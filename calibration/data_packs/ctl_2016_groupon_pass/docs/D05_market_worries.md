Title: Groupon: The strongest contemporaneous counter-case
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1490281/000149028116000178/exhibit991-q22016.htm
Date: 2016-10-10

## Recovered evidence

A skeptic can argue that increased marketing expense creates headline customer growth without profitable retention. Discounts may attract deal-seekers rather than a durable merchant relationship. Goods inventory and international restructuring can consume cash while the investment narrative focuses on Local. Reported net losses remain material despite positive adjusted EBITDA. No contractual acquirer provides an unaffected-price backstop. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Give the skeptical mechanism equal analytical weight rather than reducing it to a generic volatility warning. The central threat is that paid customer acquisition may buy transient traffic while merchant economics and customer retention remain weak. This can harm the cash earnings, the timing of their receipt or the amount attributable to the selected security. Distinguish temporary mark-to-market stress from a permanent transfer of value to creditors, customers, counterparties or new shareholders. An attractive operating business can coexist with a structurally poor security claim.

The proposed asset support is merchant/customer network and available cash less operating liabilities, without an assumed sale; test how accessible it remains under distress. Book value, collateral value, enterprise value and common equity value are different quantities. Restrictions on disposal, taxes, legal costs, financing covenants and ongoing cash needs can make an apparently visible asset a weak liquidity source. No independent appraisal is supplied merely by naming an asset. For Groupon, current recovery amounts and probabilities should remain unasserted where not recovered.

Observable checks should focus on North American Local billings, repeat cohorts and contribution after marketing, together with funding, governance and execution. Specify which adverse observations would invalidate the mechanism of a local-commerce turnaround under Rich Williams, rather than waiting for a share price to recover. Conversely, specify the operating or contractual evidence that would rebut the bear case. Portfolio sizing must allow for a delayed catalyst, correlated liquidation and imperfect exit liquidity. These are analyst stress considerations, not a claim that any particular adverse scenario occurs. Do not turn an allegation into an adjudicated fact or attribute a skeptic’s reasoning to the investor without a dated source.
