The cutoff close, basic share denominator and dated enterprise-value bridge are recovered above; fully diluted capitalization and point-in-time consensus remain unresolved. A valuation should distinguish Local from Goods and deduct central costs rather than simply applying a software multiple to aggregate revenue. Annualizing $34 million of quarterly adjusted EBITDA is an illustrative operation, not an estimate endorsed here. Cash, working capital, share compensation and restructuring need separate reconciliation. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Groupon, build the valuation around a local-commerce turnaround under Rich Williams. Potential asset support comes from merchant/customer network and available cash less operating liabilities, without an assumed sale. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are North American Local billings, repeat cohorts and contribution after marketing. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
