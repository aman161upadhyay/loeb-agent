Title: Massey Energy Company — Dated event and authority map
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/37748/000119312505210164/dex991.htm; internal desk state where labeled
Date: 2005-11-15

The October 27 results provide the latest operating checkpoint for this November desk date. Management is committing 2007 and 2008 tonnage at strong prices, but those deliveries and collections are still prospective. The board controls distributions, leverage and advisor appointments. An annual shareholder vote offers an accountability route, subject to nomination and governance requirements; no proxy-contest result is part of this information set. [1]

## Economic cross-check

Produced tons sold were 10.0 million versus 10.1 million a year earlier and 11.6 million sequentially. Revenue per ton was $41.75 versus $36.33 a year earlier. Underground production fell to about 4.7 million tons from 6 million sequentially. The issuer specifically identifies underground production as a negative earnings factor, notwithstanding strong market prices. Full-year production was estimated at 43.5 million tons. [1]

This is a fresh-capital replay: do not infer a target holding from the fund's historical filings. The desk needs a reserve/productivity model, a capital-spending budget and a credible owner-governance route. Exact firm cash, long/short exposures and current portfolio weights have not been recovered for this date. Commodity exposure must be considered across the portfolio, not only as a company-specific governance dispute.

## Underwriting interpretation

Process map for the desk: distinguish an announced intention, a signed agreement, formal approval, closing and receipt of cash. The actor with legal authority may differ from the person explaining the economic case. A minority equity holder, a secured lender and a government creditor have different remedies. Any governance route must respect actual voting rights, nomination rules, consent requirements and confidentiality obligations; a large dollar position alone does not establish control.

The next checkpoint should produce observable evidence, such as a filing, vote, hearing, financing condition or comparable operating result. A timetable expressed in quarters is an analytical horizon, not a verified promise that a transaction closes then. The document stops at its stated information cutoff. Prospective dates mentioned above remain prospective and no later resolution is assumed. If an exact deadline was not recovered, the pack does not invent one. The relevant question is what new information the process can resolve and how much capital or time can be consumed while it remains unresolved.
