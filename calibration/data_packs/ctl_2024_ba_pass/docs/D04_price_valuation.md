## Recovered evidence

The cutoff close and last disclosed share count are recovered above; a consensus forward earnings multiple remains unresolved. The same-day 8% decline is a reported price change, not an absolute valuation. The prior release’s $3–$5 billion annual free-cash-flow guidance is historical, not a new forecast. Underwrite post-repair production, compensation, rework and debt rather than multiplying the backlog by an arbitrary conversion rate. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Boeing, build the valuation around an acute safety/quality shock creating a potential franchise dislocation. Potential asset support comes from commercial-aircraft and service cash flows after debt, customer obligations and repair costs. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are deliveries accepted by customers, free cash flow and production-quality milestones. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
