FCF=CFO minus capex. ROTC=operating profit/(net PP&E + current assets − cash − non-interest-bearing current liabilities), before tax and excluding goodwill. These are proxies, not management's ROIC. The 2015 disposal gain raises net income without raising continuing operating profit. Historical amounts are reported figures for the periods, retrieved from SEC annual filings, including Exhibit 13 to the 2017 10-K for comparable continuing cash flows.

First-quarter sales were $15.2 billion, up 10%, including 6% organic growth and about three points of currency benefit. GAAP EPS was $1.62, down 6%; adjusted EPS was $1.77, up 20%. Net income was approximately $1.3 billion. Operating cash flow of $453 million less capex of $337 million produces $116 million free cash flow. Low quarterly conversion reflects working capital and shipment timing, especially at Pratt and climate systems. GAAP and adjusted earnings differ by restructuring and significant items; they should not be mixed with different peer definitions. [S1]

Accounting treatment. The figures above retain their original period, currency and definition. Reported and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable, and segment profit is not automatically consolidated distributable cash. Reconcile recurring operating performance with financing costs, taxes, exceptional charges, working capital and necessary reinvestment. An acquisition, divestiture or change in reporting perimeter can change growth and share counts without representing organic improvement. Do not sum overlapping segment or geographic disclosures.

Financial underwriting should use a bridge, not a headline ratio. Start from recurring revenue and operating profit, charge maintenance investment and financing obligations, and test cash conversion under weaker demand or higher costs. A non-cash charge may not consume cash this quarter but can reveal that prior capital allocation failed. A working-capital release may be real cash without being repeatable earnings. Debt maturities, restricted cash, leases and off-balance-sheet obligations require separate modelling beyond the annual balances. D04 distinguishes the published ownership count from annual diluted weighted-average shares; neither implies zero transaction-related dilution.

Analyst cross-check: For any apparent earnings improvement, test three reconciliations: operating income to operating cash flow, operating cash flow to free cash flow after capital spending, and enterprise earnings to the actual security's claim after interest, preferred distributions and dilution. Missing inputs prevent a precise return calculation.
