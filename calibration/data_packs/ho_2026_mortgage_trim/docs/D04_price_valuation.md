The spread between a primary mortgage rate and a Treasury yield is not an option-adjusted RMBS spread: it includes origination and servicing economics and embeds different duration/convexity. Corporate credit indices supply opportunity-cost references, not collateral-equivalent peers. Publicly quoted loan price in cents, weighted coupon, loan-level cash flows, financing terms and security identifiers remain absent from the three checked portfolio disclosures; no pool EV or issuer P/E is meaningful here. A precise discounted-loan total return cannot be calculated without those inputs.

## Recovered evidence

No pool-level cutoff market price or yield was recovered. Value the discounted loan cash flows and deduct servicing, foreclosure, financing and structural costs. A par paydown can generate an attractive return on a below-par loan, but leverage changes the sensitivity of retained equity to delays and marks. The borrower’s home equity must not be mistaken for the investor’s unencumbered collateral cushion. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

No valuation input should silently move between dates. A historical mark inferred by dividing a filing’s market value by shares is not an execution price; a repurchase average is not the cutoff close; an announced exchange ratio is not a fixed cash consideration. Options’ reported underlying value is not cash invested. When a cutoff close or current estimate is missing, a precise entry multiple, target and prospective return cannot be claimed. Arithmetic may be performed on recovered figures only with the original period and units preserved.

For Discounted first-lien residential mortgage portfolio, build the valuation around monetization and refinancing of a seasoned discounted mortgage portfolio. Potential asset support comes from first-lien residential collateral subject to senior financing, servicing and foreclosure costs. Use separate base, upside and downside scenarios, deduct senior claims and incorporate the cash required while waiting for the event. Do not count the same operating earnings in both a capitalized segment and a separately valued asset. A higher peer multiple is justified only if differences in durability, capital intensity and growth survive the comparison.

The key monitoring measures are borrower equity, loan-to-value, paydown speed, financing yield and retained-risk concentration. A scenario in which they improve is not the same as a market forecast or a manager’s stated target. Required return must compensate for execution time, interim mark-to-market loss, financing and the chance that the event does not occur. No unsupported probability weights are supplied. Where the data cannot establish a two-sided numerical skew, describe the unresolved inputs rather than manufacture an attractive asymmetry. A missing valuation is a limitation on conviction, not evidence of either overvaluation or undervaluation.
