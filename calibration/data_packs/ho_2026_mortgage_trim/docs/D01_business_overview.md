## Recovered evidence

Third Point owns discounted performing and nonperforming residential mortgages. The original thesis separates borrower property value from loan trading price: a borrower may have substantial equity even where the lender’s loan trades below par. Refinancing or liquidation can pull discounted principal toward par. This is an existing structured-credit portfolio; no named consumer-mortgage issuer or identified pool is assumed. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

For Discounted first-lien residential mortgage portfolio, distinguish the operating asset from the security through which the asset is owned. The investable mechanism is monetization and refinancing of a seasoned discounted mortgage portfolio. The existence of that mechanism does not establish a numerical expected return. Identify which customer payment reaches the enterprise, what direct expense is required to earn it, what central costs remain and which capital commitments must be funded before common shareholders receive cash. The candidate operating indicators are borrower equity, loan-to-value, paydown speed, financing yield and retained-risk concentration. A rising revenue line without the associated cash and capital bridge is insufficient evidence of improving business quality.

The proposed asset support is first-lien residential collateral subject to senior financing, servicing and foreclosure costs. This is a description of the potential source of recovery, not an appraisal or guaranteed minimum value. The claim hierarchy can matter as much as operating performance. Customer demand, supplier obligations, debt service and working capital must be considered together. An event-driven interpretation should explain why the mechanism changes cash flows or the market’s recognition of them, rather than relying on a favorable narrative alone.

The relevant actors are borrowers, servicers, financing counterparties and mortgage investors. Establish what each can approve, delay or veto before assigning value to a management change, operating reset or possible transaction. Any operating improvement should be measured against a stated baseline and the resources needed to obtain it. Unrecovered financial inputs remain missing; they are not assigned a zero value. An attractive franchise can still be an unattractive common security if senior claims consume its value, while a difficult operating situation can have a better expression elsewhere in the capital structure.
