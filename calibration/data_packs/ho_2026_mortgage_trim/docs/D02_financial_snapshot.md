## Recovered evidence

The October letter says borrowers in the relevant mortgage portfolio have 40–55% equity in their homes. That implies collateral cushion before financing and transaction costs, not a guarantee against losses. Exact pool balances, weighted-average coupon, borrower concentration, financing advance rates and current loan prices were not disclosed. No modeled recovery, notional position size or NAV percentage is invented. The February 6 update describes reperforming first-lien owner-occupied loans with over 18 years of seasoning and average balances around $150,000; these are portfolio descriptors, not an appraisal of each pool. ## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Discounted first-lien residential mortgage portfolio, the primary operating checks are borrower equity, loan-to-value, paydown speed, financing yield and retained-risk concentration. The potential asset support is first-lien residential collateral subject to senior financing, servicing and foreclosure costs. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of monetization and refinancing of a seasoned discounted mortgage portfolio is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
