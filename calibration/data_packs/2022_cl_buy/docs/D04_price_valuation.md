The October 31 historical close is $73.84. A verified contemporary forward EPS consensus and Hill's standalone valuation bridge were not recovered. Applying a premium pet-food multiple requires segment profit and capital needs, then debt, tax and stranded costs. The consolidated $0.74 quarterly EPS is neither a normalized annual forecast nor sufficient to estimate a spin distribution. Separate demand durability from the price paid for it: a defensive franchise can be overvalued if the assumed margin rebound is already capitalized. No future portfolio transaction is treated as announced. [S1; analyst valuation]

Valuation construction. All calculated multiples must match numerator, denominator, currency and share basis. A historical close is an observation rather than an entry cost. A guidance multiple is not consensus, a trailing ratio is not a forward ratio, and an activist target is not independently verified intrinsic value. No exact target is manufactured when a necessary price or earnings input is missing.

Build a base case without the most ambitious event, then a separately costed event case. Sum-of-parts values require corporate costs, debt, minority claims, taxes and transaction friction; earnings-power values require necessary reinvestment and a credible margin bridge. Cash should be counted only if it can actually be distributed. A repayment, buyback or divestiture must not be counted again after its effect is already reflected in shares, interest or earnings. Test sensitivity to both the operating outcome and the exit multiple. A margin of safety cannot be inferred from a low headline P/E or a high-quality brand alone. Exact upside/downside skew and IRR are not asserted without recoverable valuation endpoints and a realistic timetable.

Analyst cross-check: A useful sensitivity table varies the earnings base and the exit multiple independently, then subtracts financing and implementation costs. Do not assign the same probability to upside, base and stress cases merely because three columns appear in a spreadsheet. Uncertain inputs warrant ranges, not spurious precision.
