FCF=CFO minus capex. ROTC=operating profit/(net PP&E + current assets − cash − non-interest-bearing current liabilities), before tax and excluding goodwill. High ROE partly reflects a thin equity base. The five-year debt series precedes the 2018 Snyder's-Lance financing; it must not be used as current acquisition-adjusted leverage. Figures are reported figures for the periods, retrieved from Campbell's SEC annual filings/company-facts archive.

Quarterly sales were $2.125 billion, up 15%, largely reflecting Snyder's-Lance; organic sales were flat. GAAP EBIT was a $475 million loss after $619 million Campbell Fresh impairment charges. Adjusted EBIT was $308 million versus $305 million, up 1%. GAAP EPS was a $1.31 loss; adjusted EPS was $0.70 versus $0.59, up 19%. Nine-month sales were $6.466 billion and organic sales declined 1%; adjusted EBIT fell 7% to $1.127 billion. The gap between adjusted EPS growth and EBIT growth requires financing, tax and share-count reconciliation. [S1]

Accounting treatment. The figures above retain their original period, currency and definition. Reported and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable, and segment profit is not automatically consolidated distributable cash. Reconcile recurring operating performance with financing costs, taxes, exceptional charges, working capital and necessary reinvestment. An acquisition, divestiture or change in reporting perimeter can change growth and share counts without representing organic improvement. Do not sum overlapping segment or geographic disclosures.

Financial underwriting should use a bridge, not a headline ratio. Start from recurring revenue and operating profit, charge maintenance investment and financing obligations, and test cash conversion under weaker demand or higher costs. A non-cash charge may not consume cash this quarter but can reveal that prior capital allocation failed. A working-capital release may be real cash without being repeatable earnings. Debt maturities, restricted cash, lease commitments and off-balance-sheet obligations need their own schedule. Where not given above, that schedule and an exact fully diluted share count are not available in contemporaneous sources recovered. This is an explicit limitation, not an assumption of zero debt or dilution.

Analyst cross-check: For any apparent earnings improvement, test three reconciliations: operating income to operating cash flow, operating cash flow to free cash flow after capital spending, and enterprise earnings to the actual security's claim after interest, preferred distributions and dilution. Missing inputs prevent a precise return calculation.
