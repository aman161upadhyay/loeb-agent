## Recovered evidence

The annual record above and August 8 quarterly release provide reported operating figures. Club occupancy, membership fees and expansion spending should be separated from accounting revenue and adjusted EBITDA. Physical clubs carry leases and operating expenses, so the business is not equivalent to a capital-light subscription platform. Any takeover value must flow through the full equity capitalization and controlling/minority interests. [1]


## Underwriting analysis — conditional, not management guidance

Build a bridge from the reported period to the security’s cash claim. Separate revenue recognition, gross profit, recurring operating expense, working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, interest and taxes. The facts above are the recovered figures; absence of a line in this packet does not imply its economic amount is zero. Pay special attention to the denominator: quarterly figures, annual results, operating profit, net income and adjusted earnings are not interchangeable. A percentage should retain the period and accounting definition attached to its source.

For Soho House & Co, the primary operating checks are net proceeds per minority share, process independence and competing offers. The potential asset support is membership franchise cash flows after club leases, debt, funding needs and controller constraints. Neither phrase substitutes for a cash-flow or recovery calculation. Debt at a subsidiary, restricted cash, customer advances and collateral pledges can prevent apparently available assets from reaching the listed parent. Likewise, a company’s adjusted metric may exclude an expense that still requires cash or compensates employees through dilution. Where the necessary reconciliation has not been recovered, no artificial precision is introduced.

The financial test of an independent and fair sale process for minority shareholders is incremental cash earned after all incremental investment, not merely an improvement in one reported ratio. Compare the same perimeter across periods; acquired and disposed businesses, changes in share count and accounting presentation can otherwise create spurious growth. Treat management forecasts as contingent statements, not balance-sheet assets. Before assigning a target size, stress a weaker operating period, higher financing cost and slower catalyst timing separately. These are analyst sensitivities rather than additional company disclosures.
