# L8 financial and valuation enrichment

## Scope and conventions

Enriched D02, D03, D04 and D06 in all eleven Era E hold-outs. Existing titles, compiled desk dates, ordering and underwriting prose are retained, with small corrections where a former “not recovered” statement became obsolete. No evaluator keys, predictions, scores, workflows or application code were changed.

Rows distinguish observation dates, financial periods, publication dates and management forecasts. Later retrieval of an earlier reported period is labeled. Year-end 2025 results not public at a December 2025/January 2026 cutoff are not treated as known. Equity values calculated from a cutoff close and an explicitly dated historical share count are **rebased estimates**, not exact cutoff share-register capitalizations. Debt/cash perimeters and lease inclusion are stated.

Peer quotes are cutoff-day Yahoo closes, except the explicitly historical pre-separation Western Digital reference. Their valuation denominators generally use FY2024 historical financial statements, not a recovered contemporaneous TTM/forward-consensus series. This is a material remaining limitation, particularly in 2026 packs. D06 adds reported growth, standardized operating margins and diluted share counts; these counts are explicitly **not industry market-share percentages**. No unsupported market-share estimates are supplied.

Mechanical pretax tangible-capital returns use operating income divided by ending tangible equity plus debt less cash/investments. They are not issuer ROIC, average-capital returns or adjusted returns on regulatory capital. Nonpositive denominators are NM, not attractive negative yields.
