**Lululemon:** [May 27 cooperation filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1397187/000121390026061535/ea0292407-defa14a_lululemon.htm) and [June proxy supplement](https://corporate.lululemon.com/~/media/Files/L/Lululemon/investors/proxy-statements/pdf-docs/proxy-statement-june-2026.pdf) establish Wilson settlement, withdrawn nominees/new directors and proposed 2027 declassification. Do not rely only on old Elliott headlines. Settling-holder standstill is not automatically Third Point's. January 18, 2027 is a **14a-8 proposal deadline**, not a nomination window; ordinary window not recovered.

**3M:** [March 25 proxy](https://investors.3m.com/financials/sec-filings/content/0000066740-26-000151/0000066740-26-000151.pdf), advance-notice nominations **Nov 25–Dec 25, 2026**. Proxy-access window same, but 3%/three-year eligibility separate. Window ≠ defective board or verified TP campaign.

**PepsiCo:** prior Elliott portfolio agenda is historical context; [September 17, 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77476/000007747626000042/pep-20260917.htm) appoints independent director Joaquin Duato effective December 1. Current TP stake/new campaign not verified.

## Japan

[JPX July 21, 2026](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/news/news-releases/1020/20260721-01.html): revised code/listing rules effective that day. [Code page](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/listing/cg/) distinguishes Prime/Standard explanations for any noncompliance from Growth General Principles. **Comply-or-explain is not a right to force a spin.** [Nikkei August 25](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/markets/equities/japan-ranks-2nd-in-record-global-wave-of-shareholder-activism) reports H1 record activism, global +20% year-on-year, larger Japanese rise; Daikin/Toto among targets. These are others' opportunities, not TP stakes.
