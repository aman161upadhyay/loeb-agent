## Opportunity inventory: evidence → missing underwriting

* **B-/CCC loans:** July manager estimates CLOs own 70–75% of loans; ~$300B B-; historically ~20% annually downgraded to CCC. Basket limits can force selling. Need exact covenants, priming protection, enterprise recovery and liquidity; no refreshed October totals. [Q2]
* **Maturity walls:** manager says 25% of market matures next three years; >30% of 2028–29 maturities trade >1,000bp. Adverse selection after strong borrowers refinance. Need named stack, cash burn, LME/court timetable and sponsor incentives. [Q2]
* **AI financing:** unprecedented issuance may overwhelm demand; September NSCALE/CoreWeave/SoftBank longs show selective participation. Need customer concentration, contract duration, power, debt service and hardware-obsolescence collateral stress. AI demand is not a recovery floor. [Q2,S]
* **Bausch:** existing credit long; BLCO stake/capital structure needs maturity-specific prices, leakage restrictions, litigation/priority. BHC equity's low forward PE is **not a credit model**. [S]
* **Paramount/Skydance:** named September debt long, October WBD completion (F01/F04); live issue is financing/integration. Need new obligor/guarantees, leverage and debt quote; $6B issuer synergy target is not cash already earned. [S; F04]
* **Software, housing/building products, food/packaging, cable:** AI disruption; affordability/remodel weakness; PE leverage/excess capacity/GLP-1 demand; fiber/fixed-wireless substitution. Distinguish avoid/short from recapitalization long; validate cash-flow floor and priority before buying. [Q2]
* **First-lien mortgages:** Q1 refinanced a transaction, sold >$300M risk inside 5% yield, retaining term-levered exposure. Q2 favors smaller balances, NQM financing and amortization. Need LTV/FICO, delinquency/servicing/geography and slower-refinancing stress after September hike. Historical sale yield ≠ current yield. [Q1,Q2]
* **Rental-car / consumer ABS:** Q1 quarter-end liquidity provision and secondary purchases. Need vehicle residuals, bankruptcy-remoteness, servicer continuity and consumer/job-loss stress. September consumer exposure validates relevance, not a new October purchase. [Q1,S]
* **Junior-BBB CLO / equity:** July insurer capital-rule selling near BB yields; possible Q4 BDC sales when equity misses distributions. Need attachment, OC/IC tests, CCC pool and reinvestment expiry. September rounded zero CLO makes this a **watchlist**, not current allocation. [Q2,S]
* **Data-center ABS:** July suggested AI retracement could create entries, tempered by long-duration IG demand. Need tenant/take-or-pay, power/interconnection and re-leasing/stranding analysis; no executable spread. [Q2]
