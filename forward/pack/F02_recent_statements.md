## Letters: publication dates, not reporting quarter ends

**February 6, 2026, Q4 2025 [L1]:** Offshore +1.9%. Rotation from software toward semiconductors, memory/equipment, physical infrastructure, European defense and recovering healthcare. “We own many of these winners and continue to look for new ones.” AI challenges formerly capital-light moats; crowding led to a larger single-name short book and foreign exposure. February's benign disinflation view is **not October regime evidence**.

SK Square: ~47% NAV discount, Hynix ~97% NAV at 6.5x 2026 EPS; effective ~3.5x through holding company. Debt-funded buybacks/new-investment moratorium are **proposals**, not completed actions. Somnigroup: Mattress Firm distribution/supplier consolidation. Quebecor: Freedom Mobile low pricing and regulatory roaming advantage. These are February valuations, not October marks. [L1]

**April 10, Q1 2026 [L2]:** Offshore -0.6% versus S&P -4.3%. “Our exposure reduction began in February, when many of our positions reached a target price or were beneficiaries of factor rotations, not a change in fundamentals.” Rails and Kimberly-Clark successor exposure to Kenvue exited; risk subsequently raised somewhat but remained defensive. Short strategy +7% gross/+6.6% net on $2.4B average exposure/~60 names: **strategy return, not NAV contribution**. Themes: housing affordability, GLP-1 demand destruction, AI disruption and leveraged roll-ups. Indra remains a national-defense/backlog long.

CoStar: “We no longer believe that our original thesis holds true today and have disposed of our position in its entirety.” Board changes failed to stop Homes.com spending; parachute/segment-reporting changes hardened objections. Corporate credit raised ~50% “over the last few weeks” as of April 10, **not all before March 31**. GSE exposure is preferred, not common. X/xAI debt called at premium after SpaceX acquisition. [L2]

**July 31, Q2 2026 [L3]:** Offshore +7.7% (+7.6% excluding legacy privates) versus S&P +15.2%; average net ~45%. CRH integrated aggregates/paving/water, **new Block and Flex** discussed. Block: installed-base lending monetization/proprietary data/AI costs. Flex: CPI separation and power/cooling/rack mix change. The letter also describes **July** AI/Korea momentum losses, not Q2 losses. Forced-selling explanations are manager commentary, not independently audited causation.

Credit: “we expect more compelling entry points ahead”; B-/CCC downgrades, maturities, software, housing, PE-owned food/packaging. Structured: insurer-driven junior-BBB CLO selling, prospective Q4 equity-tranche distribution pressure and amortizing mortgages/consumer ABS. [L3]
