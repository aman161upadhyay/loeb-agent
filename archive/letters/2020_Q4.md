# L14 — Q4 2020: public/private continuum and short-book asymmetry

**Date:** 2021-02-10. Era C. [Full reproduction](https://docslib.org/doc/2195106/letter-to-investors).

**Results/context:** Offshore +16.1% Q4 and +20.5% full year. Upstart's IPO is the largest contributor; the letter compares venture-to-public transitions with earlier bankruptcy-to-public transformations. Do not assign the full fund return to this one investment.

**Intel:** engineering talent losses, strategic review and better leadership are the catalyst. Gelsinger's return is January 2021 information, **after** Q4. The December 29 campaign does not amount to an unconditional instruction to divest all fabs.

**Prudential:** Jackson separation and Asian business valuation offer distinct capital/ownership catalysts; expected Q2 2021 completion is forward-looking. SPAC/PIPE opportunities require instrument/structure analysis rather than treating all newly listed growth equities identically.

**Risk quote:** “It is tempting to think that lower nets imply lower risk” but leverage is a “double-edged sword.”

**Short sizing/exits:** lower gross, careful liquidity, avoiding crowded high-short-interest names and cutting short losses promptly follow the early-2021 squeeze experience. This is subsequent-event risk learning, not a forecast made at December 31.

**Disconfirmation:** weak underwriting of growth, poor new-public-company economics, short squeezes and delayed corporate actions can all offset seemingly attractive nominal exposure.
