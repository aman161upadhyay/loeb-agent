# L06 — Q4 2013: normalized earnings, capital structure and separation

**Date:** 2014-01-21. Era B. [Primary PDF](https://ftalphaville-cdn.ft.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/Third-Point-Q4-2013-Investor-Letter-TPOI.pdf).

**Framework quote:** “We are concentrating on identifying companies that have been under-earning relative to normalized earnings power.”

**Dow:** petrochemical separation/self-help and an ethylene cycle provide a route to earnings power; evaluate integration benefits against costs rather than assuming every breakup creates value. Commodity assumptions and implementation remain risks.

**Ally:** restructuring, TARP exit and prospective IPO can repair ownership and valuation. Approximately 9.5% cited ownership is **issuer ownership**, not 9.5% fund NAV. Capital requirements and transaction pricing affect equity recovery.

**SoftBank:** ARPU and Sprint-related strategic possibilities create operating/transaction optionality; merger permission is uncertain, not a completed catalyst.

**Sony:** 2013 operating/margin disappointments remain visible despite the public value-unlock proposal. Sony's August rejection of the minority entertainment offering is counterevidence against treating the activist's desired catalyst as inevitable.

**Sizing/exit:** no general maximum or fixed stop is disclosed. Model normalized earnings, attach reasonable business-specific multiples, then probability-weight management/transaction execution. Do not infer a fundamental exit solely from a later US long-equity filing.
