# L15 — Q4 2022: valuation and catalysts after a large loss year

**Date:** 2023-02-08. Era C. [Primary PDF](https://a.storyblok.com/f/166217/x/c179ba3ef3/third-point-q4-2022-investor-letter-tpil.pdf).

**Failure/context:** Q4 +1.2%, full-year **-21.7%**. Late defensive positioning lags the rally; private/crypto markdowns and write-offs are distinct from public-equity mark-to-market. Do not substitute a later recovery for this contemporaneous result.

**Quote:** “The current environment seems favorable for an investment approach that focuses on companies trading at attractive valuations with catalysts to realize full value.”

**Value/catalysts:** AIG's Corebridge separation, expense improvement and buybacks; Bath & Body Works operational leadership; DuPont litigation/capital returns; PG&E dividend restoration and selling-overhang reduction. These require execution and/or regulatory/legal resolution, not just low multiples.

**Completed action:** Twitter is held through deal closure; that is catalyst completion, unlike an unresolved operating turnaround.

**Risk construction:** rate protection and seniority matter in credit. Models relying on cheap money are no longer economic under the new financing regime.

**Sizing/exit gaps:** no position-specific mechanical stop or universal hurdle is established. Bath & Body Works settlement timing is not inferred from this discussion. FTX retrospective diligence lessons in 2026 must remain labeled later knowledge.

**Subsequent event, not February-letter knowledge:** the [SEC-filed issuer release, 2023-03-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000119312523061608/d439203ddefa14a.htm), confirms Kuhn's appointment effective March 10, Brady's earlier appointment, Third Point's recommendation of both and withdrawal of nomination plans. This is the verified campaign resolution; the guide's February 2022 settlement date is wrong.
