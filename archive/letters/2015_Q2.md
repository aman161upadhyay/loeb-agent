# L08 — Q2 2015: compounders before 2016

**Information date:** 2015-08-03 contemporaneous reporting; not asserted original signature date. Era B. [Market Folly letter discussion](https://www.marketfolly.com/2015/08/third-points-q2-letter-allergan-suzuki.html).

**Evidence:** Constellation Brands, Mohawk and Roper are discussed alongside Allergan and Suzuki. The source explicitly describes compounder investments; do not date the start of all quality investing to 2016 or 2020.

**Value/catalyst:** Constellation combines volume/margin growth, declining investment demands and prospective capital returns. Mohawk adds cyclical recovery and acquisitions. Roper combines decentralized operations, organic growth and disciplined allocation. A roughly 15% expected compounding rate for Roper is a forecast, not realized return.

**Sizing/exit:** exact NAV sizes, hurdle rates and position-specific sales are not recovered here. The causal thesis is multi-year earnings/cash-flow growth, not merely a one-day rerating.

**Risk:** paying too much, optimistic cycle normalization, integration/capital-allocation errors and finite reinvestment runways. Quality and events can coexist in one holding.

**Campaign separation:** Baxter's August 5 standalone letter is a different document, after Baxalta's completed separation. It requests CEO/board influence and should not be inserted into the August 3 Q2 letter as if already discussed there.
