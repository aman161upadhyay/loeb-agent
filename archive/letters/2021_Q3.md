# L27 — Q3 2021: Shell's conflicting stakeholder mandates

**Date:** 2021-10-27. Era C. [Primary PDF](https://goldseiten-forum.com/wcf/attachment/158736-third-point-q3-2021-investor-letter-tpil-pdf/). [Shell response, same date](https://shell.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/shell-acknowledges-third-point-llc-investor-letter).

**Trigger/value:** Shell trades below 4x EBITDA in the letter's analysis. Combining legacy energy cash generation with transition investment imposes conflicting stakeholder mandates; structural optimization/separation could permit more coherent allocation and accelerate decarbonization.

**Catalyst/risk:** engagement rather than a completed board-approved split. Management's response points to 89% AGM support for its own transition strategy, important counterevidence about implementation probability.

**Other evidence:** Vivendi/Universal and Dell/VMware use separation and cash-flow valuation; Dell's roughly 10% FCF yield, 2.5% dividend and 1.5x leverage are contemporaneous valuation references. Prudential/Jackson follows an actual corporate pathway; Entain has uncertain transaction optionality.

**Sizing:** Upstart and SentinelOne are the two largest positions during Q3; a thematic growth concentration exists alongside mature-business activism. Exact NAV weights are not inferred.

**Quote:** “It is often most impactful to invest in companies where the opportunity for positive change is the greatest.”

**Negative/exit:** corporate architecture alone cannot eliminate commodity cycles, policy risk or execution conflict. No Shell realized return or final exit reason is established by this letter.
