# L03 — Q3 2009: value realization recycles capital

**Information date:** reporting 2009-12-01, not asserted original signature date. Era B. [Market Folly contemporaneous letter discussion](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/12/dan-loeb-sees-favorable-investing.html).

**Trigger/evidence:** recapitalizations, reorganizations and recovering asset markets change the expected return of securities purchased during distress. Delphi, Dana, Bank of America, Fortis, Popular and mortgage exposures are discussed as contributors.

**Instrument/value:** Popular preferred conversion is analyzed as a route into common stock at an implied favorable price; do not mistake a preferred purchase price for an ordinary-share purchase price. Reorganization rights and conversion terms are part of valuation.

**Sizing/catalyst/exit:** merger arbitrage, previously over 20%, approaches zero as Pfizer/Wyeth and Merck/Schering-Plough close. This is a concrete natural exit mechanism: completion removes the spread and frees capital. No fixed holding-period rule is established.

**Result/risk:** reported Offshore +19.8% Q3 and +27.6% year to date are fund results, not security-level realized IRRs. Rapid exposure increases carry correlated economic and liquidity risks even when individual instruments appear cheap. Secondary reporting is weaker than an intact original letter; use directional reasoning rather than reconstructing undocumented trade tickets.
