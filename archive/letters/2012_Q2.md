# L04 — Q2 2012: panic prices and post-bankruptcy earnings

**Date:** 2012-07-31. Era B. [Full-text reproduction](https://pdfcoffee.com/2012-third-point-q2-investor-letter-tpoi-pdf-free.html).

**Trigger/action:** European stress creates forced/panicked selling. The letter describes a European iTraxx equity-tranche dislocation trade and subsequent normalization exit, alongside defensive currency protection. It increases risk again in July, a post-quarter action.

**Delphi evidence/value:** DIP entry was **June 2009**. The reorganized business has improved geography, operations, pension/union exposure and balance sheet. A $35–40 target implies approximately 30–40% prospective upside at the cited price. Free cash flow and buybacks support the bridge; old-industry stigma and hedge-fund ownership create risk.

**Sizing:** Delphi described as a core 2–5% position. This is a dated range, not a universal Third Point equity maximum.

**Other catalysts/exits:** Yahoo proxy resolution brings board nominees, an Alibaba repurchase agreement and new CEO Marissa Mayer. Progress Energy **Resources**, the Canadian company, is monetized through Petronas's C$20.45 offer; it is not the US utility transaction.

**Quote:** “In times of turmoil, we look for ‘fat pitches’” arising from dislocation and forced selling.

**Disconfirmation:** Europe can deteriorate beyond a transient spread shock; Delphi can disappoint operationally or suffer crowded ownership selling. **Correction:** this is not the Greek sovereign bond thesis letter. That thesis is recorded in Q3 2012 and later reviewed in L05.
