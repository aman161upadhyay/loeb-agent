# L07 — Q4 2014: distinguish investing from trading execution

**Date:** 2015-02-09. Era B. [Full reproduction](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/315517/daniel-loebs-third-point-q4-2014-investor-letter).

**Context:** Offshore's full-year return is reported at 5.7%; Ultra's 6.8% is a different fund and cannot be substituted.

**Process evidence:** the manager discusses poor trading and premature exits alongside the challenge of managing volatility. A research thesis can be reasonable while position management destroys its payoff.

**Quote:** “Since the financial crisis, managing volatility and risk has proven to be almost as important as good stock-picking in generating investment returns.”

**Opportunity set:** solid cash flow, consistent growth and constructive engagement appear alongside Amgen and Fanuc self-help/capital-allocation cases. Cheapness requires a bridge to distributable value; shareholder engagement may change payout or business configuration.

**Valuation/sizing/exits:** this note does not recover an account-level sizing formula, exact entry price or realized investment-level return. References to trading mistakes are not evidence of a universal stop-loss rule.

**Negative case:** avoid hindsight sorting where winners count as investment skill and early sales as unrelated noise. Preserve the original catalyst, expected time and subsequent reduction together. This letter also predates the supposed sharp 2016 transition into quality.
