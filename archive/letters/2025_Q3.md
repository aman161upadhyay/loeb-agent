**Date:** 2025-10-31. Era E holdout. [Primary PDF](https://assets-malibu-life.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/system/uploads/fae/file/asset/1673/Third_Point_Q3_2025_Investor_Letter_TPIL.pdf).

**AI evidence:** efficiency improvements need not destroy compute demand if reasoning/post-training increases consumption. The thesis remains conditional on realized demand, not a guarantee that all capex will earn returns.

**New exposures:** SK Hynix/SK Square in Korea; Ebara in Japan. Hynix's >50% HBM share and design-cycle stickiness support a different earnings interpretation from commodity DRAM. Approximately 7x 2026 earnings plus SK Square's roughly 60% NAV discount imply indirect Hynix exposure at about 3.5x in the manager's framing. These are dated forward valuation estimates, not permanent discounts.

**Ebara:** CMP/advanced packaging and operating mix create prospective earnings and multiple uplift. Semiconductor cycle, customer concentration, holding-company leakage and governance remain risks.

**Other opportunity:** Tricolor's distressed securitizations prompted capital-structure research and small investments. First Brands was **not a direct exposure**; structured-credit and Birch Grove teams instead evaluated CLO tranche price moves as the large default affected loan/CLO investors. These are distinct instruments and trade rationales; contagion is not evidence that every issuer is solvent.

**Results/failure:** Offshore +3.2% Q3. Kenvue is among event-driven disappointments; the letter's AI winners do not erase that failure.

**Sizing/exits/timing:** exact NAV stakes and a mechanical exit rule unknown. October EA transaction discussion is post-quarter. Do not assign it to September holdings or performance without separate evidence.
