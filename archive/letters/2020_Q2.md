# L13 — Q2 2020: quality as an explicit screen

**Date:** 2020-08-06. Era C. [Full reproduction](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/1205100/daniel-loebs-third-point-2ndquarter-investor-letter).

**Process action:** Loeb says he resumed sole-CIO responsibility and took fuller control of idea generation and the portfolio. This is a distinct 2020 change, not the same event as the post-2008 single-P&L reorganization.

**Framework/evidence:** quality combines great leadership, unique products, growing end-markets, share gains, earnings/free-cash-flow growth and high returns on invested capital. The screen is operational, not a synonym for a popular ticker.

**Quotes:** “today, quality is also an essential screen”; “investing in compounders and event-driven situations are not mutually exclusive activities.”

**Valuation:** attractive entry matters precisely because quality companies often command high multiples. Yahoo's Alibaba exposure and businesses at margin/ROIC inflection points show how quality and events overlap.

**Sizing/exit:** corporate-credit exposure is reduced from 23% to 17%; no permanent equity sizing formula or fixed stop is stated. Improved expected compounding is not authorization to ignore valuation or downside.

**Failure boundary:** quality language follows the pandemic drawdown. It should not be retroactively assigned to every original purchase thesis, nor should 2020 be presented as the first time the firm ever owned compounders.
