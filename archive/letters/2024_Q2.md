# L19 — Q2 2024: distribution moats and misunderstood transitions

**Date:** 2024-08-23. Era C. [Primary PDF](https://assets.thirdpointlimited.com/f/166217/x/dfadb3684e/third-point-q2-2024-investor-letter_tpil.pdf). Full text recovered through Exa after direct fetch rate limiting.

**Apple:** initiated April 2024 amid stagnant-growth and AI-loser sentiment. Apple Intelligence could drive upgrades and make the installed base an AI distribution platform. This is conditional on execution and monetization, not a documented upgrade supercycle already achieved.

**Corpay:** initiated Q4 2023. Maturing fuel-card/EV fears obscure B2B payments becoming the main growth driver. Approximately 13x P/E is compared with high margin/ROIC and expected >10% EPS growth. Terminal-value risk in fossil-fuel processing and transition timing are the countercase.

**ICE:** originally invested April 2023; mortgage workflow automation, acquisition cross-selling and AI services extend the growth runway. A mortgage-volume trough must actually improve, and integration must deliver.

**Quote:** “If Apple can execute on this opportunity, the monetization form factors will follow.”

**Results/risk:** Offshore +1.8% Q2 and +9.8% first half. Technology strength is tempered by consumer/event-driven laggards including Bath & Body Works and Advance Auto Parts.

**Sizing/exit:** exact account weights and sales rules unknown. These are compounding/catalyst combinations, not evidence that cheap multiples alone protect consumer turnarounds.
