**Date:** 2024-04-30. Era C. [Primary PDF](https://assets.thirdpointlimited.com/f/166217/x/785ef96e1a/third-point-q1-2024-investor-letter_tpil.pdf).

**Value/evidence:** substantial Alphabet purchase after Gemini-related operational embarrassment depresses sentiment; the thesis requires distribution, data and financial resources to support durable AI economics. TSMC added at a reported 10-multiple-point-plus P/E discount to the SOX; advanced manufacturing/AI demand and cyclical recovery are the bridge.

**Quote:** “this new technology favors incumbents who are deploying their financial and intellectual war chests to win the AI arms race.”

**Vistra:** Energy Harbor nuclear acquisition, dispatchable power scarcity and changing demand support cash generation and rerating. The source distinguishes cheap legacy valuations from better asset economics; market reform and electricity pricing remain risks.

**LSEG:** mission-critical information, network effects and machine-assisted financial workflows create a business-model thesis.

**Advance Auto Parts:** Worldpac disposal and operating repair offer potential value; implied RemainCo per-store value below $2m compared with roughly $10m peers is not proof that all stores deserve peer value. Execution and deteriorating operations matter.

**Sizing/exit:** position adjectives are not exact NAV weights. Offshore reports +7.8% Q1. No universal profit target/stop is supplied; AI as a key thesis in many holdings also introduces common-factor risk.
