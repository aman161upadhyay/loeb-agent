# L10 — Q1 2018: breakup economics and defensive repositioning

**Information date:** reproduction 2018-05-07; original signature date not independently verified. Era C. [Full reproduction](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/677941/daniel-loebs-third-point-1st-quarter-investor-letter).

**UTX:** position began Q4 2017. Separate Otis, climate/control and aerospace businesses to expose distinct peer economics. The analysis uses roughly 15x, 13x and 15x EV/EBITDA references respectively; ~$20bn/>20% incremental value and ~$210 per share including GTF value are forecasts, not achieved proceeds. Management's willingness to make a full split is a key uncertainty.

**Other value:** DowDuPont separations offer near-term corporate catalysts; Lennar's roughly 6x pro-forma earnings depends on CalAtlantic integration and housing; Dover's discount connects to Apergy separation and leadership change.

**Risk action:** net reduced by more than twenty percentage points; longs reduced around fifteen points and short exposure increased toward 25% of AUM. Fundamental and quantitative baskets supplement individual positions. Rich credit valuations prompt corporate-credit caution and reductions in longer-duration mortgage exposure.

**Quote:** “Our job is to listen carefully and to take decisive action when we suspect change is afoot.”

**Exit/disconfirmation:** stalled separation, incorrect sum-of-parts assumptions, cyclical earnings disappointment or higher rates can impair the thesis. Housing's low multiple is not itself a floor; later Reddit criticism of Lennar is external evidence of skepticism, not a documented manager exit.
