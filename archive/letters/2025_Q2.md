# L22 — Q2 2025: re-entry, strategic mergers and overlooked moats

**Date:** 2025-07-30. Boundary of C/E. [Primary PDF](https://assets.thirdpointlimited.com/f/166217/x/aa404bcfeb/third-point-q2-2025-investor-letter_tpil.pdf).

**Nvidia/action:** re-initiate after tariff, recession and capex fears depress shares. Timely hedge covering and Nippon Steel/US Steel completion help returns; US Steel contributes about 200bps gross. Do not confuse gross contribution with net fund return (+7.5%).

**Rocket:** new position treats Mr. Cooper combination as a synergy-rich merger of technology/distribution leaders in mortgages. Integration, financing and housing-cycle sensitivity remain risks.

**Casey's:** operational culture, differentiated offer and disciplined expansion; Fike's transaction around 7x pro-forma EBITDA is an acquisition multiple, not the listed company's multiple.

**Informa:** events network effects, scale and process power, at roughly 14x forward earnings. The forecast assumes durable attendance/exhibitor economics rather than immediate one-off rerating.

**Quote:** “When a company chooses not to do the easy thing, it is often a powerful signal that something special is at work.”

**Negative/credit:** three disappointing credits in Q1 are acknowledged; credit underweights technology because of AI transition risk. Rental-car ABS monetization and Birch Grove research broaden capital allocation.

**Sizing/exit:** exact NAV stakes and universal sell thresholds unknown. Re-entry illustrates a changed reward-to-risk judgment, not a statement that all prior exits were mistaken.
