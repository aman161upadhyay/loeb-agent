# L11 — Q3 2018: post-October critique of process

**Date:** 2018-11-09. Era C. [Full letter reproduction](https://docslib.org/doc/1334701/november-9-2018-third-quarter-2018-investor-letter-review-and-outlook).

**Timing boundary:** this Q3 letter includes the subsequent October rout. Do not describe all deleveraging or losses discussed as occurring by September 30.

**Quotes:** “Underperformance forces us to take a hard look at how our process let us down”; “It always seems like the perfect storm in hindsight.”

**Action/evidence:** reduce exposure, cut technology risk and increase short protection as the market regime changes. Quantitative/fundamental work and cash-flow/productivity scrutiny supplement narrative underwriting. American Express is discussed through strategic/operating evolution.

**Value/catalyst:** operating improvements and durable cash generation remain necessary, but price sensitivity, factor crowding and financing conditions affect realization. The author explicitly questions the post-crisis “buy the dip” paradigm.

**Sizing/exit:** no universal loss limit emerges. Exposure reductions respond to aggregate risk and changing expected returns, not necessarily a declaration that every underlying long is worthless.

**Failure use:** retain this critique next to the earlier confident UTX/Lennar/DowDuPont underwriting. A perfect ex-post explanation is not an ex-ante risk system. Full Campbell-board nomination is separately sourced to the September campaign letter P06.
