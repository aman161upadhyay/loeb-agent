- Q4 2024 full manager letter is February 4, 2025; TPIL's February 5 release is a separate highlights notice.
- Q3 2025 letter date October 31 differs from Reddit's November 12 link-addition date.
- Bath & Body Works settlement date should be verified independently; no February 2022 settlement claim is used here.
- Corporate events reported in letters after quarter-end remain subsequent events. In particular July 2026 losses are not included in Q2 returns.
