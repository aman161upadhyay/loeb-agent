# L01 — Q2 2008: pre-crisis opportunity set

**Information date:** 2008-07-25. Era A. **Primary:** [original letter PDF](https://aboveaverageodds.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2009/12/loeb_thirdpoint_q22008.pdf).

The letter reports Offshore +6.2% for Q2 and +1.6% year to date. The July document is a snapshot before the autumn financial collapse, not a retrospective crisis-management statement. It discusses an energy/financials opportunity set; it must not be rewritten as proof that the portfolio anticipated every subsequent credit failure.

**Decision use:** preserve the manager's willingness to take sector and macro-sensitive exposures alongside fundamental situations. Distinguish a profitable quarter from protection against discontinuous financing/liquidity risk.

**Valuation, sizing and exits:** no universal position-size or stop-loss rule is inferred. Firm assets cited in the letter are not a portfolio denominator for a particular trade. Security-specific forecast reconstruction has not been completed.

**Failure/forward link:** the January 2011 institutional diligence report documents post-2008 restructuring of portfolio responsibility, single P&L, reduced activism and illiquid co-investments. Those are retrospective evidence of adaptation, not information available on July 25. The Q4 2008 original remains a retrieval gap.
