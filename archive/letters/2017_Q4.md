# L09 — Q4 2017: a replicable process must evolve

**Date:** 2018-01-22. Era C. [Primary letter](https://www.valuewalk.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Third-Point-Q4-Investor-Letter-TPOI.pdf).

**Quote:** “A replicable investment process is not static.”

**Organizational evidence:** Loeb frames building a business rather than a sequence of trades. Sector/asset teams, collaboration, honesty, risk tools and data science support repeatability. The specialist transition began in **2009**, according to this retrospective account, rather than beginning with the 2017 letter.

**Portfolio construction:** more individual shorts in place of index hedges support higher reported net exposure around 68%. This is not evidence that shorts remove all factor risk or that higher net is always warranted.

**Nestlé value/catalyst:** branded franchises plus operational margins, simpler portfolio, capital returns and monetization of the L'Oréal stake. The valuation bridge relies on future earnings and execution, not solely the quality of existing brands.

**Risk/exit:** management may move incrementally or reject asset disposals; currency and consumer shifts can alter earnings. Campaign success is not guaranteed by a large dollar investment. No exact mechanical exit rule is disclosed.

**Forward separation:** the July 2018 NestléNOW escalation belongs to a later document and signals dissatisfaction with pace, not knowledge available in January.
