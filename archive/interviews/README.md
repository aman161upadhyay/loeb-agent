# Interviews, panels and public presentations

Cutoff/retrieved **2026-10-25**. **13 dated records**, including two primary campaign presentations. Not all are interviews. Source type, publication/event distinction and inaccessible-transcript limitations are explicit. A source announcing an appearance is not evidence of everything said on stage.

## I01 — Yahoo, SALT / CNBC, 2012-05-09

[CNBC interview reporting](https://www.cnbc.com/2012/05/09/loeb-says-hell-fight-yahoo-as-long-as-it-takes.html). Contemporary reporter account after the conference appearance. Loeb calls Yahoo bloated/unfocused but a repairable business; the catalyst is leadership and board repair, not simply an internet-sector bet. The article's title conveys willingness to continue the contest, **not** a universal rule never to abandon activism. Exact fund NAV size, valuation model and exit trigger are not established by this interview. Compare the September 2011 SEC letter and the later proxy resolution, rather than importing the resolution into May 9.

[Reuters, published 2015-05-07](https://www.reuters.com/article/idUSKBN0NS0DB20150507/); [Institutional Investor, 2015-05-07](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsv4pgkzjvt2h5ceb5s0/portfolio/the-morning-brief-a-lively-day-one-at-salt). ## Evidence hierarchy and gaps

Primary PDFs and complete recorded speech outrank press summaries; public campaign decks remain advocacy. Several panel records establish attendance/topics but not full Loeb-specific reasoning. This register does **not** claim thirteen complete interview transcripts. Pre-2008 voice remains disproportionately represented by public letters and later retrospectives. No unattributed Mr. Pink transcript is presented as authenticated.
