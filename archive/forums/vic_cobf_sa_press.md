# VIC, CoBF, specialist forums and press: corroboration/countercases

Cutoff/retrieved **2026-10-25**. Separate manager statements, company responses and external hypotheses. A source restricted behind login/paywall is not represented as fully read.

## V01 — VIC Sony, 2019-04-17

[Value Investors Club, Sony 6758, author bdad](https://valueinvestorsclub.com/idea/Sony_/3646012808). Accessible indexed thesis, not Loeb-authored. Sony described as misunderstood with strong oligopolistic franchises; gaming/music shift toward subscriptions; image sensors provide growth; potential Third Point involvement offers a catalyst.

**Useful distinction:** this predates Third Point's June 13 public deck. It is an independent investor's hypothesis about possible engagement, not proof that all of Third Point's later recommendations were already known.

**Valuation/countercase:** accessible table shows ~10.7x/9.5x 2019/2020 EPS references and P/FCF ~9.6x/8.4x. Those are the VIC author's contemporary figures, not Loeb forecasts. Examine hit-driven entertainment, cyclicality, sensor competition and capital-allocation feasibility. Guest/login restrictions may limit comments; no invented discussion is supplied.

## S01 — SemiWiki Intel, 2021-01-06

[Dan Loeb and Bob Swan Talking](https://semiwiki.com/forum/threads/dan-loeb-and-bob-swan-talking.13559/). Named forum users discuss a reported Zoom call and possible leadership outcomes; one user's greenmail/golden-parachute scenarios are speculation. Do not report them as established negotiations.

**Use:** industry readers distinguish TSMC manufacturing progress from financial-market pressure. Cross-check [Intel's December 29 official response](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1434/intel-corporation-statement-regarding-third-point-llc-letter) and P07. A forum's opinion that an executive has no choice does not establish activist control.

## P01 — Sony rejection: primary company countercase, 2013-08-06

[Sony official letter](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/News/Press/201308/13-091E/); [Variety contemporaneous reporting, 2013-08-05 US time](https://variety.com/2013/biz/news/sony-rejects-third-point-offer-to-spin-off-entertainment-assets-1200574449/).

Management argues retaining 100% entertainment ownership is integral to sustained profitability and shareholder value, while accepting better financial disclosure. This is a **rejected minority IPO proposal**, not a successful entertainment spinoff. The one-day difference is publication/time-zone context, not two rejections.

**Test:** net value of transparency/discipline versus lost synergies, financing alternatives and transaction costs.

## P02 — Baxter settlement: constructive engagement, 2015-09-30

[WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/articles/baxter-gives-two-board-seats-to-loebs-third-point-1443622630). Accessible headline/text reports **one** Third Point board seat for Munib Islam and involvement in CEO search. The URL's “two-board-seats” wording does not supersede the actual account. Third Point's August letter had requested two nominees.

**Test:** requested outcome versus actual settlement; board access is an intermediate catalyst. Do not assert that an activist caused the already-completed Baxalta separation.

## P03 — Nestlé escalation, 2018-07-02/03

[Reuters, 2018-07-02](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-nestle-thirdpoint/loeb-pressures-nestle-for-more-sales-restructuring-idUSKBN1JR26K/); [Reuters, 2018-07-03](https://www.reuters.com/article/business/loebs-third-point-takes-new-approach-in-battle-with-nestle-idUSKBN1JT0IR).

Reports urgency after a year of engagement and the new campaign website/presentation. This tests a simplistic narrative that quieter, institutional Loeb never escalates. Pace of change, not merely acknowledgment of the thesis, affects catalyst value.

## P04 — Sony 2019 credibility challenge, 2019-06-18

[Bloomberg reporting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-06-18/sony-analysts-question-loeb-credibility-after-strategy-flip-flop). Accessible headline/summary: analysts question the reversal from separating part of entertainment in 2013 to using chip separation to support entertainment focus in 2019.

**Test:** distinguish intellectual updating from opportunistic story changes. Compare actual segment economics and portfolio context across dates. Paywall limits detailed analyst-attribution recovery; no full quotes or claims of consensus are invented.

## P05 — Shell response, 2021-10-27

[Primary company response](https://shell.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/shell-acknowledges-third-point-llc-investor-letter). Shell notes 89% AGM support for its Powering Progress strategy. This does not disprove Third Point's structural value argument, but shows the company has a different supported pathway.

**Test:** stakeholder feasibility, commodity/transition capital needs, and whether separation actually reduces conflict rather than moving it elsewhere.

## P06 — Short squeezes and changing implementation, 2023-08-06

[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-08-06/dan-loeb-surrendered-but-meme-army-still-hits-bears-for-millions). Accessible account links retail-driven rallies to dangerous short implementation. Its dramatic “surrendered” framing should be checked against L17: reducing single-name shorts/increasing baskets is not evidence Third Point permanently stopped shorting.

## P07 — Relative underperformance, 2024-07-08

[Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2dgwwf8g37yb2zz9ozegw/hedge-funds/why-dan-loebs-third-point-is-underperforming). Accessible figures distinguish long, short, credit and private gross contributions. Useful to test whether a credit-heavy firm narrative matches flagship return drivers. Do not mix gross contributions with net fund return, or infer that strong long-book performance implies every activist situation worked.

## P08 — Activist becomes target, 2022-02-18

[FT](https://www.ft.com/content/3d773728-4d72-45ad-a042-0d2e3ef9d307?syn-25a6b1a6=1). Reporting on TPIL discount/activist pressure. Limited accessible text; no settlement terms invented. Important distinction between the management firm, flagship funds and the listed closed-end vehicle: their discounts, fee structures and shareholder mandates are not the same security-level question.
